Debrief in game

Your pull recap inside World of Warcraft, a few seconds after every boss pull: your numbers, your top rotation slips, your boss mistakes, who died and one thing to try next pull.

Download for Windows 2.2 MB Just the addon 0.2 MB

Version 0.4.5. The app keeps itself and the addon up to date.

How to set it up

  1. Run Debrief-Setup.exe.One click: it installs the app and the WoW addon. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The app is new and isn't signed yet.
  2. Raid as normal.The app starts with Windows and sits quietly in the background, reading your combat log after each boss pull. The addon turns combat logging on for you in raids.
  3. When a pull ends, press Ctrl+V.A small "See your recap?" prompt pops up in game, ready for Ctrl+V. Your card opens, and everyone in the raid with the addon gets theirs by itself.
Only one person in the raid needs the app. Everyone else just needs the addon: download Debrief-addon.zip and unzip it into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns.

What you get

Your card

Your damage or healing against your last pull and your best, your biggest slips and your boss mistakes.

Raid view

The raid's biggest mistakes with names, who died first and to what, and the Next pull line.

Big window

Every pull of the night, your numbers pull by pull, and how far each pull got.

Make it yours

Simple, Medium or Heavy; accent colours, mini bar, goals, and one-click posting to raid chat for raid leads.

Type /debrief demo in game to see a sample card, and /debrief options for the settings.
