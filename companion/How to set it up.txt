DEBRIEF IN GAME (test version 0.1)
==================================

What it does
  After every boss pull, your recap shows in WoW: your damage or healing (against your last pull and your best),
  your biggest rotation slips, your boss mistakes and one thing to try next pull. Raid leads also get a Raid view.

TRY IT RIGHT NOW (test mode is on in this version)
  - In WoW, type /debrief demo: a sample card from our Heroic Ula'tek kill shows, no paste needed.
  - Or open "Test code - paste this in game.txt", copy everything (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C), and in WoW press Ctrl+Shift+D
    (or type /debrief paste), then Ctrl+V.
  - The Debrief box pops up after every fight (a training dummy works) and when you log in. /debrief test turns that off.

One person in the raid (whoever keeps the combat log) does this
  1. Double-click "Debrief Companion.bat".
     A small window opens. It also puts the Debrief addon into your WoW AddOns folder by itself.
     Keep the black window open during raid (you can minimize both).
  2. In WoW, log the raid: type /combatlog when you zone in (or keep using the Warcraft Logs uploader's logging).
     If WoW was already open, type /reload once so the addon loads.
  3. After each boss pull you hear a chime. In WoW the Debrief box pops up: click it (or press Ctrl+Shift+D),
     then press Ctrl+V. Your card shows, and the addon sends everyone else their card.

Everyone else
  Just have the Debrief addon installed (copy the "Debrief" folder from "addon" into
  World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns). Their card shows up by itself after each pull.

Handy commands in WoW
  /debrief          show or hide the card
  /debrief paste    open the paste box
  /debrief raid     Raid view on or off
  /debrief reset    move the card back to the middle
  Drag the card by its top bar. Esc closes it.

If something's off
  - Windows says "Windows protected your PC" when you double-click the .bat: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
    (It's because the file came from a download.) Unzip first; don't run it from inside the zip.
  - The window says it can't find your WoW Logs folder: paste where WoW is installed, for example
    C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft
  - The addon says "Out of date" on the character screen: tick "Load out of date AddOns".
  - Nothing happens after a pull: check that the combat log is on (/combatlog) and the black window is still open.
