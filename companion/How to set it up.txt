DEBRIEF IN GAME (version 0.2)
=============================

What it does
  After every boss pull, your recap shows in WoW: your damage or healing (against your last pull and your best),
  your biggest rotation slips, your boss mistakes and one thing to try next pull. Raid leads also get a Raid view.

The person who logs the raid
  1. Double-click Debrief-Setup.exe. That's it.
     It installs Debrief Companion (with a Debrief icon on your desktop), puts the Debrief addon into WoW,
     and opens. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway (it's new, so Windows
     doesn't know it yet).
  2. In WoW, type /reload if it was open. In a raid, the addon turns the combat log on by itself.
  3. After each boss pull you hear a chime and the Debrief box pops up, ready: press Ctrl+V.
     Your card shows, and the addon sends everyone else their card.
  You can close the app's window: Debrief keeps running in the background. Quit (top right) stops it.
  Next time, open it with the Debrief icon. It updates itself when there's a new version.

Raiders
  Just install the Debrief addon (from CurseForge, or the Debrief-addon zip into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns).
  Their card shows up by itself after each pull.

Handy commands in WoW
  /debrief          show or hide the card
  /debrief paste    open the paste box
  /debrief demo     a sample card
  /debrief log      combat log in raids on or off (on by default)
  /debrief focus    paste box ready for Ctrl+V after a pull, on or off
  /debrief test     test mode: the box after every fight, on or off
  /debrief raid     Raid view on or off
  /debrief reset    move the card back to the middle
