DEBRIEF IN GAME
===============

What it does
  After every boss pull (raid or dungeon), your recap pops up on your PC by itself: what you did well, what to fix first,
  and one thing to try next pull. In WoW, /recap shows the same recap: click Save this pull when the card offers it.
  Nothing to copy or paste: the app does it all.

Setting it up (everyone who wants their recap)
  1. Double-click Debrief-Setup.exe. That's it.
     It installs Debrief Companion (with a Debrief icon on your desktop), puts the Debrief addon into WoW, and opens.
  2. In WoW, type /reload if it was open. The first time, a short guided setup helps you pick what your card shows.
     The addon keeps the combat log on for you (with Advanced Combat Logging).
  3. Play as usual. After each boss pull, your recap pops up on your PC, over the game.
     In WoW, type /recap, and click Save this pull when the card offers it.
  You can close the app's window: Debrief keeps running in the background. Quit stops it.
  It updates itself between pulls. When the addon was updated, the pop-up tells you to /reload.

Handy commands in WoW
  /recap           show or hide your card
  /db              your settings: the style, what the card shows, colours and sizes
  /debrief big     the big window with every pull of the night
  /debrief setup   the guided setup again
  /debrief demo    a sample card
  /debrief log     combat logging on or off
  /debrief reset   move the card back to the middle

Something off? Press Report a problem at the bottom of the Debrief Companion window.
