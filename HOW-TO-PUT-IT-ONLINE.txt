Put WoW Log Analyzer online (about 15 minutes, once)
=====================================================
After this, anyone can open your site and paste a Warcraft Logs link. They never need a key or any setup.

1. Warcraft Logs API client (if you don't have one yet)
   warcraftlogs.com > your account settings > API clients > Create client.
   Any name. Redirect URL: put your site address later, or https://example.com for now.
   Copy the Client ID and Client Secret.

2. Free Cloudflare account
   Sign up at https://dash.cloudflare.com (the free plan is enough).

3. Create the Worker
   Dashboard > Workers & Pages > Create > Create Worker (the "Hello World" starter).
   Give it a name, for example: wow-log-analyzer. Click Deploy.
   Click "Edit code". Delete everything in the editor.
   Open worker.js (from this folder) in Notepad, select all, copy, paste into the editor.
   Click Deploy.

4. Add your key as secrets
   Open the Worker > Settings > Variables and Secrets > Add.
     Type: Secret   Name: WCL_CLIENT_ID       Value: your Client ID
     Type: Secret   Name: WCL_CLIENT_SECRET   Value: your Client Secret
   Save / Deploy.

5. Open your site
   The address is shown on the Worker's page, like https://wow-log-analyzer.yourname.workers.dev
   Paste a report link and click Analyze.
   Share links look like: https://wow-log-analyzer.yourname.workers.dev/?report=AbCd1234&fight=12

Updating later: when you get a new worker.js, repeat step 3 (Edit code, paste, Deploy). Your secrets stay.
Your key never reaches visitors' browsers. Reports are cached at Cloudflare, so popular pulls don't use your API limit again.
