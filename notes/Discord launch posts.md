## 1. #start-here: What is Debrief? (with 3 pictures)

Pictures, in order: a map of a mechanic (Gloombomb), the "What went wrong" box with its small maps, the Assignments how-to.

**What is Debrief?**
Debrief reads your raid's combat logs and turns every pull into a short list: what went wrong, what went well, and what to fix before you pull again. It's built for Mythic progression in The Venomous Abyss.

**What you get**
🗺️ **A map of every mechanic.** Open one and see where everyone stood, then watch the few seconds before it went wrong.
❌ **What went wrong, at a glance.** Every pull starts with its problems, worst first, each with a small map of the moment.
📋 **Your raid's plan, checked.** Paste your NSRT note once, and Debrief checks every soak, kick, orb and cooldown in it on every pull. No other site does this.
📈 **Graphs.** Damage, healing, casts, cooldowns and more, for one pull or the whole night.

**How to start**
1. Go to https://debrief.james-m-nemecek.workers.dev
2. Paste a Warcraft Logs report link, or open a combat log from your PC.
3. Raiding right now? Press **Go live**, and each pull shows up about a minute after it ends.

Questions go in #questions, ideas in #suggestions, and anything broken in #bug-reports (or use **Report a problem** on the site).

## 2. #updates: the first patch notes

**Patch notes · October 18**
Debrief's first patch notes. Here's what's new for launch.

**Maps first**
• Every map on Mechanics now shows by itself. No more Map buttons.
• Maps play the few seconds leading up to each mechanic, so you can see why it went wrong, especially for deaths.
• Every "What went wrong" line that has a map shows it small. Hover to make it bigger, and click to open the full map.
• Each mechanic is drawn in the colour of its spell icon: the purple circle is Gloombomb, the red one is Coiling Ichor, and so on.
• Maps that only showed player dots, without the mechanic itself, were removed.

**New tabs**
• **Graphs** (it was Performance): damage, healing, damage taken, casts, resources, cooldowns, Power Infusion, buffs, utility and survivability, each one tab away.
• **Rotation** is coming soon: how each player played their own spec.
• An **All pulls** card next to the pull cards shows the whole night on Graphs and Players.

**Assignments**
• A short how-to explains the tab, and **Try it with an example** shows it working before you paste your own NSRT note.

**Power Infusion**
• Rebuilt in plain sentences: who got it, when, and what to do next time, with the target's big cooldown drawn for its full length.

**Everything else**
• Every "What went wrong" box now shows all its lines.
• **Report a problem** in the site's menu sends a bug straight to us.

Found something off? Tell us in #bug-reports.

## 3. #questions: FAQ (a pinned forum post)

Title: **FAQ: read this first**

**How do I open my raid?**
On the site, paste a Warcraft Logs report link. You can also open a combat log from your PC (WoWCombatLog.txt), or drop it anywhere on the page.

**Can I use it during raid?**
Yes. If someone in your raid live logs to Warcraft Logs, press **Go live** on the site. Each pull shows up about a minute after it ends.

**Why are there no maps?**
Maps need player positions, which come from Advanced Combat Logging. Whoever logs should turn it on in game (Options, Network, Advanced Combat Logging).

**What's an NSRT note, and where do I get it?**
It's your raid's plan from Northern Sky Raid Tools: who soaks, who kicks, and who presses which cooldown. Your raid leader shares it, or you can copy it from wowutils. Paste it on the Assignments tab.

**Is my note shared with anyone?**
No. Your NSRT notes and settings stay in your own browser.

**Which bosses does it cover?**
All eight bosses of The Venomous Abyss. It's built for Mythic. Heroic and Normal pulls work too, though a few checks are Mythic only.

**Why is Rotation greyed out?**
It's still being built. It will show how each player played their own spec.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes, every page works on a phone.

**Something looks wrong. What do I do?**
Use **Report a problem** in the site's menu, or post in #bug-reports with a link to the pull.
