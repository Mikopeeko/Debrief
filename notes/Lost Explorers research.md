# The Lost Explorers (Mythic): research notes

Sources: the Method Mythic and Heroic guides. These notes may name sources; the app's text stays neutral.

## Fight shape
- Three explorers: Trader Gebbo, Scrollsage Iku and First Mate Nama. Mor'zahi, the puppet master, possesses one at a time.
- United Defense: 99% damage reduction when all three are within 30 yd.
- Mor'zahi builds energy. At 100 he casts Final Ascension (keeps hitting until the raid dies). A Disgusting Fish (from the boxes)
  stops it and moves him to the next explorer, whose empowered moves come out (Gebbo: bombs and mushrooms; Iku: Frostfire Volley;
  Nama: Mighty Thud). There are three fish; the fourth Final Ascension is the hard enrage.
- Boxes (Throw Junk): pop them before they glow (Relic Rupture). On Mythic every pop is a raid bleed (Splinters), so pop on a rhythm:
  two stacks, drop, two more.
- Iku:
  - Burning Flames is cleared in a frost patch; Piercing Frost in a fire patch. Clear before the next volley, or Elemental Explosion.
  - Blink Nova: spread; its target stays 20+ yd from the melee.
  - Icebound Flames: kick. Shredding Shards: tank.
- Nama:
  - Mighty Thud: three leaps, each split within 6 yd; an empty one hits the raid. Aftershock craters.
  - Shell Spin frontal (stun). Steady Strikes: tank.
- Gebbo: Explosive Surprise, a bomb then a lethal Blast Wave. Bounce over it on the mushroom (one per bomb on Mythic).
- Lust on pull. Feed order varies (Wolfthorn: Gebbo, then Iku, then Nama on the kill; Gebbo, Nama, Iku on the wipe).

## Thresholds that are my call (not from a guide)
- Splinters amber at 4+ stacks after a pop (the guide's rhythm is 2, 3 if needed).
- United Defense amber when up, red for more than 5 s.
- Frostfire: amber if cleared after more than 8 s.
- Mighty Thud: amber under 5 soakers on a leap.
- Tank stacks: shown, not judged (no guide number); deaths are red.
