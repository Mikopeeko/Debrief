# Mechanic nicknames

## What went in (Oct 18, your go-ahead: "keep them simple")
One- or two-word nicknames on 25 headings. Headings that already said what the mechanic is after the colon got none, so
nothing repeats ("Stone Breaker: tank catches the circles" stays as it is).
- The Coiled Altar: Guillotine (axe) soaks, Dreadmarch (mind control): ghosts and falls, Spiritcackle (fear adds),
  Gloombombs (player bombs)
- The Twin Fangs: Caustic Globule (orb) soaks, Barbed Bulwark (orb shield), Corrosive Spit (add attack)
- Entombed Sentinels: Toxic Droplets (puddles)
- Sszorak: Ravage (frontal), Mutilate (soak frontal), Raging Crosswinds (arrows), Serpent's Fury (stack circle),
  Virulence (line bursts)
- Nek'zali: Restless Amani (adds), Soulcoil Ignition (boss channel), Essence Rend (dispel)
- Vashnik: Living Venoms (adds), Dripping Fangs (tank buster)
- The Lost Explorers: Final Ascension (enrage), Frostfire Volley (fire and frost), Explosive Surprise (bomb)
- Ula'tek (reviewed Oct 18): Spectral Coils (soak circles): enough soakers, Toxic Incubation (beam): blocked by the tank,
  Blightscale Wretch (bubble add): raid inside, killed in time, Grasping Fangs (tethers): broken together, Serpent's Bite
  (soak). Caustic Waves has no nickname (the name already says it).
- Nymrissa and Kith'ix: none (they only have the simple checks, not Mechanics sections)

Also done: the three clumsy names below (as proposed), Soulcoil Ignition's summary line ("2 players were hit by the
circles over 3 Ignitions, 1 of them by two or more, and 1 of 3 started with Ritual Burn up."), and the Survival small
print, now "up to the wipe filter (raid cooldowns: the whole pull)". Not done: the other 10 "results" counts at the bottom.

## The first draft (longer nicknames, for reference)

How it would look: the plain nickname goes in brackets right after the game's name, and the rest of the heading stays.
For example, **Corrosive Spit (add line attack): standing in the line**.
Every nickname comes from what the mechanic does in our own boss notes. None are invented slang.

Headings that aren't one ability (Avoidable damage, Interrupts, Twin balance, Boss balance, Tanks, Tank stacks, Energy,
Blood pools, Phase changes) get no nickname.

## The vague "results" count
Soulcoil Ignition's summary counts "results". Each Ignition has a "going in" line plus one line per player hit, so it
should say what happened instead. For example: "3 players were hit by the circles, and nobody went in with Ritual Burn."

The same vague word shows up in 10 other places. I'd fix them in the same pass. The proposed words are my reading of
what each one counts, so they'd be checked against the code before changing:
| Section | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Final Ascension | results | casts |
| Grasping Depths | well results | Drowned Echoes |
| Nek'zali intermission | intermission results | intermissions |
| Malignant Totems | totem results | Imbibes |
| Spiritcackle adds | add and kick results | add sets |
| Dreadmarch | Dreadmarch results | Dreadmarches |
| Eggs | egg results | eggs |
| Grasping Fangs | tether results | tether breaks |
| Wardens and Doomscales | Doomscale results | Wardens |
| Serpent's Bite | bite results | bites |
