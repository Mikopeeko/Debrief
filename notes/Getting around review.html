Getting around Debrief

I walked the website the way a raider would: the front page, a team, a raid night, every tab, the menus, and the same on a phone. Below is everything that made moving around slower or more confusing than it needs to be, then a smoother layout, drawn as mockups for you to pick from. Nothing is built yet.

What I found

Red numbers are the ones raiders hit on every visit.

Moving back and forth

1

The browser's Back button always leaves Debrief. From any page (a pull, a tab, a team page), Back takes you to whatever site you were on before Debrief. On a phone, the back swipe does the same. every visit

Fix: every page you open is a step Back can return to: front page ← team page ← pull ← tab.

2

Each page makes its own way back: "‹ All teams", "‹ Back to The Coiled Altar, pull 13", the logo, "Start screen" in the menu.

Fix: with Back working, keep one small "‹ All teams" link on team pages and let the logo always mean Home.

Picking the boss, the pull or the whole night

3

The boss is picked in three places: the small "3 bosses · The Coiled Altar · Mythic" pill in the top bar, far from the big banner that also names the boss, and the "Also tonight" chips on Night summary. every visit

Fix: the boss name in the banner is the picker (click it to switch boss). The top-bar pill goes.

4

"All pulls" means three different things. The "∑ All pulls" card in the pull strip doesn't show all pulls of the tab you're on: it jumps to the Night summary tab, so the card and the tab are two buttons for one place. Graphs and Players also have their own "All pulls / Pull 13" switch inside them. every visit

Fix: one choice, in one place (see Option A and B below).

5

The tabs are split into "This pull" and "Whole night", but Graphs (in "This pull") can show the whole night, and Players (in "Whole night") can show one pull.

Fix: goes with 4.

6

The wipe filter sits in the pull strip, among the pull cards. It's a counting setting, not a way to move around, and on a phone it takes almost half the strip.

Fix: a small chip in the banner, "Counting up to 2 deaths ▾", that opens the same setting.

7

"Live: off" shows on every report, even a night that ended last week.

Fix: only show Live when the report is still being logged (Debrief already knows), or when you opened it with Go live.

The ☰ menu

8

"Start screen" does what the Debrief logo does, and the same page is called the start screen, the front page and home in different places.

Fix: drop it from the menu; call it Home everywhere.

9

"Paste a link" and "Open combat log" repeat the front page's boxes (and "Paste a link" is the only item with no line under it).

Fix: one item, "Open a report or log", for when you're deep in a pull and want another night.

10

"Saved progress" ("which logs are your raid, saved pulls") comes from the PC app's live logging. On the website, your teams do this, so it overlaps "Your teams" and confuses.

Fix: hide it on the website.

11

"Tips for this page" and "Take the tour" are two help items side by side.

Fix: one "Help" item: this page's tips, with the tour one click inside.

Your teams

12

The add button has four names: "+ Add a team or character", "Add a team or your character", "Add your team", "Add a team or character" (the box).

Fix: one wording everywhere: "Add a team or character".

13

From a pull, there's no way to your team's page unless the report was logged by a team on your list (the banner links to it only then), so you go through the Your teams menu.

Fix: fine once Back works (Back returns to the team page you came from); keep the banner link.

On a phone

14

Navigation takes 463 of the 812 pixels of the first screen. The top bar is three rows (logo; boss pill; Live, Your teams and ☰), then the banner, the pull strip and the tabs. Only three of the seven tabs fit, and the strip shows one pull card next to the wipe filter. every visit

Fix: one-row top bar, a shorter banner, the strip and tabs as single sliding rows: the page starts at about a third of the screen.

Hidden ways around

15

Clicking a player's name anywhere opens them on the Players tab. It's handy, but nothing shows it's clickable, so it surprises people (and gets in the way of selecting a name to copy).

Fix: a hover underline and pointer on names, and "Open on Players" in the name's hover box.

16

What already works well, and stays: Night summary's little squares open that pull on the right tab and row; Graphs' "See it in" buttons; Show one player carries across Mechanics, Night summary and Graphs.

Keep.

Two ways to make it smooth

Either way, the quick fixes come along: Back works, the boss name is the picker, the wipe filter moves into the banner, Live only shows when it's live, the menu gets shorter, and the phone top shrinks. The difference is how you pick a pull or the whole night.

Option A · smaller

Keep the tabs, drop the repeats

  • The ∑ All pulls card goes; the Night summary tab is the one way to the whole night.
  • Graphs and Players keep their own All pulls switch.
  • Tabs stay in two groups, This pull and Whole night.

About 1½ hours. Less change for people who already know the site.

Option B · my pick

Pick a pull or the night, then a tab

  • The pull strip starts with a "Whole night" card. Whatever you pick there, every tab follows it.
  • Recap on the whole night is today's Night summary; Mechanics and Assignments show their rows across the pulls (Night summary's sections); Graphs, Rotation and Players their All pulls views.
  • One row of tabs, no groups, no switches inside tabs.

About 3 hours. Mostly rewiring views that already exist.

My take: do Option B. Today a raider has to learn three ideas of "all pulls" and three places to switch boss. With B there's one question at the top ("which pull, or the whole night?"), then the tabs. It's mostly rewiring views we already have, so the risk is low. Do the quick fixes first (Back, the menu, the phone top, Live, the wipe filter chip), since those help everyone right away, then the pull-or-night switch.
