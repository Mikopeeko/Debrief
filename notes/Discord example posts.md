## 1. #bug-reports: How to report a bug (pinned)

**How to report a bug**
Found something that looks wrong or doesn't work? Post it here. Copy the layout below, fill it in, and send it as one message.

**Boss and difficulty:** (for example, Twin Fangs Mythic)
**Link to the pull:** (the Warcraft Logs link, and which pull)
**What you saw:**
**What you expected:**
**Screenshot:** (if you can, drag it into your message)

One bug per message, please. It helps us fix each one faster.

**The other way:** on the site, open the menu (☰) and pick **Report a problem**. It sends the page and pull you're on with it, so you don't need the link.

## 2. #questions: an example question (tags: Assignments, Answered)

Title: How do I add our raid's NSRT note?

Post:
Our raid leader shared our NSRT note with us. Where do I put it on Debrief, and do I have to paste it again for every pull?

Reply (the answer):
Here's how:
1. Open any pull of that boss and go to the **Assignments** tab.
2. Press **Add your NSRT note**.
3. Paste the whole note, from the EncounterID line at the top to the end, and press **Save and check**.

You only do this once per boss. The note stays in your browser, and Debrief checks it on every pull of that boss, tonight and on later nights. When the plan changes, press **Edit note** and paste the new one.

Two tips:
• Names in the note must be character names. NSRT nicknames can't be matched yet.
• Want to see how it works first? On the Assignments tab, press **Try it with an example**.

## 3. #suggestions: an example idea (tag: Website)

Title: Compare two pulls side by side

Post:
**The idea:** pick two pulls of the same boss and see them side by side.

**What it's for:** seeing what changed between a good pull and a bad one, so we know what really made the difference.

**Example:** our best Twin Fangs pull got the boss to 22%, and the next one wiped at 61%. Side by side, I'd see that the first set of Broodlings lived 20 seconds longer on the second pull, and that two soaks were missed.

👍 this post if you'd like it too.
