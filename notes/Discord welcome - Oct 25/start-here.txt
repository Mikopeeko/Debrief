**What is Debrief?**
Debrief reads your raid's combat logs and turns every pull into a short list: what went wrong, what went well, and what to fix before you pull again. It's free, and built for Mythic progression in The Venomous Abyss.

**What you get**
🗺️ **A map of every mechanic.** Open one and see where everyone stood, then watch the few seconds before it went wrong.
❌ **What went wrong, at a glance.** Every pull starts with its problems, worst first.
📋 **Your raid's plan, checked.** Paste your NSRT note once, and Debrief checks every soak, kick, orb and cooldown in it on every pull.
📈 **Graphs.** Damage, healing, casts, cooldowns and more, for one pull or the whole night.
🧾 **A report card for the night.** What went well, what to work on, and one thing for next pull, for every raider.
⚖️ **Compare.** Put yourself next to a top player of your spec.
🎬 **Boss guides.** Animated maps for all ten bosses, Heroic and Mythic. Open to anyone.
🎮 **In your game.** A small Windows app and addon show a recap card in game after each pull (in beta).

**How to start**
1. Go to https://debriefme.io/
2. Paste a Warcraft Logs report link, or open a combat log from your PC.
3. Raiding right now? Press **Go live**, and each pull shows up about a minute after it ends.
