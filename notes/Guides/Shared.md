# Shared facts (same for many specs) — Midnight Season 2, patch 12.1.5

## Stat diminishing returns (both guides, every spec)
Rating thresholds; each step cuts further gains by 10% more:
| Stat | −10% | −20% | −30% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haste | 1320 | 1760 | 2200 |
| Crit, Mastery | 1380 | 1840 | 2300 |
| Versatility | 1620 | 2160 | 2700 |
Percent effects (Bloodlust#2825, Power Infusion, talents like Lycara's Teachings) ignore diminishing returns.
Rating per 1% at max level (Wowhead, Resto Druid): Crit 46, Haste 44, Mastery 46 (spec-dependent %), Versatility 54.

## Threat (Icy Veins, tank guides)
Each enemy keeps its own threat table (no decay). To take aggro: 110% of the top threat in melee (8 yd), 130% at range. Tanks have +950% threat.
Taunt: 6 s fixate (diminishing returns: each repeat −35%, min 1.1 s), sets you equal to the top threat, +800% threat while it lasts.
Most self-healing gives no threat; healing potions do (split across enemies).

## Systems
- Apex talents (new in Midnight): a 3-4 point capstone node in each spec tree.
- Omnium Folio (12.0.7): five rune nodes after a quest chain; node 4 is the stat node. Small bonuses.
- 12.1.5: Ascendant Venomstones (from Oct 20) upgrade Myth/Hero neck, trinkets, weapons/off-hands. New Kith'ix raid items: a trinket, ring and chest.
- Midnight healers: no interrupts except Restoration Shaman (Wind Shear).
