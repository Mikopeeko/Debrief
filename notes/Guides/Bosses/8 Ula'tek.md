# Ula'tek (Venomous Abyss final boss)

Read Oct 19 2026: Wowhead cheat sheet + strategy + journal, Icy Veins (Mythic section not written yet), Mythic Trap, Tactyks Mythic video (khgd47t-ONQ, first kill, ~1 h). The app has an Ula'tek module (see memory "Ula'tek test logs"). Mechanic names style: "Grasping Fangs (Tether breaks)".
Fight style: two targets (boss + tail / heart), 3 phases + intermission. **Lust: Wowhead says Phase 3; Icy Veins says the first Rage of the Shackled (~5 s before the heart opens); Tactyks uses it for the second heart burn after P2 (or as a "prog lust" in P2 if people are dead).** Two raid halves needed.

## Core rule: eggs (Blightscale Spawn)
Eggs fall during the fight. **Broken early** (smashed by Spectral Coils, or damaged to 0) → weak **Blightscale Rawling** (ignore-ish; Heroic: Poisonous Bite stacks). **Hatched** (its cast finishes, it touches venom — Caustic Waves, the water off the platform, or the carrier dies) → deadly **Blightscale Viper** (priority; Heroic+: Petrifying Sting heal absorb + DoT, Acidic Burst; Putrid Membrane raid DoT for 1 h per viper).
Players **pick eggs up** by walking over them (Malignant Shell DoT while carried) to keep them safe until a soak breaks them.

## Phase 1 — Fury of the Serpent Mother (~2 min)
- A tail spawns left or right of the boss; boss and tail share health. **One tank on the boss, one on the tail at all times** (Rattler Slam raid hit if the tail has no one in melee; Unchecked Rage if nobody melees the boss). Swap during Caustic Waves.
- **Caustic Waves#1292403**: she pulls one wing back → waves from that side first, then the other; the tail also sends waves; bubbles/orbs show safe gaps. Hit = huge DoT; carried eggs hatch.
- **Spectral Coils#1287265**: two soak circles beside the tail; damage reduced by players inside → everyone except the boss tank soaks; carried eggs break. Heroic+: **Soul Constrictor** (can't help the next one) → **two groups**, one per soak.
- **Call of the Serpent#1304012**: more eggs fall (dodge the impacts) — pick them up.
- **Mother's Wrath#1298367** (tank): knockback; run back into the red pool in front of her, take the bites (Stone Venom DoT + Hobbled 55% slow → remove with Blessing of Freedom etc.).
- **Mephitic Thrash#1296301** (tail): get out of 35 yd (tank runs out and back), keep eggs safe.
- **Rage of the Shackled#1286860** (after 2 cycles): her **Venomous Heart** is exposed 20 s and takes **+100%** (12.1.5 journal, checked Nov 10), shares health → hit the heart, cleave the boss, dodge **Falling Debris** (hits hard; not a one-shot since Nov 6). Save 2-min cooldowns/potion for it. Tactyks: below ~67% after the first burn is on pace.

## Phase 2 — Children of the Doomscale
Raid splits to the two side ledges (cross the venom; mobility out, Warlock gateway back). Each side:
- **Doomscale Warden**: interrupt **Malice#1290779**; tank it on glowing eggs to cleave them; **Grasping Fangs#1301117** tethers (3 a side on Mythic; 2 a side on Heroic since Nov 6; break → Blight Vein DoT; Heroic+: on the **whole raid**, both sides) → break 2 at a time, wait for the DoT to end (Icy Veins) — Tactyks/Mythic: one solo break, then a 5-break under stacked raid cooldowns.
  **Warden's Protection**: eggs can't be touched (and hurt within 7 yd) while it lives. **Writhing Gestation** (Shadow Molt) turns nearby eggs into a clutch that hatches in 20 s → cleave them.
- After it dies: collect all small eggs, then **one player takes the big Doomscale Egg** last (90 s to pick up, then 20 s to throw it into the **Doomscale Cauldron#1313355**) — small eggs first. Heroic: touching the Doomscale Egg starts **Mass Gestation** on remaining spawn.
- The **Weakened Doomscale**: interrupt **Anguished Cry#1305650** (raid stun), Desperate Thrash frontal away from people; drag adds back to the center.
- At ~4:45 the second heart burn (same as the first).

## Intermission — The Shattering
Eggs rain down — pick them up; tails rise around the room and cast Spectral Coils repeatedly: **Team 1 soaks 1/3/5, Team 2 soaks 2/4/6** (Heroic). Then the platform shatters into four — stand toward the side, not the middle.

## Phase 3 — Ula'tek's Ascension
- Melee/tanks on the boss's platform; ranged/healers one platform counter-clockwise (left).
- **Call of the Serpent** → **Blightscale Clutches** on platforms (circles first) → kill them; eggs that fall into the venom hatch **Blightscale Shriekers** (big add; interrupt **Vicious Echoes**, raid stun + stacking DoT) → tank them to the boss and cleave.
- **Serpent's Bite#1295905**: two players get a 15 s venom since the Nov 6 hotfix (a melee and a ranged in the kills since; before it, one melee, one ranged, one healer) → others stand in their circle until it's fully leeched, or they become a **Calcified Corpse** (wipe). Heroic: one soak group per red circle (melee; ranged and healers). Mythic: the two reds stack and three assigned soakers stand in both (since Oct 6 three clear a set). Soakers then get **Volatile Purge#1306086** → spread 5+ yd.
- **Caustic Waves** with gaps — stand where there are no orbs.
- **Circling Prey#1301510**: she destroys the platform she's at → everyone rotates one platform counter-clockwise; personal defensives for the 2nd and 3rd breaks. Final platform: last Bite set, then the final heart phase — if she's at 1-2% jump off behind her to survive the slam a few seconds longer.

## Mythic
- **Eggs have a health shield** (Hardened, absorbs damage; can't be moved until broken) → spread "egg damage" specs (Balance, Shadow; 2 is enough). Carrying gives **Noxious Shell**: more damage and a **3 yd circle — touching another small-egg carrier hatches a viper** (Noxious Splash). Breaking an egg gives **Rancid Yolk** (+200% Noxious Shell damage 20 s) → wait before the next pickup.
- **Toxic Womb → Blightscale Wretch** (P1 halfway and P3): enrages after 30 s (tank damage only). **Fester Burst**: a 10 yd bubble around it; anyone **outside** takes a big hit + DoT → everyone (incl. egg carriers) gets in; cast speeds up when enraged. Kill wretches before their 3rd bubble.
  **Toxic Incubation**: a spectral Ula'tek beams the wretch (Mother's Boon) → the off-tank soaks it (Toxic Burn) and must not send the resulting waves into eggs.
- All adds enrage (Rawlings after 25 s gain Acidic Expulsion; Doomscales enrage when one dies → kill them evenly; P3 adds too).
- Intermission: **three** soak groups (faster tail cadence).
- P3: Call of the Serpent spawns **Slithering Clutches** that crawl to the venom → **grip them** (needs two Death Knights; a Blood DK's Gorefiend's Grasp helps). Serpent's Bite soakers spawn waves when done → soak with as few players as possible (Tactyks: minimum soak = target + 4).
  Soakers take ~2 million when the circle ends → big defensive (Bear form, Turtle, Ice Cold, Bubble, Desperate Prayer), then personals/externals for an 18 s DoT.
- Tactyks' comp/plan: two DKs (Blood DK tanks common), egg-shield damage (2× Balance/Shadow), funnel/heart burst (Rogues, Warriors, Hunters), Mages for bad egg spots (Blink), Evokers (Time Spiral), at least one Warlock (solo-gate trick with Soulburn for both sides).
  8 egg carriers with zone assignments relative to the boss's facing; assigned soak/bubble spots per tentacle side; NSRT wave-direction macros (three presses in P1), interrupt rotations for the 4 Doomscales, transition soak macros, pre-pot at -15 s / 4:45 / 9:45. P1 is the hardest part.

## What a log can check (module exists)
Vipers hatched (and why: waves, water, carrier death, Noxious Splash), eggs broken in soaks; Spectral Coils soaker counts; Mother's Wrath with nobody in reach; Rattler Slam / Unchecked Rage (tank out of melee); heart damage in each burn; Grasping Fangs break timing; Malice / Anguished Cry / Vicious Echoes interrupts; Doomscale Egg delivery time; Serpent's Bite completion (Calcified Corpse), Volatile Purge clips; deaths to Circling Prey and Falling Debris.
