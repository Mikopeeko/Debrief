# The Twin Fangs (Venomous Abyss boss 6)

Read Oct 19 2026: Wowhead cheat sheet + journal, Icy Veins, Mythic Trap, Tactyks Mythic video (zncJeqViiTs, kill after the early hotfixes). No older research note. Encounter ID 3421.
Fight style: two bosses, **Vexhul** and **Ithraz** (separate health, kill together — Uncoiled Wrath#1308583: the survivor gains +30% damage every 4 s). Lust on pull. Two main-phase sets per platform, then a **Submerge** intermission moves you to the next part of the room; enrage after three platforms (~8 min). Heroic: soak groups needed.

## Core rule: Eternal Venom#1290336
Permanent stacking DoT from many mechanics; too many stacks = death. **Normal 11 (massive damage + stun) / Heroic 10 / Mythic 10** (Tactyks: changed from 9 to 10 by an early hotfix; Mythic Trap still says 9). Ithraz's **Ravenous Feast** removes 1 stack per hit soaked.
Mythic: dying with Eternal Venom drops extra Caustic Globules (Tactyks: you die at 10 → drop 3 orbs → a battle-rezzed player comes back at 7).

## Vexhul
- **Caustic Deluge#1289192**: tank beam that pushes the tank back (stay in range — Concentrated Spittle if not); acid splashes nearby (+1 stack to anyone hit) leave **Caustic Globules#1289993** that explode on the raid after 10 s unless soaked (+1 stack only to the soaker). Low-stack players soak. Envenomed: tank vulnerability to the next Deluge.
- **Venomous Emergence#1291404**: 3 **Spawn of Vexhul** in the center (+1 stack to everyone). Each casts **Corrosive Spit** lines (+1 stack). DPS priority — kill before the **second** Spit. Targets: reposition once, then stand still.
- **Stir the Depths#1290956**: raid pulses + waves crossing the platform (+1 stack) — remember where waves come from and dodge.

## Ithraz
- **Ravenous Feast#1290516**: big soak circle smashed 3 times (split damage, −1 stack each, knockback). Heroic+: **Feasted** = only one hit per player → **three soak groups** (one per hit). Mind the knockback direction (waves/puddles).
- **Coiling Ichor#1290809**: DoT ramping as its circle shrinks; on expiry a **Congealed Gore** puddle (permanent; Heroic+ slows) → go to the platform edge, pack puddles tightly. Time the personal defensive so it covers the **last ticks**.
- **Stone Breaker#1288538** (tank): push-back, then 3 slams in order (marked spots) — the tank stands in each in the order they appeared; missed = raid damage + knockback. Swap after each set (33% vulnerability per hit).

## Intermission — Submerge#1308556
Both dive; venom showers (Noxious Slick puddles, +30% damage taken). **Vile Flood** (Vexhul) = a frontal beam that rotates around the platform (+stacks) — **watch the orbs circling Vexhul to see the direction**; it starts on Vexhul's tank (Icy Veins: move the opposite way of that tank). **Sanguine Storm** (Ithraz) = gore swirlies to dodge (Heroic+ leave puddles).
Tactyks (Mythic): the beam now rotates further — always dodge with the beam's direction; prefer stepping in a puddle over being hit by a wave; mobile players walk, others use a pre-placed Warlock gateway after the waves pass; movement speed and Time Spiral help.

## Mythic
- **Blood Torrent#1303230**: at the same time as Caustic Deluge — a huge healing absorb on the tank (biggest tank damage of the fight; Prot Paladins rotated Bubble / Spellwarding / Bubble; else big externals) and it **shields every Caustic Globule** (Barbed Bulwark: knocks back soakers) → **CC the globule** to break the shield, then soak. Spread AoE CCs around the room (Incapacitating Roar, Song of Chi-Ji, Shockwave, Blinding Light…); wait for nearby orbs to land; single-target CC as backup.
- **Rouse the Brood#1308356**: **14 Broodlings of Ithraz** spawn in ~10 slots around the platform; each casts **Visceral Burst** (raid hit + stacking DoT) — **interrupt every one** (interrupting sends it away). First spawn per platform: one per slot + 4 random; the second spawn per platform: 14 fully random (double/triple ups). Prot Paladins (Divine Toll, Avenger's Shield) and Prot Warriors make this much easier.
  Tactyks' plan: six ground markers lined up on add #3 per side; named slots (L1-L4, R1-R4, front-left/right, L0/R0); tanks take the front ones, extra fillers per side (Resto Shaman), call "back up left 3". **Warlocks: watch your pet** (it got stuck out of range → wipe).
- **Tainted Blood#1310099**: after each Feast, red orbs spawn in a triangle (soak circles, healing absorb, 8 s) — not soaked = raid wipe. Assign: the group knocked **right soaks first, left second, front/middle last** (≥6 per soak; can't immune-solo since the hotfix).
- **Stack pace (Tactyks)**: aim for ~1 stack per orb set → ~3 stacks at the first orb set on platform 2, ~6 on platform 3; up to one above pace is fine. Call "≤3 soak, ≥4 wait". On the last platform **8 is the cap**: the Emergence adds give +1 (→9) and a first Corrosive Spit then kills you — at 9 call it and die in the middle so your orbs are easy to soak.
- Damage plan: cleave from Vexhul into execute first (execute specs), then swap to Ithraz at 35%; on pace ~25% Vexhul / 40% Ithraz at the last platform; kill Vexhul around the final soak before the enrage (then stacks no longer kill and orbs stop exploding).

## What a log can check
Eternal Venom stacks per player over time (deaths at the cap); Corrosive Spit casts per add set (second casts = slow kills); globules exploding unsoaked; Ravenous Feast soakers per hit and Feasted double-soaks; Stone Breaker missed slams; Mythic: Visceral Burst casts not interrupted, Tainted Burst explosions, Barbed Bulwark knockbacks; boss HP gap at the end.
