# The Lost Explorers (Venomous Abyss boss 3, council)

Read Oct 19 2026: Wowhead cheat sheet + journal, Icy Veins, Mythic Trap, Tactyks Mythic video (week-1 kill, his tank POV). Older notes: `notes/Lost Explorers research.md`. Encounter ID 3497.
Fight style: three bosses (two stacked + one wandering). Lust on pull (both written guides). Tactyks: the second-easiest Mythic boss.

## Fight in one line
Mor'zahi (untargetable) controls three tortollans: **First Mate Nama**, **Scrollsage Iku**, **Trader Gebbo** (wanders counter-clockwise, no melee, can't be tanked). They don't share health. Mor'zahi's energy rises to **Final Ascension** (wipe; the control is Dark Whispers#1295451).
To stop it, find the **Disgusting Fish#1292490** in Gebbo's crates and feed it to one tortollan: that resets Mor'zahi and gives *that* boss its ultimate for the next stretch. Each boss can be fed only once.

## Rules
- **United Defense#1297646** (Heroic+): if all three are within 30 yd, they take 99% less damage → only one other boss near Nama at a time. Rotate Nama between Gebbo and Iku; always cleave Nama.
- If a tortollan dies early, the others get angry: Gebbo Smashing Shovel, Iku Cataclysmic Invocation (raid hit, grows), Nama Relentless Escalation (+100% damage every second for 30 s) → **kill them evenly**. Enraged Gebbo is the easiest to handle (tank kites him).
- Ultimate order: Wowhead/Mythic Trap/Icy Veins **Gebbo → Nama → Iku**; Tactyks (and many top guilds) **Iku → Nama → Gebbo**, Gebbo last because with gear you may skip the last empowerment. Any order works with good tuning.
- Feed the fish at **90-95 Mor'zahi energy** (Northern Sky Raid Tools has a countdown) to stretch the fight.

## Base mechanics and solutions
- **Throw Junk#1291933** (Gebbo): crates land on marked spots (dodge). Walk over a crate to break it → stacking bleed **Splinters**. One crate holds the fish. Unbroken crates explode after 25 s (Relic Rupture, big raid hit).
  Solution: assigned breakers (Tactyks: one ranged/tank does most, a different player carries the fish), break **in pairs**, stagger. **Melee must not step on crates by accident.**
- **Shell Spin#1296061** (Nama): 3 shells in a cone at a random melee, stun 4 s. Melee bait toward the edge, away from ranged, then step out.
- **Blink Nova#1292793** (Iku): she teleports to a marked ranged player; raid damage falls off with distance. Take it away (Tactyks: just in front, toward the edge, so cleave continues).
- **Icebound Flames#1286921** (Iku): interrupt (2-3 person rotation); if it lands, dispel.
- **Evil Eyes#1292388**: statue swirlies — dodge.
- Tanks: **Shredding Shards#1295854** (Iku, magic vulnerability) → swap after each. **Steady Strikes#1291930** (Nama, +4% physical per melee) → Tactyks: above ~25 stacks Nama's autos kill tanks; **swap right after Shredding Shards**. The worst overlap is high Nama stacks + Icebound Flames on the tank.

## Ultimates
- **Explosive Surprise#1297625** (Gebbo): bombs on players → drop them at the edge, away from mushrooms. The explosion sends an expanding **Blast Wave** (lethal) → use a **Bouncy Mushroom** (Mushroom Toss) to jump over it as the wave reaches it. Don't trigger mushrooms early (they burst). Heroic+: Spreading Flames fire puddle.
  Mythic: **only one mushroom** — don't waste it; bait it, then drop the bomb across from it.
- **Mighty Thud#1296092** (Nama): 3 players marked, Nama leaps to each (split damage within 6 yd, knockback; unsoaked = raid damage), leaving Aftershock ground. Options: line the 3 up and get knocked from one into the next with the whole raid (Tactyks: stand at the bottom of each soak),
  or three teams (Groups 1/2, 3/4, 5/6). Avoid overlapping with many Splinters stacks or a Blink Nova.
- **Frostfire Volley#1295893** (Iku): ranged get fire, melee (and tanks) get frost; each drops a patch and DoT. Drop fire on one side, frost on the other, at the edges; then clear your DoT by walking into the *opposite* patch (raid damage → healing CDs).
  **Never touch the opposite element while still debuffed** (Elemental Explosion, lethal raid hit). Personal defensive here.

## Mythic changes
- **Splinters is raid-wide**: breaking a crate puts a 6 s bleed on everyone → manage crate breaks as raid damage; fewer crates appear. Unbroken crates still explode.
- Splinters/Blink Nova/Mighty Thud overlaps are the danger moment.
- Positioning (Tactyks): the two tanked-together bosses trail Gebbo; the third boss sits just behind, 30+ yd from the pair. Ranged (Evokers especially) can hit all three.
