# Nymrissa Wavecaller (listed by Mythic Trap as "Boss #1" of The Venomous Abyss)

Read Oct 19 2026 from Mythic Trap only. Wowhead and Icy Veins list just 8 bosses (Nek'zali first) and don't mention her, so she may be a side or optional encounter, or added later. **Check before building anything for her.** Spell IDs come from Mythic Trap.

- Single phase, 6-minute enrage (**Unending Tides#1294867**).
- **Alluring Bubble#1257717**: a bubble in the middle lures Murloc adds from several spots. Kill them before they reach it (they're empowered when it pops).
- **Swirling Whirlpools#1258668**: whirlpools form at the edges and move to the center. Find the one safe gap. When they reach the bubble it pops (raid hit and knockback).
- **Chilling Frost#1313393**: players drop Frost Orbs every tick. Soak them by running through (DoT, 15% slow per orb, ice puddle). Heroic: orbs explode if not soaked within 30 s.
- **Abyssal Rain#1260837**: raid pulses, then a long DoT. Heroic: +5% boss damage per cast.
- **Iceblade Flurry#1282937**: a tank DoT with +20% to the next one, so swap tanks.
- Mythic: **Waterfog Shield#1273091**. Bubblefin Frostscale adds give nearby adds 99% damage reduction, so kill them first. **Frost Burst#1313450**: each orb soak hits the raid, so soak slowly. **Water Jet#1258901**: a tank frontal beam (+20% per tick) that also clears ice puddles.
