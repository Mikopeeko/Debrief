# Vashnik the Malignant (Venomous Abyss boss 4, first wing end)

Read Oct 19 2026: Wowhead cheat sheet + journal, Icy Veins, Mythic Trap, Tactyks Mythic video (xoTdiZPLU7U, week-2 kill). Older notes with log IDs and Wolfthorn's pulls: `notes/Vashnik research.md`. Encounter ID 3455.
Fight style: single target with adds. Lust on pull (both written guides). One phase on a loop; soft enrage = stacking Toxic Vapor#1284561 (+ bigger infusion hits each Imbibe).

## Fight in one line
The room is split in thirds by three fountains — **Flame (orange), Shadow (purple), Blood (red)**. At 100 energy (~every 84 s) **Imbibe#1283164** drains the **two fountains nearest Vashnik**: raid hit per fountain, an infusion stack (+100% that fountain's hit and +50% its adds' HP for 90 s, stacking),
that fountain's adds, and that fountain's version of **Adaptive Infection**. Adds crawl to the **Malignant Cavity** in the middle → **Malignant Burst** (huge raid hit + DoT) if they arrive. Toxic Vapor stack each Imbibe.

## Adds (kill before they reach the middle)
- **Burning Venom** (Flame; 2, **3 on Mythic**): Burning Presence pulses raid damage while alive → highest priority, grip/drag them into the others. Death = **Caustic Surge** raid hit (Heroic+: stacking DoT) → **kill them several seconds apart** (Tactyks/log ~9-10 s). After 60 s any add hardens (CC immune, +50% speed).
- **Shrouded Venom** (Shadow; 5, 8 seen on Mythic): **Miasmic Coating** absorb = 100% HP (killed twice); on death Umbral Ejection swirlies → dodge. Lowest priority; slow/root (Mass Entanglement) and cleave.
- **Clotting Venom** (Blood; 1): CC-immune, slow; splits twice on death into smaller CC-able clots.
- Priority (Icy Veins): Burning → Clotting → Shrouded.

## Infections (by fountain)
- Flame — **Exploding Infection#1295173**: dispellable; removing it explodes (Caustic Explosion) on the raid, damage falls with distance → targets run out; **dispel one at a time** (not together). Mythic: its DoT ramps every 1.5 s until dispelled; sets of 4.
- Shadow — **Stygian Infection#1294994**: big heal absorb; the player drops Stygian Burst zones → keep moving away from the raid while healers clear the absorb.
- Blood — **Siphoning Infection#1299941**: 100% healing reduction + absorb; others stand in the circle so Siphon Blood drains them to heal it off (Mythic: Thinned Blood doubles the drain damage).

## Other mechanics
- **Plague Froth#1281907**: a few players; after ~6 s four **Plague Waves** shoot out in the cardinal directions (big hit). Targets spread, stand still, aim lines away from people.
- **Malignant Catalyst#1282525** (Heroic+): raid hit + **Catalytic Bile** circles; each must be soaked by ≥1 player or it hits the whole raid → ranged spread around the room for coverage.
- **Dripping Fangs#1280935**: tank, +200% physical taken, stacking DoT → swap every cast.
- Tanking: position Vashnik **between the two fountains you want**. Icy Veins rotates (Shadow+Flame → Flame+Blood → Blood+Shadow, never the same fountain more than twice); the Blood fountain is farther — cross well past the floor line.

## Mythic
- **Malignant Totems** (Icy Veins calls them Malignant Tumors, Hardened 99%): each Imbibe raises ~23-24 around the two empowered fountains. Only **Plague Waves** destroy them (they turn white when hit). Any left at the next Imbibe cast **Malignance** (raid hit + 1-min DoT): **one is survivable, two+ is a wipe**.
  Plague Froth targets 5 players, twice per Imbibe cycle → between the two sets, hit every totem.
- Mythic Malignant Burst adds a stacking 1-min DoT.

### Tactyks' "cheese" (and Wolfthorn's plan): always Flame + Shadow
- Hold Vashnik between Shadow and Flame **every Imbibe** — never touch Blood. Little movement, no Clotting Venoms. The DPS check gets easy; the **healing check gets hard** (the same infusions stack to 4-5) → **5 healers** recommended.
- **Two Death Knights** ideal (any spec): one pre-walks and grips the fire adds in, one grips the first fire add to the boss; one fire add banished (unbanish when ready); a light CC (Freezing Trap / Paralysis) on the far one, perma-CC at 4-5 infusion stacks. Shadow adds: Mass Grip on odd sets, Typhoon/Ursol's on even (Sigil of Chains works every time).
- **First Froth of each cycle — "mow the lawn"**: ground markers in a slightly diagonal line across the totem area; boss pinned between two markers, melee pixel-stacked under the boss (safe gap); melee targets take the two center markers, ranged the outer ones (X, triangle, moon) — the lines don't cross anyone.
  **Second Froth**: call where totems remain ("two to blue, one to orange…"); assigned ranged favor each side; mobile melee leave to clear far totems. Totem coverage first, not hitting melee second. Damage doesn't matter during these sets.
- Dispels in pairs of healers (2 per set). No need to rush Exploding Infection; never dispel right before the 4th/5th Imbibe.
- **Imbibe 5 = biggest hit of the fight** (double 5-stack): stack Rally, Spirit Link, Aura Mastery/Devotion, AMZ, personals. Burn the boss so the last totem set can be ignored; in the final set don't kill the fire adds (their death damage + the DoT kill people) — CC them and burn Vashnik before Imbibe 6.

## What a log can check (see research note for IDs)
Imbibe fountains chosen; adds reaching the cavity; Burning Venom deaths too close together; Exploding Infection dispels too close together; totems left at each Imbibe (Malignance casts); unsoaked bile (1282616); Plague Wave hits on players; Dripping Fangs swaps; deaths at Imbibe 5.
