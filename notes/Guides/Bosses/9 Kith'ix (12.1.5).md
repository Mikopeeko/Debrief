# Kith'ix — The Unbinding of Kith'ix (patch 12.1.5, single-boss raid, live Oct 13)

Read Oct 19 2026: Wowhead overview/cheat sheet + journal, Icy Veins, Mythic Trap, Tactyks Mythic video (6qoUDq6wU2Q, day-one kill, 25-man flex). Boss NPC 267861. Difficulties: Story, LFR, Normal, Heroic, **Mythic Flex (15-25)**. Loot: cantrip items (no tier, no mount, no CE); ilvl 285/298/311/324.
Fight style: single target with add cleave. Lady Liadrin helps. **Lust: Phase 2** (Wowhead, Icy Veins); **Tactyks lusts on pull on Mythic** (see below). Dispel: Overwhelming Fear.

## Phase 1 — Darkness' Domain (until Kith'ix 100 energy)
- **Voidswarm#1301511** (6 s channel, raid damage) → adds. Keep adds away from Kith'ix (**Commanding Presence#1305008**: −50/75% damage taken near him; not eggs). **All adds must be dead before the intermission** (survivors get Dark Frenzy: +100% speed, CC immune, raid pulses).
  - **Venomous Hulk#268190**: off-tank takes it onto a Voidweaver for cleave, faces it away (**Venom Roar** cone + knockback — negate with Death's Advance etc.).
  - **Voidweaver#268192**: stationary caster, DPS priority (Icy Veins); **Nightfall#1302705** stacking raid DoT; Voidweave beams on random players.
  - **Bomber Beetle#268194**: fixates players for 30 s; explodes on contact or at 30 s → **Digestive Juices#1302319** stacking raid DoT. CC/slow them and detonate **one at a time** (Death Grip them into a player / an assigned runner).
  - Heroic+: **Incubation#1307407**: dead adds drop **Mindstinger eggs** → kill before they hatch (Mindsting stun + DoT).
- **Extinguish#1304424** (8 s cast): huge shadow raid hit that removes Light's Embrace → Liadrin scatters **Light's Embrace#1304508** motes near living adds; **everyone grabs one** (−90% shadow). No mote = personal/external.
- **Unspeakable Horrors#1304045** → **Overwhelming Fear#1304046**: marked players have 6 s to run out, then they're feared/rooted; dispelling releases a scream whose raid damage falls with distance → dispel them **one at a time**, away from the group.
- **Twisted Appendage#1303681**: tentacle slam circles — dodge.
- **Dark Devastation#1304930** (tank): big 15 yd circle on the tank → run it away from the raid, defensive; **Devastated** (+150% physical/shadow) → swap every cast. Heroic+: leaves a **Voidscar#1304950** puddle (knockback on contact) → place toward the edge.

## Intermission (Kith'ix at 100 energy) — Suffocating Darkness
Darkness closes in (Suffocation outside = pacify + heavy damage). **Stay inside Liadrin's Refulgent Bulwark#1302952**; keep handling P1 boss mechanics in less space. **Abyssal Grasp#1303406**: heal absorb + pull to the dark — remove within 8 s (health pot/stone yourself).
Heroic+: Voidscars release **Eclipse Fragments#1308674** that float to Liadrin → **intercept them** (stacking DoT on you and within 3 yd); a fragment reaching her = raid DoT and a smaller bubble. Tanks help. Drop the Devastation circle just outside the bubble edge.
Liadrin at 100 energy → **Divine Radiance** clears the dark and stuns Kith'ix 8 s (not a damage window); she gains **Exhausted** (smaller/weaker each time — soft enrage).

## Phase 2 — Last Light (25%)
Stygian Howl stuns everyone; all adds are absorbed. **Last Light#1305427** full heal + **Luminous Grace#1305428** (+damage, +100% healing). **Eightfold Eclipse#1301479**: an eclipse (raid hit) about every ~8 s; the **8th kills everyone** — ~1 minute to kill him.
- **Null Gate#1308873**: a melee, a ranged and a healer get a gate dropped at their spot (Mythic: +1 ranged) → spread to assigned drop spots; gates spray **Nullshards#1308881** → dodge. **Nullify#1308875** stacks kill you at: Normal 6 / Heroic 5 (Wowhead) — the journal says 4 — **Mythic 3** (Icy Veins, Tactyks).
- **Negation#1318467** (tank): +100% speed, damage over time, immune to shards → taunt swap; run into a Null Gate within 8 s (closes it; **Explosive Restabilization** raid hit). Mythic: needs **three** gates (stacks) before it expires.

## Mythic and Tactyks' plan
- Bomber Beetles also drop an egg (Hulk 5, Voidweaver 3 eggs on Mythic) → **grip beetles onto the Hulk/Voidweaver one at a time** so eggs land in the cleave (two DKs ideal; slow but don't root — Death and Decay / Chains of Ice). Nobody stands in the middle during adds (stepping on a beetle: 1 Juices stack free, 2 dangerous, 3 death).
- Light motes go toward the Hulk if alive, else a Voidweaver, else the boss → walk the Hulk to the raid's Voidweaver so the orbs spawn there.
- Voidscar fragments on Mythic: 6 yd splash, 10 s → no solo soaks; two soakers alternate per scar (ranged-only soakers, since Horrify picks one per role) with backups; tank soaks get split (the non-debuffed tank takes most).
- Horrify dispels in the intermission: each role goes to its marker (healer skull, melee star, ranged X); dispel the healer first. Intermission overlap (Horrify + Abyssal Grasp) = raid cooldowns (Rally, AMZ, Darkness, Spirit Link).
- **The hardest part is the 3rd intermission → skip it**: Lust + potions + all cooldowns on pull, hold the 2-min cooldowns from the 4-min mark for P2, and push to 25% before the third beetle set (~4:10-4:20). P2 is then an easy DPS check (they had 15 s spare with many deaths).
- Tactyks: about Mythic Twin Fangs difficulty; many guilds will just bonus-roll Heroic for the Mythic-track loot.

## What a log can check
Adds alive at the intermission (Dark Frenzy), adds near the boss; Digestive Juices stacks / beetles popped together; Mindstinger hatches; Extinguish hits without Light's Embrace; Fear dispels too close together; Devastation swaps; Abyssal Grasp drags; fragments reaching Liadrin; Nullify deaths; Negation gates; time to 25% and Eclipse count at the kill.
