## 0. Nymrissa Wavecaller (3379)
Pages: **Frost Orb soakers**, **Murloc grips and slows**, cooldowns.
- Frost Orbs (Chilling Frost drops them; Mythic: every soak hits the raid with Frost Burst, so soak slowly, few at a time).
  Logic: few soakers who can take stacks or clear them: the off-tank first, then Ice Cold Mages, Rogues (Cloak/Feint),
  Hunters (Turtle), Anti-Magic Shell Death Knights. A small fixed crew, not everyone.
  Kills: exactly that. One tank took most (Blood DK 84 with Anti-Magic Shell 10 times; Protection Paladin 39; Blood DK +
  Vengeance 50), helped by Arcane Mages, Rogues, a Hunter, a Balance Druid, a Restoration Shaman.
  Rule: Orb Soak = the off-tank, then 3-4 of: Mage, Rogue, Hunter, Death Knight, Balance Druid (reason names their tool).
- Murlocs must not reach the bubble; Frostscales first. Grips pull strays in, slows hold them.
  Kills: Blood DK Death Grip 6-11 a fight, Gorefiend's Grasp, Chains of Ice, Typhoon, Binding Shot.
  Rule: Grips = every Death Knight (Death Grip), then Balance (Typhoon/Ursol's), Hunter (Binding Shot), Vengeance (Sigil of Chains).
- Healers: all three top kills ran 3 healers (a fact, not a job).

## 1. Nek'zali the Soulcoiler (3470)
Pages: **Well teams** (Mythic), **Echo kicks** (Mythic), **Hungering Pyre soak / stay-out**, **Essence Rend dispels**, grips.
- Well teams (Grasping Depths, Mythic): go into the well, kill the Drowned Echo, kick Soulcoiler's Curse (2 melee kicks or
  3 others); Soul Exhaustion means teams alternate. Logic (guide, Tactyks): two teams of 4 physical DPS + 1 healer
  (Warriors, Hunters, Monks, Rogues, Death Knights); keep magic-damage ranged (Balance, Shadow, Warlocks) upstairs to break
  the Amani's magic shields. Kills: the fastest raids barely needed it (one solo Havoc DH in one kill, none in two), but that's
  gear outpacing the mechanic. Rule: keep the guide's two teams; each team lists its 1st and 2nd kicker (melee).
- Hungering Pyre: logic: melee + tanks + healers soak near the center; mobile ranged stay out on purpose (Cremation burns
  stray corpses). Kills: exactly that in all three (10-14 soak; Warlocks, Mages, Shadow, Hunters out).
  Rule: Pyre Soak = tanks, melee, healers (+ a ranged or two to reach the count); Don't soak = Mages, Warlocks, Shadow
  Priests, Hunters (they burn corpses).
- Essence Rend dispels: logic: dispel at the edge, one at a time; healers with magic dispels. Kills: 2-3 healers shared it.
  Rule: two named dispellers (healers), with a backup.
- Grips: Blood DK Death Grip on stray Amani (6-11 a fight). Rule: every Death Knight; Chains of Ice to slow.

## 2. Entombed Sentinels (3445)
Pages: **Blood / Breath sides**, **Miasma soakers**, **Toxic Droplets**, **Blighted Blood dispels**, cooldowns.
- Sides: logic: half the raid per boss, 2 healers each, multi-dotters split evenly, strong two-target melee on the green
  (Breath) side (more adds). Kills: 12/8 and 10/10 splits, 2 healers each side, Arms Warriors on green in two of three.
  Rule: split evenly, healers 2/2 (or 3/2), melee leaning green, Warlocks/Balance/Shadow split across both.
- Unstable Miasma (Mythic 8+): logic: 8+ with defensives and stacked raid cooldowns. Kills: most of the raid soaked each one.
  Rule: name 8-10 soakers from the red side plus spares, raid cooldowns on it (Anti-Magic Zone, Spirit Link, Rally).
- Toxic Droplets: logic (guide): tanks with a defensive soak most. Kills: a tank most, but healers and ranged with
  defensives took many (14, 11, 10…). Rule: the green tank first, then 3-4 green-side players with good personals
  (Barkskin, Turtle/Bubble/Cloak at the worst overlap) as droplet helpers.
- Blighted Blood: dispels by healers (kills: 3-4 healers shared). Rule: two named dispellers.

## 3. The Lost Explorers (3497)
Pages: **Fish carrier**, **Crate breakers**, **Icebound Flames kicks**, **Mighty Thud plan**, cooldowns.
- Fish: logic: a mobile player who isn't breaking crates, alive all fight. Kills: an Arcane Mage twice, a Restoration
  Shaman once. Rule: a Mage first (Blink), else a mobile ranged or healer; never a crate breaker.
- Crates: logic (guide): assigned breakers in pairs, staggered (Mythic Splinters hits the whole raid); one ranged or tank
  does most. Kills: can't be read (Splinters is raid-wide). Rule: two pairs of breakers (a tank + mobile ranged).
- Icebound Flames (Iku): logic: a 2-3 person kick rotation, melee/tank on Iku. Kills: the tank kicked the most (Blood DK
  Mind Freeze 7 of 11; Protection Paladin's Avenger's Shield), plus 1-2 melee. Rule: Iku's tank first, then two melee.
- Mighty Thud: the whole raid soaks in order (kills agree). A note line only, no names.

## 4. Vashnik the Malignant (3455)
Pages: **Grips and add CC**, **Exploding Infection dispels**, **Catalytic Bile soakers**, **Plague Froth spots**, cooldowns.
- Grips/CC: logic (Tactyks): two Death Knights grip the fire adds (one pre-walks), a Warlock banishes one, light CC on the
  far one, Mass Grip / Typhoon / Ursol's on shadow adds. Kills: Blood DK Death Grip 17 (+ a second DK 3-7), Gorefiend's 2-3,
  Banish 3-4 by a Demonology Warlock. Rule: every DK on grips, a Warlock on Banish, then Hunter/Monk/Druid light CC.
- Exploding Infection: logic: one at a time, in pairs of healers. Kills: 2-3 healers (Restoration Shaman 10, Holy Priest 8…).
  Rule: two healer dispellers in order, one backup.
- Catalytic Bile: logic: ranged spread round the room. Kills: Hunters, Mages, Priests, Restoration Shaman. Rule: 5-6 ranged
  and healers, one per spot.
- Imbibe 5: stacked raid cooldowns (kills: Rally, Anti-Magic Zone, Aura Mastery, Spirit Link). Comes from the cooldown plan.

## 5. Sszorak (3420)
Pages: **Mutilate groups**, **Fury bait**, **Cyst poppers / exit popper**, cooldowns.
- Mutilate (Heroic+: two groups alternate; Mutilated Gash): logic: two halves, one tank in each (the tanks swap per
  combo), healers split, melee and ranged mixed. Kills: exactly that in all three (two groups of ~10, one tank each).
  Rule: Group 1 / Group 2 of half the raid each, tank + half the healers each.
- Serpent's Fury (Mythic): one mobile player baits it (farthest from the boss), 14 stack on them. Logic: a Hunter or other
  mobile ranged (Mage, Evoker). Rule: one bait + a backup.
- Cysts: the 4th (exit) cyst popper: a mobile player. Rule: one named popper.
- Raging Crosswinds pairs are positional (raid lead's call), not assigned.

## 6. Ula'tek (3492)
Pages: **Doomscale kick teams** (per side), **Egg carriers**, **Transition soak groups**, **Spectral Coils teams**,
**Serpent's Bite soakers**, **Toxic Incubation (off-tank)**, **Grips** (P3 clutches), cooldowns.
- Doomscale kicks (Malice, Anguished Cry; Shrieker Vicious Echoes): logic: per side, the side's tank + 2 melee + a ranged.
  Kills: melee and a Mage per side, tanks often. Rule: each side's team of 3-4, tank first.
- Egg carriers (Mythic: 8, no two carriers touching): logic: mobile players and those who can survive Noxious Shell;
  Balance/Shadow free to break shields. Kills: Arcane Mages, Hunters, Frost DK, Arms, Retribution, Rogues, a Restoration
  Shaman. Rule: Mages and Hunters first, then melee, then a healer; never the off-tank or the egg-damage specs.
- Toxic Incubation: the off-tank (kills: Blood DK most, the other tank some). Rule: the off-tank.
- Grips (P3 Slithering Clutches): two Death Knights (kills: Blood + Frost DK 7-26 grips). Rule: every DK.
- Blessing of Freedom on Hobbled: Paladins (kills: 5-7 each). Rule: a Paladin per tank as a reminder.
- Serpent's Bite soakers (minimum target + 4, big defensive): three groups (melee / ranged / healers).

roots); Voidscar soaks by two ranged per scar alternating; Horrify dispels by role markers, healer first; Null Gate: a
melee, a ranged, a healer (Mythic +1 ranged) to marked spots. Check against top kills once they're listed.

## What all the kills share (for every boss)
- **Death Knights are the grip class**: every boss with adds used Death Grip heavily (6-26 a fight), Gorefiend's Grasp too.
- **Protection Paladins kick far above one kicker** (Avenger's Shield), and tanks kick a lot in general.
- **Mages** are the go-to carriers and soakers (Blink/Shimmer, Ice Cold): Nymrissa orbs, the fish, Ula'tek eggs, Coiled
  Altar purples.
- **Dispels are shared by 2-3 healers**, never one.
- **Raid cooldowns stack on the one biggest moment** (Vashnik's Imbibe 5, Sentinels' Miasma), which the cooldown plan finds.
