# Frost Death Knight (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): no ability/talent changes; power shifted out of Pillar of Frost#51271 (nerfed) into weaker abilities. Buffed: Howling Blast#49184, Empower Rune Weapon#47568, Glacial Advance#194913,
Icy Death Torrent#435010, Frost Fever, Remorseless Winter#196770, Frostscythe#207230, Frost Strike#49143, Permafrost, Deathly Blows. Nerfed: Pillar, Obliterate#49020, Breath of Sindragosa#1249658,
Frostbane#455993, Frostwyrm's Fury#279302, Wither Away, Reaper's Mark#439843, Exterminate#441378, Undeath.

## The spec in one line
Spend Killing Machine#51128 on Obliterate (ST) / Frostscythe (3+), spend Rime#59057 on Howling Blast, spend Runic Power on Frost Strike / Glacial Advance, and use Empower Rune Weapon to avoid ever
pressing Obliterate without Killing Machine. Pillar of Frost on cooldown with Frostwyrm's Fury inside it.

## Hero trees and builds
- **Deathbringer** — Wowhead "Best" in raid, M+ and delves; wins all multi-target. Reaper's Mark (45 s) syncs with every Pillar; Exterminate = free cleave + 2 Killing Machines (and −1 Rune on Obliterate/Frostscythe);
  Bind in Darkness#440031 buffs Howling Blast.
- Rider of the Apocalypse: better on Patchwerk single target and when you need mobility (Death Charge#444010) or big 90 s spikes; needs Frostwyrm's Fury for Apocalypse Now#444040.
- Icy Veins: **Dual-Wield Breath of Sindragosa build in all content** (Shattering Blade#207057/Frostbane route is the alternative; Wowhead: Frostbane doesn't hit hard enough now).
- Raid Rider: only swap is Frostreaper#1230301 → Northwinds#1230284 if pressing Frostscythe more than Obliterate. Deathbringer cleave build for fights with lots of adds.
- Apex **Chosen of Frostbrood#1265632**: Frostwyrm's Fury +100% and a Haste buff; casting it in Pillar extends Pillar 2/4 s; final rank +10% Frost damage, another +100%, and a recall at 50% for 45 s.
  **Never cast Frostwyrm's Fury outside Pillar.**
- Runeforges: Fallen Crusader#53344 always; Shattering Blade builds put Razorice#53343 on main hand; other dual-wield builds use Stoneskin Gargoyle#62158 + Fallen Crusader (Glacial Advance applies Razorice). 2H: Fallen Crusader.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload.

## Single target priority — Deathbringer (Wowhead)
1. Empower Rune Weapon if at 2 charges.
2. Reaper's Mark → Pillar of Frost → Breath of Sindragosa → Frostwyrm's Fury.
3. Obliterate with 2 Killing Machine stacks (or 1 + Exterminate).
4. Howling Blast with Rime. (Frostbane when it procs.)
5. Frost Strike to avoid Runic Power waste.
6. Obliterate with Killing Machine.
7. Empower Rune Weapon to generate Killing Machine.
8. Frost Strike. 9. Obliterate without Killing Machine. 10. Howling Blast.
Rider: same minus Reaper's Mark/Exterminate.
Icy Veins ST: Obliterate with KM → Frost Strike at 5 Razorice (Shattering Blade) → Howling Blast with Rime → Frost Strike → ERW → no-KM Obliterate.
- Nuance (Icy Veins): with 3+ Runes take the Killing Machine; if low on Runes and 70+ RP, Rime/Frost Strike first. Always spend 2 KM stacks at once. Don't *aim* for 2 stacks (Killing Streak#1230153 isn't worth it — Wowhead).
- No-KM Obliterate is the last thing you press; use all Runic Power first.

## AoE (3+ targets)
Frostscythe replaces Obliterate, Glacial Advance replaces Frost Strike (Wowhead: Glacial Advance at 4+ in the cleave build). Remorseless Winter if not on Frozen Dominion. Frostbane above Rime if talented.
Stand at the edge of the pack facing in (Breath, Glacial Advance, Icy Death Torrent are frontal).

## Openers
- Wowhead Deathbringer: ERW → Pillar → Reaper's Mark → Breath → Obliterate → Frostwyrm's Fury → rotation. Rider: ERW → Obliterate → Breath → Pillar → Frostwyrm's Fury → ERW.
- Icy Veins ST: ERW → Obliterate → Reaper's Mark → Remorseless Winter → Breath + Pillar + Frostwyrm's Fury + potion + trinket + Raise Dead → Obliterate → ERW → Obliterate.

## Cooldowns
- **Pillar of Frost** (+20% Strength 12 s, 45 s): on cooldown with potion/trinkets; can hold a few s for a priority target. Macro it with an on-GCD spell so the first GCD is inside it.
  Obliteration#281238: during Pillar, Frost Strike/Glacial Advance/Howling Blast each give Killing Machine → weave generate/spend every GCD. Enter with resources (avoid no-KM Obliterate in the 5 s before when under 4 Runes).
- **Frostwyrm's Fury**: at the start of Pillar (Haste buff + Pillar extension); recall ~12 s later still inside Pillar (off GCD), ideally with Unholy Strength#53365 + Bonegrinder#377103 up. Rider: right after Pillar (Apocalyptic Conquest overlaps Pillar's Strength).
- **Reaper's Mark**: on cooldown, same GCD as Pillar (macro). Don't reapply to a target that already has it (ends the old one early).
- **Breath of Sindragosa**: 60 RP to start, 8 s, extended by spending KM and Rime (up to 30 s). Pool 60 RP before Pillar. It grants an ERW charge — never press it at 2 ERW charges.
- **Empower Rune Weapon**: off GCD; use before capping charges, and when you'd otherwise press no-KM Obliterate; lots of value in Pillar.
- Remorseless Winter: automatic with Frozen Dominion#377226 (from Pillar). Without it: on cooldown, synced to Pillar (Gathering Storm), consume Cryogenic Chamber at 20 stacks.
- Killing Machine procs from auto-attack crits: dual wield 30/60/90/100% escalating chance; 2H always procs on crit but ~28% fewer procs overall.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: Remorseless Winter damage gives stacking attack speed and Icy Death Torrent damage.
- 4-piece: Remorseless Winter ticks 25% more often and the buff lasts longer. Passive (Remorseless is automatic from Pillar).

## Stats
- Wowhead (both trees): Strength > Crit > Mastery > Haste > Vers. Mastery: Frozen Heart#77514 = 2% Frost damage per 1%; first DR at 76%.
- Icy Veins: Crit > Haste > Mastery > Vers (Deathbringer a bit lower on Haste — sim).
