# Subtlety Rogue (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5).
**12.1.5 change** (Wowhead): Shadow damage interactions reworked — clone attacks work reliably and spells get each damage amplifier only once; Subtlety got +14% to compensate. Builds may shift over time.
12.1: Goremaw's Bite#426591 reworked (no CP; bleed on up to 3 targets that repeats 20% of your finishers' damage — now baseline); Shadow Techniques#196912 buffed, Shadowcraft#426594 Energy nerfed; Lingering Shadow#382524 works with Shuriken Storm
(taken only with the 4-piece); Secret Technique#280719 shadow part reworked. Midnight removed Flagellation, Symbols of Death, Rupture; Secret Technique static 25 s (haste-reduced), Shadow Dance#185313 static 20 s; Shadow Blades#121471 doubles builder CP.

## The spec in one line
A 90-second burst spec: **Shadow Blades + two Shadow Dances** every 90 s, plus a Shadow Dance whenever Secret Technique is ready. Inside Dance: Secret Technique as the first finisher, Shadowstrike#185438 builder,
Eviscerate#196819 / Black Powder#319175 (2+ targets) at 6+ CP. Outside: Backstab#53 / Shuriken Storm#197835, finish at 6+, and accept Energy starvation. Combo points come largely from Shadow Techniques (stay in auto range).

## Rotation (both guides)
- Open from Stealth#1784 with Shadowstrike. Goremaw's Bite on cooldown right before your other cooldowns.
- **Shadow Dance** when Secret Technique is ready or during Shadow Blades, entered at very low (≤2) or finishing (6+) CP — never on a GCD (Deepening Shadows#185314 adds only a little time, every ms counts; macro it with the first spell).
  Don't Dance if Shadow Blades is <10 s from ready (you want two Dances in Blades). The second Dance in Blades has no Secret Technique (unless Bloodlust).
- Shadow Blades during/with Shadow Dance (+ trinket, Light's Potential, racials).
- Finishers at 6+ CP: Deathstalker: Eviscerate with Darkest Night first → Secret Technique (in Dance) → Coup de Grace → Eviscerate (Black Powder at 2+). Secret Technique can go outside Dance if it comes back for the next Dance.
- Builders: Shadowstrike in Dance/Stealth, else Backstab (Shuriken Storm at 2+). Vanish#1856 on cooldown at low CP (outside Dance) → Shadowstrike.
- Setup: stop finishing a few seconds before Secret Technique is ready to bank 5+ Shadow Techniques stacks (keep building at full CP to not cap Energy); Trickster enter Dance with 7 CP + 5+ stacks + Ancient Arts.
  Deathstalker: e.g. full CP + 1 Mark stack + 4-5 stacks, or Darkest Night up + 5+ stacks. At 4+ targets: Secret Technique + Black Powder with Supercharger (first 2 finishers) and Ancient Arts.
- Thistle Tea: auto. Pre-pull: Shadow Dance once >20 s before the pull to prime Supercharger#470347.

## Openers
Deathstalker ST: Shadowstrike → Eviscerate → Vanish → Shadowstrike → Goremaw's Bite → Eviscerate → Shadow Dance + Shadow Blades + trinket + potion → Shuriken Storm → Secret Technique → (Shadowstrike, Eviscerate) x.
Trickster ST: Shadowstrike → Eviscerate → Vanish → Shadowstrike → Goremaw's Bite → Shadow Dance + Blades → Secret Technique → Shadowstrike/Eviscerate. AoE: Shadowstrike → Goremaw's → (Eviscerate) → Dance + Blades → Storm/Secret Technique → Storm → Black Powder.
Poisons: Instant + Atrophic (Numbing if a second rogue). Slice and Dice is kept by Cut to the Chase#51667.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: cheaper builders → fill nearly every GCD outside Dance with Backstab/Shuriken Storm.
- 4-piece: Lingering Shadow also affects Eviscerate/Black Powder (mandatory talent); second half of Shadow Blades stronger. No rotation change.
