# Arcane Mage (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): new Refractive Images#1309497 (arcane stagger defensive); Apex replaced with **Prismatic Bolt#1295924**; Orb Mastery#1243435 no longer eats Clearcasting#263725; Sunfury max 3 Spellfire Spheres; Arcane Missiles#5143 buffed a lot.
Midnight removed Nether Precision, Shifting Power, Ice Floes, Mass Barrier; Mirror Image/Greater Invisibility no longer reduce damage; Arcane Harmony → Arcane Salvo#384452; barriers half GCD.

## The spec in one line
Arcane Surge#365350 + Touch of the Magi#321507 every 90 s (bigburn), Touch alone at 45 s (miniburn). In between: build Arcane Charges#36032 (4) and Arcane Salvo stacks with Arcane Blast#30451 / Arcane Missiles (Clearcasting),
spend on Arcane Barrage#44425 at the right Salvo count, and fire every Prismatic Bolt. Mana barely matters this season.

## Hero trees
- **Sunfury in all content** (both guides). Arcane Phoenix during Surge; after Surge ends: **Arcane Soul#451038** (Barrage gives Clearcasting, 4 Charges, 5 Salvo and doesn't spend Salvo → spam Barrage) + Ashes of Inspiration#1260272.
  Max Salvo 25. Merely a Setback / Time Twist strong defense.
- Spellslinger: Arcane Splinters, Arcane Orb#153626 focus (orbs can fall through floors / miss elevated targets); max Salvo 20; weaker defense.
- Raid single target → cleave: swap Impetus#383676 + Arcane Familiar#205022 + Energized Familiar for Resonance#205028 + 2x Aether Attunement#1243307 (easy for Sunfury).
- Apex **Prismatic Bolt**: Barrage has 1% (2% at rank 3) per Salvo consumed to replace your next Arcane Blast with Prismatic Bolt (big AoE-ish hit, +4 Charges, holdable 1 min); rank 2: 50/100% Clearcasting, Missiles +15/30%; rank 3: Barrage +15%.
  So: Barrage at 12+ Salvo when you can rebuild Charges quickly (Orb/Clearcasting) — more Bolt procs than slow-building to max.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Void-Touched Orbs, Void-Tainted Shell (group) / Self-Mending (solo), Lingering, Critical Power, Echoes.

## Opener / cooldowns
Long (≥15 s) potions/trinkets first → **precast Arcane Surge** → Time Warp#80353 (group call) → short (≤15 s) potions/trinkets/racials → Arcane Missiles → Arcane Barrage → **Touch of the Magi while the Barrage (or a Prismatic Bolt) is mid-air**
(or with ≤13 s of Surge left) → 45 s later Touch again. Touch is exactly half Surge's cooldown — don't delay it or Surge slips.
- Arcane Surge: drains mana for a hit (5 targets), then +35% spell damage and huge mana regen for 15 s. Use on cooldown; delay only for Time Warp, a vulnerability, or adds. Window needs ~23 s (Sunfury) / 17 s (Spellslinger).
- Touch of the Magi (45 s): stores 20% of your damage for 12 s then explodes; +4 Charges. Use on a target that lives the whole 12 s.
- Raid pull resets Arcane Charges — cast Orb/Blast after combat starts. Arcane Intellect#1459, Arcane Familiar check, Prismatic Barrier#235450 before pulls; Mirror Image for threat.
- Defensives: Prismatic Barrier (cast it often, 0.5 s), Alter Time#342245 (also mobility), Refractive Images. Shimmer#212653 + stutter-step; keep SpellQueueWindow at 400.
