# Mistweaver Monk (healer)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 tuning (Wowhead): started −15% all healing (dominated S1); then +8% (Aug 18), +5% & 4-piece proc 25% (Sep 1), +5% / Vivify +15% /
Sheilun's Gift +15% (Sep 22), Invigorating Mists cleave +15% / Enveloping Mist +15% (Oct 6). Start of S2: new Vital Expenditure#1299572 (Soothing Mist +300%
healing, +200% cost; choice with Dancing Mists#388701); Mana Tea per stack +20%; Mastery +125%; SCK −15%; Way of the Crane 280% transfer; Jadefire Teachings 320%.
Midnight removed Expel Harm, Spear Hand Strike, Chi Burst, etc. Soothing Mist follows your Vivify/Enveloping target baseline. Mana Tea no longer reduces costs after
(only returns mana). Thunder Focus Tea lost its Vivify/Expel Harm effects.

## The spec in one line
Renewing Mist is the most important spell — cast it whenever available, never sit at max charges (3). Keep Rising Sun Kick / Rushing Wind Kick rolling,
spend procs on damage (not downtime), and never overcap Mana Tea (20 stacks).

  Strength of the Black Ox#443110 procs (Enveloping Mist shields), Celestial Conduit#443028 (90 s channel heal/damage → Unity Within#443589 summons all Celestials),
  Flowing Wisdom#1262672 Haste. Defensive node: Jade Sanctuary#443059 (better in progression) or Niuzao's Protection#442747.
- **Raid build: Yu'lon** (Invoke Yu'lon, the Jade Serpent#322118), spreading Renewing Mist#115151 across the raid; Rushing Wind Kick#1269159 extends HoTs (Rising Mist#274909);
  Misty Coalescence#1268817 + Way of the Serpent#1243155 + Mistline#1280297 scale Renewing Mists. Chi-Ji raid build: "could not recommend this less" (Wowhead).
  Energizing Brew#422031 default over Lifecycles#197915; Refreshment#467270 + Chrysalis#202424 always (mana from Life Cocoons). Zen Pulse#446326 (+Deep Clarity#446345)
  alternative: hold Vivifies after TFT for damage. Gift of the Celestials#388212 is bugged with Yu'lon — don't take it in raid.
- **Mythic+: Chi-Ji "Apexless"** build (both guides): skip Spiritfont, damage-to-healing via Jadefire Teachings#467293 / Way of the Crane#388779 Spinning Crane Kick.

- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Burning Haste, Overload.

## Raid priority (Wowhead, Conduit + Yu'lon; Icy Veins nearly identical)
1. Vivify#116670 to save someone about to die (Life Cocoon in Icy Veins' M+ list).
2. Rushing Wind Kick#1269159.
3. Renewing Mist if at 3 charges. 4. Mana Tea#115869 if at 20 stacks.
5. Spiritfont at 2 stacks → spend one (Icy Veins: Enveloping Mist at 2 Spiritfont; Vivify at 2 Zen Pulse stacks).
6. **Thunder Focus Tea#116680 on Renewing Mist** (+10 s duration; Haste from Secret Infusion#388497). With Conduit, use it or lose it (Heart speeds its own cooldown).
7. Remaining Renewing Mist charges.
8. Vivify with high Renewing Mist coverage and an injured raid (Invigorating Mists#274586 heals everyone with Renewing Mist).
9. Spiritfont procs when the raid is injured.
10. Enveloping Mist#124682 with a Strength of the Black Ox proc — on someone taking (or about to take) damage.
11. Mana Tea if any stacks. 12. Blackout Kick#100784 (Tiger Palm#100780 if on cooldown) as filler — only if nothing else matters.
- Keep Mystic Touch#8647 on enemies (any damage applies it). Pair Heart of the Jade Serpent with cooldowns, especially Yu'lon (Haste).
- Unity Within manually only if the raid is badly hurt during the Conduit channel and RWK just went on cooldown.

## Yu'lon window (both guides)
Before: Secret Infusion Haste up, spend Renewing Mist charges. Damage starts: Invoke Yu'lon → several Enveloping Mists on different players (each spawns a Renewing Mist via
Rapid Diffusion#388847; Celestial Harmony#343655 = Chi Cocoons on 5 + 30% faster Enveloping) → after 3-4 Enveloping, RSK/RWK each time it's up (Rising Mist extends) →
Vivify to cash in the many Renewing Mists → keep weaving Enveloping every couple of GCDs. Conduit: first Tiger Palm/Vivify after invoking gives White Tiger → Black Ox.
Jade Bond#388031 (2 min, stronger: cocoons leave Enveloping Mists, Soothing Breath +500%) vs Gift of the Celestials (1 min, half duration).

## Chi-Ji (M+ / alternative)
Melee heals via Gust of Mists#343819 (2 allies x3 per kick; Blackout Kick scales with Teachings of the Monastery stacks up to 30 events at 4). Invoking gives 4 stacks —
open with Blackout Kick. Instant, cheaper Enveloping Mists. Burstier damage → build more stacks first.

## Cooldowns
- Thunder Focus Tea (30 s, off GCD): Renewing Mist (+10 s) normally; Enveloping Mist (instant full heal) for single target; RSK (−9 s) for Versatility in a defensive moment.
- Life Cocoon#116849: big absorb (+50% HoT healing taken). Best on low-health targets; great for solo soaks. Don't be afraid to use it.
- Revival/Restoral: instant raid heal (+ dispel up to 3 Poison/Disease/Magic each with Revival; heals before it dispels). Uplifted Spirits#388551: 2:30 cd, +15%.
- Sheilun's Gift#399491 (replaces Vivify when talented): clouds every 8 s (4 s with Veil of Pride#400053; Tranquil Tea#1270621 = 1 cloud per Mana Tea stack); +500% on the
  primary target → always cast on the most injured.
- Soothing Mist#115175: 1 s GCD channel; makes Vivify/Enveloping/Cocoon instant. Only worth channeling before 3+ Enveloping Mists or an emergency chain on one target;
  never before a single Vivify. Statue#115313 adds its own channel (1.3x).

## Renewing Mist rules (Wowhead)
It jumps when it would overheal (to an injured ally within 20 yd without one). Re-casting on a target that has one makes the old one jump — or just refresh if no one is eligible.
Best practice: cast it on **full-health players who don't have one** (next tick jumps safely; Dancing Mists#388701 can split).

## Teachings of the Monastery (Wowhead)
Each Blackout Kick hit has 20% to reset RSK. Way of the Crane: one Tiger Palm (2 stacks) then Blackout Kick. Way of the Serpent: Blackout Kick at 0 stacks.
If RSK has ≤3 s left, wait and press RSK first, then Blackout Kick.

## Pre-pull
100% mana (drink Mana Tea), repaired, Thunder Focus Tea ready (doesn't reset on wipe), consumables, right talents, Windwalking only if no other Monk.
