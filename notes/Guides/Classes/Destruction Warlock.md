# Destruction Warlock (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Midnight stripped many debuffs: removed Eradication, Decimation, Power Overwhelming, Burn to Ashes, Ritual of Ruin, Rolling Havoc, Pyrogenics, etc. New: Apex Embers of Nihilam#1265770, Chaotic Inferno#1244788, Destructive Rapidity#1244928,
Improved Havoc#1244460, Lake of Fire#1244877. **Shadowburn#17877 is now an execute (under 20%), no cooldown, 1 shard** (Conflagration of Chaos#387108 lets it be cast at any health and always crit); Roaring Blaze#205184 spreads Immolate to 3 on Conflagrate;
Dimensional Rift#387976 passive (choice with Soul Fire#6353); Avatar of Destruction#1245089 Overfiend.

## The spec in one line
Generate Soul Shard fragments (10 per shard; start fights at 3 shards) with Incinerate#29722 (2), Conflagrate#17962 (5, 2 charges, Backdraft#196406 speeds the next Chaos Bolt), Immolate#348 ticks, Soul Fire (10), Infernal Bolt (2 shards);
spend on **Chaos Bolt#116858** (2 shards; always crits, scales with crit) single target / **Rain of Fire#5740** (3 shards) AoE / Shadowburn in execute — never overcap 5. Keep Immolate (or Wither#445468) up (pandemic 5.4 s, 6.3 with Scalding Flames).
**Summon Infernal#1122** (2 min) and **Havoc#80240** (copies single-target spells at 60% onto a second target and doubles shard generation; put it on the less important target) are the cooldowns.

## Hero trees
- **Hellcaller**: Wither replaces Immolate (Blackened Soul#440043), **Malevolence#430014/442726** 1-min window (spenders give double Wither stacks; have Withers out first; dump shards before it ends). Best single target, 2-target and spread cleave.
- **Diabolist**: Diabolic Ritual#428514 demons from spending (Overlord → Mother of Chaos → Pit Lord, fixed order; resets on raid pull, not in dungeons). Trigger each with **Chaos Bolt** (Touch of Rancora#429893).
  Overlord charges and cleaves (aim at packs; Cloven Souls 15 s amp); Mother of Chaos makes the next Incinerate an Infernal Bolt#434506 (+2 shards → be at ≤2 shards first); Pit Lord turns the next Chaos Bolt into **Ruination#434635** (aim at the biggest pack). Best stacked cleave/AoE.
- Both guides: Hellcaller for raid single target and cleave (Wowhead: Best for ST, Raid Cleave, Raid Council, Delves); Diabolist for M+/AoE.
- Mayhem#387506 (passive 6 s Havocs) vs Havoc — Mayhem is better than a Havoc you forget to use.
- Apex **Embers of Nihilam**: background procs from Incinerate / spenders (crit+haste buffs); no rotation change.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending / Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power, Residual Energy ≈ Overload.

## Single target
- Wowhead Hellcaller: maintain Wither → Shadowburn when available (and near cap) → Chaos Bolt to avoid capping → Soul Fire under 4 shards → Conflagrate to stay under 2 charges or on the move → Conflagrate for shards/Backdraft (spend Backdraft on Chaos Bolt) → Incinerate.
- Wowhead Diabolist: maintain Immolate → Chaos Bolt above 4 shards with Infernal Bolt ready → Incinerate.
- Icy Veins: maintain Immolate/Wither → Summon Infernal → Malevolence → Shadowburn whenever available → Soul Fire under 4 → Chaos Bolt near cap → Cataclysm#152108 (not a gain in pure ST) → Infernal Bolt under 4 → Channel Demonfire#196447 → Conflagrate at 2 charges (to speed a long cast) → Conflagrate → Incinerate.
- Max quick Chaos Bolts: Chaos Bolt → Conflagrate → Chaos Bolt → Conflagrate → Chaos Bolt.

## AoE / cleave
- Spender choice (Wowhead): Rain of Fire at **3+ targets** without Havoc, at **5+ with Havoc**; Chaos Bolt at 1-2 targets, or up to 5 with Havoc up.
- Icy Veins: Immolate on the main target and any target living 10+ s (spread with Conflagrate/Roaring Blaze); Wither on as many as possible; Cataclysm on cooldown (delay a few seconds for more targets) → Channel Demonfire; Malevolence with max Withers;
  Chaos Bolt/Shadowburn to trigger Diabolic Rituals; Rain of Fire at 4+ (3+); Havoc whenever a second target exists.
- Shadowburn in multi-target (Icy Veins): Diabolist up to 7 targets with Conflagration of Chaos (4 without; never with Destructive Rapidity but no Conflagration), +1 with Fiendish Cruelty; Hellcaller up to 4 (6 with Fiendish Cruelty).
- Havoc tip: hold ~4 shards and Conflagrate charges if an add is coming in ~10 s; Havoc it on spawn, then Chaos Bolts. Havoc range 60 yd, no line of sight needed.

## Openers
Wowhead Hellcaller: precast Soul Fire → Summon Infernal + potion/trinkets/racials → Malevolence → Conflagrate → spend everything before Malevolence ends. Diabolist: precast Soul Fire → generate/spend, Backdraft Chaos Bolts.
Icy Veins: precast Soul Fire / Incinerate / Immolate / Cataclysm → Wither → Conflagrate → (Cataclysm) → Summon Infernal + trinkets/racials → Malevolence → Chaos Bolt → Conflagrate.

## Cooldowns
- Summon Infernal: stun + damage + passive shards; on cooldown (Crashing Chaos#417234 empowers spenders → pair trinkets/racials); can delay if no use is lost.
- Cataclysm: a way to apply Immolate/Wither to many targets, not burst.

## Tier set (Season 2)
2-piece Incinerate damage and doubled Embers chance; 4-piece damage amp on targets with Embers. Passive.

## Stats
- Wowhead: Int > Haste > Mastery ≥ Crit > Vers.
- Icy Veins: item level > **Haste to 22%** > Crit > Mastery > Haste > Vers. Crit boosts Chaos Bolt 1:1 and Immolate shard crits. Mastery: Chaotic Energies (not pets).

## Disagreements
- Stats after Haste (Wowhead Mastery ≥ Crit; Icy Veins Crit > Mastery).
