# Arms Warrior (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Midnight removed Skullsplitter, Bitter Immunity, Thunderous Roar, Warbreaker (now part of Colossus Smash). 12.1: Mortal Strike no longer reduces Demolish cooldown; Slayer gets many more Sudden Death procs.
Rage comes mostly from auto-attacks (Skyfury#462854 helps) — downtime is the biggest loss.

## The spec in one line
Keep **Colossus Smash#167105** (main burst, Anger Management keeps it short) and **Avatar#107574** on cooldown together, put **Bladestorm#227847** (Slayer) / **Demolish#436358** (Colossus) inside Colossus Smash, use Mortal Strike#12294 on cooldown,
Execute#163201 with Sudden Death#280776 (stacks 2 — don't overcap), Overpower#7384, keep Rend#772 up (Cleave#845 applies it), Heroic Strike#1269383 (Apex proc) over Slam#1464 filler. Below 35%: execute priority. Fill every GCD in Colossus Smash.

## Hero trees
- **Slayer in all content** (both guides): more Sudden Death procs, stronger Execute and Bladestorm (Unhinged auto Mortal Strikes; consuming Sudden Death cuts Bladestorm cooldown); Bladestorm can interrupt/stun. Wowhead Colossus is Best only for Delves.
- Colossus: Demolish (3-hit channel; only the third hit is AoE — a long frontal cone with little backward reach; immune to most stuns/knockbacks; off-GCD Charge/Leap/Die by the Sword usable during it), Colossal Might#429634 stacks from Mortal Strike,
  Ravager#228920 buffs Cleave (Crushing Combo#1261056); weaker single target.
- Raid: single-target Slayer build (Sweeping Strikes#260708 + Bladestorm cover small adds); 2 targets often: Just Warming Up → Broad Strokes#1261049, Cleave → Powerful Momentum#1261050. Wrecking Throw#384110 on shielded targets (+500% vs absorbs).
  Stance Mastery#1280961 is a big defense if you actually use Defensive Stance#386208.
- Apex **Master of Warfare**: procs replace Slam with Heroic Strike (higher priority, armor-pen stacks); final point: Heroic Strike boosts Colossus Smash. Passive to track.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Masterful Cunning, Echoes.

## Single target — Slayer (both guides)
(Cleave/Rend to apply or refresh Rend at <10 s if Colossus Smash is ready) → Avatar on cooldown → Colossus Smash on cooldown → Execute at 2 Sudden Death → Execute right before Bladestorm if <3 Imminent Demise#444769 stacks →
**Bladestorm during Colossus Smash** (early in it) → Heroic Strike → Mortal Strike → Execute with Sudden Death → Overpower (Icy Veins: at 2 Opportunist stacks first) → Rend/Cleave refresh at <5 s → Slam.
Execute phase (<35%): Avatar → Colossus Smash → Heroic Strike → Bladestorm in Smash → Mortal Strike at 2 Executioner's Precision#386634 (Icy Veins: or Rend <3 s / Fierce Followthrough) → Execute with Sudden Death or >40 Rage → Overpower → Execute.
Storm Bolt#107570 during Bladestorm is a tiny gain. Imminent Demise glow is not a signal to Bladestorm.
## Single target — Colossus (Wowhead)
Mortal Strike under 10 Colossal Might → Rend refresh (<10 s, Smash ready) → Ravager just before Smash → Avatar → Colossus Smash → **Demolish in Colossus Smash** → Heroic Strike → Execute at 2 Sudden Death → Mortal Strike → Execute (Sudden Death) → Overpower → Rend <5 s → Slam.
Execute phase: Execute with Sudden Death or >75 Rage; plain Execute only with Deep Wounds#1261060.

## AoE
Slayer: Sweeping Strikes on cooldown → Cleave early (Rend) → Avatar → Colossus Smash → Cleave at 3 Collateral Damage#334779 → Bladestorm → Execute (Sudden Death; Reap the Storm#444775 now procs from it) → Cleave → Overpower at 2 charges → Execute → Overpower → Mortal Strike → Slam.
Colossus: Sweeping Strikes → Ravager → Avatar → Colossus Smash → Cleave (spammed with Crushing Combo; Fervor of Battle auto-casts Heroic Strike/Slam at 3+) → Mortal Strike/Demolish (at 10 Colossal Might, or late in Smash) → Overpower → Execute (Sudden Death) → Slam.
No separate execute phase in AoE.

## Openers
Always open with Charge#100 (Rage). ST (Icy Veins): Charge → Mortal Strike → Rend/Cleave → (Ravager) → Avatar → Colossus Smash → Bladestorm/Demolish → Heroic Strike → Mortal Strike → Overpower → Execute (Sudden Death) → priority.
Stat trinkets on pull; save combat potion for execute unless two potions fit. Pre-combat: Battle Shout#6673.

## Cooldowns and tips
- Don't delay cooldowns more than ~10-15% of their length unless it gains extra targets or it's the last use.
- Cycle Charging: step out and Charge between swings outside Colossus Smash for extra Rage; Heroic Leap#6544 out of mechanics at the last moment, then Charge back.
- Defensives: Die by the Sword, Defensive Stance, Ignore Pain, Spell Reflection, Impending Victory; Rallying Cry (raid cooldown, on GCD).

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: Mortal Strike and Execute +10%; Slam also hits enemies within 8 yd.
- 4-piece: Overpower +15%; Mortal Strike and Overpower stack +20% on the next Slam (max 5). Passive — no need to track.
