# Devourer Demon Hunter (25-yard caster DPS, new in Midnight)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). **Real 12.1.5 changes** (both guides): Collapsing Star#1221167 no longer gets a 5 s cooldown if its cast is cancelled, doubles its range while casting,
slows Fury drain for 1.5x its cast time; Voidpurge#1244220 −2.5 s Void Ray; Soulforged Blades 18%; many talents moved. Void-Scarred reworked: Demonic Intensity#452415 makes Collapsing Star reset The Hunt;
Violent Transformation#452409 now resets Soul Immolation#1241937 (not The Hunt); Monster Rising#452414 Int 10%, Collapsing Star +20%. → Void-Scarred now plays like Annihilator (long Meta, many Stars).
Brings Chaos Brand.

## The spec in one line
Build Fury and Soul Fragments with Consume#473662 / Reap#1226019, spend Fury on Void Ray#473728, and at 50 Souls (35 with Soul Glutton#1247534) enter **Void Metamorphosis#1217607** — no fixed duration:
it lasts until Fury drains out, so extend it and cast as many **Collapsing Stars** (every 30 Souls inside) as possible.

## Hero trees
- **Annihilator — recommended for all content except delves** (both guides). Voidfall#1256301: Consume/Devour and Void Ray build stacks; at 3 your next Reap calls Void Meteors (+2 Souls each).
  Entering Meta: Reap reset + 3 Voidfall stacks. Aim **4-5 Collapsing Stars per Meta single target, 5+ AoE**. Defense: Phase Shift#1256245 (2-8% DR); mobility: State of Matter#1253402 (3rd Shift charge) over Path to Oblivion.
- Void-Scarred: highest theoretical damage but easy to disrupt (long uncontrolled melee leaps); uses the melee package (Voidblade#1245412 → Hungering Slash#1239519 → Vengeful Retreat Voidstep; The Hunt#1246167).
  In Meta they become Pierce the Veil#1245483 / Reaper's Toll#1245470 / Predator's Wake#1259431 and each first cast triggers Voidsurge#1246160. Aim 3-4 Stars ST, 4+ AoE; Voidblade and The Hunt before each Star.
  Wowhead: delves "Best".
- Raid builds (Wowhead): Annihilator Raid Cleave (Voidrage#1242956) for most fights; pure single target → drop Demonic Instinct#1247538 for Second Helping#1239537 (2 Reap charges).
- Apex **Midnight** (4 points): Collapsing Star always crits; +3% Cosmic and +50% of crit chance as Star crit damage per point; final point: entering Meta spawns 5 Souls and gives one Collapsing Star immediately.
- Emptiness taken in all builds (Haste per Soul consumed in Meta).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Masterful Cunning, Overload.

## Outside Void Metamorphosis (single target, both guides — Annihilator subset in Wowhead)
Consume if it triggers Soulburst#1297432 (tier) → **Reap at 4+ Souls** (tier) → Void Metamorphosis when available (potion + trinkets) → Void Ray (at 100+ Fury, Icy Veins) → Soul Immolation (if not active) → Consume.
Void-Scarred adds: Voidblade (save for right before Meta if Meta is ≤10 Souls away) → Vengeful Retreat after Voidblade + The Hunt right before Meta → Hungering Slash → Vengeful Retreat with Voidstep.
## Inside Void Metamorphosis
Devour#1217610 with Soulburst → **Void Ray on cooldown** (pauses Fury drain; Annihilator: unless it overcaps Souls) → (Void-Scarred melee: Reaper's Toll, Voidstep retreat, Pierce the Veil, Predator's Wake) →
**Collapsing Star** at 30 Souls → Cull#1245453 at 4+ Souls (or at 2 charges) → (Annihilator: Voidblade for Devourer's Bite#1241532 before a Star/Cull) → Devour → Soul Immolation as you drop out.

## AoE (2+ targets)
Outside: Consume with Soulburst → **Eradicate#1226033 at 10 Souls** (or Moment of Craving#1238488 expiring; Annihilator: 4+ if it procs Voidfall) → Void Ray at 100 Fury → (Voidblade/Hungering Slash) →
Reap/Eradicate at 3 Voidfall → Reap at 4+ → Soul Immolation → Consume.
Inside: Collapsing Star if you'd go past 40 Souls → Cull/Eradicate at 3 Voidfall → Eradicate → Void Ray → (melee) → Collapsing Star → Devour → Soul Immolation on exit.
- Void-Scarred in 2+ targets basically never Reaps (it robs Eradicate). Annihilator 2+: Reap only for Voidfall or to reach a Star.
- **Always smuggle an Eradicate into Meta in AoE** (Void Ray right before Meta, carry the proc in — +30%). Annihilator: don't consume an available Voidfall right before Meta.

## Resources and tricks
- Fury cap 120 (Annihilator) / 140 (Void-Scarred) outside Meta — don't overcap (fewer Void Rays). Souls give 4 Fury each; max 10 on the ground (extra auto-collect); collected Souls past 50 (35) outside / 40 inside are wasted.
- **Smuggling**: leave Souls on the ground when entering/leaving Meta to get the next Star/Meta faster; carry Hungering Slash (Voidblade right before Meta) and Eradicate in.
- **Huggling** (Void-Scarred single target): Voidblade → Vengeful Retreat → The Hunt → press Meta during the charge (~14-15% Haste, 15+ yd) so it lands as Predator's Wake.
- Wiggling: strafe while casting Consume to grab Souls only when a few short of Void Ray / a Star / a last Star late in Meta.
- Shipping (Void-Scarred AoE): carry a Soulburst Devour or an Eradicate out of Meta to start the next one faster.
- Rolling Torment: Souls left when Meta ends give Fury and damage.
- Vengeful Retreat use is a tiny gain (0.3% ST, 1% AoE) — skip it if unsafe (Wowhead).
- Movement: Consume/Devour castable while moving, Reap instant, Shift#1234796 (dash to cursor, not a teleport), Glide + Wings of Wrath.
