(mostly rank 1), Dragonrage#375087 every 2 min (extend it with empowers), Deep Breath on cooldown as Scalecommander, and always be casting (Hover#358267).

## Cooldowns and mechanics
- Dragonrage (2 min): guaranteed Essence Burst from fillers, full Mastery: Giantkiller#362980 (Tyranny#376888), fires 3 Pyres; extended by Fire Breath / Eternity Surge (Animosity#375797) to ~32-34 s.
- Disintegrate chaining: re-cast after the 4th tick (of 5 with Azure Celerity) without loss; don't chain Mass Disintegrate into a normal one (loses the buff portion).
- Bombardment cleaving (Scalecommander): each Mass Disintegrate's primary target gets its own Bombardments; Essence spells extend all of them.
- Fury of the Aspects#390386 = Bloodlust (group decision). Pre-pull: Blessing of the Bronze#364342; heal yourself to fill Scarlet Adaptation#372469. Very high threat, no threat drop.
