# Affliction Warlock (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). Oct 27 hotfix: Unstable Affliction damage +20%. No priority change (audit, Oct 28).
12.1 (Wowhead): few changes; Nocturnal Yield and Patient Zero removed; new Hedonic Gorging#1311969, Impetuous Wrath#1312998; **Shard Instability#1260269 can now give a free Seed of Corruption#27243** as well as a free Unstable Affliction#1259790.
Weak single target in stacked-AoE builds (pathing can't reach both).

## The spec in one line
Keep **Agony#980** (sole Soul Shard source — ramps, never let it drop) and Corruption#172 / Wither#445465 up (pandemic: 30% of duration), spend Soul Shards (max 5 — don't overcap) on Unstable Affliction (Seed of Corruption at 3+ stacked),
Haunt#48181 on cooldown, **Summon Darkglare#205180 (2 min, +20% to active DoTs) before chaining Unstable Afflictions**, Dark Harvest#1257052 (~40 s with Cull the Weak#1259886), Malefic Grasp#1261149 filler in Darkglare, Drain Soul / Shadow Bolt filler.
Spend Nightfall#108558 and Shard Instability procs. Very mobile (refresh DoTs while moving), great defenses.

## Hero trees
- **Soul Harvester**: Dark Harvest gives 3 shards (Shadow of Death#449638) → go down to ≤2 shards before Dark Harvest; spend shards fast; Sataiel's Volition Nightfall procs; Gorebound Fortitude, Friends in Dark Places#449703, Gorefiend's Resolve.
- **Hellcaller**: Wither replaces Corruption (Blackened Soul), **Malevolence#442726** 1-min cooldown (haste, +6 stacks on each Wither) — pair every other one with Darkglare (Through the Felvine#1266799); Curse of the Satyr.
- Which is better:
  - Icy Veins: **Soul Harvester dominant** (~2% single target, more in cleave/burst; better in any dungeon); Hellcaller only listed for M+.
  - Wowhead: overview says Soul Harvester favored; its talent page marks **Hellcaller Best for Raid and Raid Cleave** ("recently pulled ahead due to tuning") and Soul Harvester Best for M+ and Delves.
- Apex **Shadow of Nathreza**: Haunt hits harder, cleaves, bigger damage amp — still just cast Haunt on cooldown.
- Pet: Sayaad (Succubus/Incubus) best single target; Imp for dispel/distant swaps; Felhunter for interrupt/purge.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending / Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power (Masterful Cunning past 35% crit), Residual Energy ≈ Overload.

## Single target
- Wowhead Hellcaller: Haunt → Agony → Wither → Dark Harvest → Darkglare → Malevolence → as many Unstable Afflictions as possible → Malefic Grasp.
- Wowhead Soul Harvester: Haunt → Agony → Corruption → Darkglare → Unstable Affliction → Dark Harvest → Unstable Affliction → Malefic Grasp.
- Icy Veins: maintain Agony + Corruption/Wither → Unstable Affliction at max shards → Haunt → Malevolence → Dark Harvest with a DoT up (≤2 shards for Soul Harvester) → Shard Instability free UA inside its window → UA to avoid capping → Drain Soul#198590 / Shadow Bolt#232670 filler.
- Pandemic windows: Agony 5.4 s (4.5 s with Creeping Death), Corruption/Wither 4.2 s (3.5 s). Pool shards only before Darkglare/Malevolence, adds, or a burn phase.

## AoE
Seed of Corruption (applies Corruption/Wither to stacked 3+) → Haunt on the priority target → Agony on a handful (Icy Veins: up to 10, 14 for Hellcaller) → Darkglare → Malevolence → Dark Harvest (DoTs on every target) → Seed as much as possible
(at 5 shards with 3/6+ close) → Nightfall procs woven in → free UA on dying targets / priority target → filler. 2 targets: Unstable Affliction on the second during Darkglare.

## Openers
Wowhead Hellcaller: precast Haunt → Agony on pull → Wither → Dark Harvest → Darkglare → Malevolence → Malefic Grasp. Soul Harvester: precast Haunt → Agony → Corruption → Darkglare → 2-3 UA → Dark Harvest → Malefic Grasp.
Icy Veins: precast Haunt + Shadow Bolt → Agony → Corruption/Wither → (Malevolence) → UA to 2 shards → Dark Harvest → Darkglare + trinkets/racials → dump shards on UA → Dark Harvest → UA → Nightfall Drain Soul/Shadow Bolt → Malefic Grasp until the last UA expires.
Pre-pull: pet, Soulwell (Healthstones), Demonic Circle / Gateway placement.

## Cooldowns
- Darkglare: have Agony at full pandemic-extended duration before it so no GCD goes to refreshes; spend every UA before 8 s left; Nether Plating needed for 5-target cleave.
- Malefic Grasp window: maximize GCDs of Malefic Grasp with UAs ticking — don't move, don't refresh.
- Dark Harvest: before Darkglare; on cooldown otherwise.
- Demonic Gateway#111771 is a raid movement cooldown; curses via Foul Mouth (Tongues for casters, Weakness for melee).

## Tier set (Season 2)
2-piece Corruption and Agony damage; 4-piece lets UA-tied talents trigger from Seed of Corruption in AoE. Passive.

## Stats
Both: Intellect (item level) > Haste > Crit > Mastery > Vers (close; balance them). Haste = more DoT ticks = more shards. Mastery: Potent Afflictions (not pets, not shards).

## Disagreements
- **Hero tree for raid**: Icy Veins says Soul Harvester; Wowhead's builds page says Hellcaller (its own overview says Soul Harvester). Check top logs before grading hero choice.
- Agony target cap advice differs by build (10 vs 14).

## What a log can check
Agony and Corruption/Wither uptime (Agony drops on a priority target are the worst mistake); Soul Shards capped; Darkglare / Haunt / Dark Harvest / Malevolence casts vs possible; Unstable Afflictions spent inside Darkglare (and before its last 8 s);
Dark Harvest cast above 2 shards (Soul Harvester); Shard Instability / Nightfall procs expired; Seed of Corruption used at 3+ stacked; casting uptime.
