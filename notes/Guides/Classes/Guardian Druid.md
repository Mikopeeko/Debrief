# Guardian Druid (tank)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): Lunation#429539 now a flat −20 s on Lunar Beam#204066 (kills S1 Moonfire spam); Innervate gives 20% mana over 8 s; Gory Fur#200854 now: Ironfur can make the next
Maul/Raze/Ravage free and vice versa. Apex reworked (Wild Guardian, below). Midnight removed Rage of the Sleeper, Pulverize, Thorns of Iron, Renewal, Nature's Vigil, Earthwarden.
New: Red Moon#1252871, Harnessed Rage#1253035, Waking Nightmare#1254168, Sundering Roar#1253799, Dream Guide#1278886, Natural Resilience#1278789.

## The spec in one line
Thrash#77758 and Mangle#33917 on cooldown, keep Moonfire/Red Moon up, spend Rage on Raze/Maul **above 80 Rage** for damage (Harnessed Rage) or Ironfur#192081 for defense — but
**unneeded Ironfur is now a big DPS loss**. Barkskin often, Survival Instincts for big hits.

## Hero trees
- **Elune's Chosen**: Wowhead "Best" in raid, M+ and delves (tankier, simpler, better AoE). Icy Veins: recommended for AoE/M+; raid "very close".
  Lunar Beam is a core offensive and defensive button (big Mastery).
- **Druid of the Claw**: Ravage#441583 (Maul replacement, big hit + DoT), bursty, slightly higher single-target ceiling. Icy Veins: "generally recommend if you understand Guardian well",
  stronger consistent mitigation. Raid build plays around Killing Blow#1252994 + Harnessed Rage + Red Moon; Flashing Claws#393427 if you don't need Ursoc's Guidance#393414.
- M+: Elune's Chosen + Twin Moonfire#372567, Ursoc's Fury#377210 (much tankier), Raze#400254 as spender, Elune's Favored#370586 defensive flex.
- Apex **Wild Guardian#1269614**: Thrash/Mangle have 10% to summon an 8 s spirit (8 Rage per hit); adds Mangle to the 12 s DoT (keep it up with Maul or Mangle every 12 s); spirit resets Thrash and
  Mangle and they deal +30%. Don't spend Rage just before Thrash/Mangle come back (spending can reset them).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Versatile Warrior, Overload.

## Rotation (both guides)
Wowhead single target: Moonfire#8921 on the primary target → 3-5 Thrash stacks → Red Moon on cooldown → Mangle on cooldown → Thrash on cooldown → Rage on Maul#6807 (offense) or Ironfur (defense) →
Frenzied Regeneration#22842 when low → Moonfire with Galactic Guardian#203964 procs → Barkskin / Incarnation as often as possible → Swipe#213771 filler.
Icy Veins single target: Thrash → Mangle → Lunar Beam (align with Red Moon if close) → Red Moon → Heart of the Wild#1261867 in Cat Form → Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc#102558 →
Raze (offense) / Ironfur (defense) → Swipe. Keep Red Moon up by Mangling often; Raze/Ravage above 80 Rage (Harnessed Rage → Gore resets Mangle).
AoE: same, Twin Moonfire instead of Red Moon; Heart of the Wild in Moonkin Form on AoE pulls (Starfall), Cat Form on bosses (Feral Frenzy).
Opener: Moonfire + Thrash up first, then cooldowns. Pre-pull: Mark of the Wild, Bear Form, consumables.
- Catweaving as Druid of the Claw is a **DPS loss** now (Icy Veins) — only shift to Cat every 2 min for Heart of the Wild (safe single target). Ripweaving (Wildpower Surge after 6 Mangles) is optional/advanced.

## Ironfur (Icy Veins)
+112% of Agility as armor for 7 s; stacks overlap (durations don't). Armor caps at 85% (~7-8 stacks, 3-4 with an Augmentation Evoker). Useless vs bleeds/magic — use cooldowns or
Frenzied Regeneration there. Proactive: no point pressing it if no new damage is coming. Keep ~1-2 stacks where needed (Wowhead).

## Rage (Icy Veins)
Bear Form +25, auto-attack 4, being hit 3 (1/s), Mangle 10 (+4 Gore, +5 Soul of the Forest), Thrash 5, Galactic Guardian Moonfire 8, Bristling Fur#155835 1 per % health lost, Blood Frenzy#203962 2 per Thrash tick,
Wild Guardian spirit 8 per hit. Ursoc's Guidance turns Rage spent into Incarnation cooldown.

## Cooldowns
- Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc (2-3 min): more health, faster Thrash/Mangle, Mangle hits 3 → offensive on cooldown mostly; can hold for known danger. Spend Rage so you don't cap.
- Lunar Beam: use Maul/Raze a lot inside it; big health boost → strong defensive too.
- Barkskin#22812 (40-60 s): on cooldown to smooth damage (Brambles damage).
- Survival Instincts (−50%, 2 charges in Midnight): plan for the biggest hits. Don't overlap defensives unless you'd die otherwise.
- Heart of the Wild: Cat = Feral Frenzy#274837, caster = Wild Growth#48438, Moonkin = Starfall#191037.

## Tier set (Season 2 — "Bark of the Enigmatic Dreamwatcher")

- 4-piece: Thrash calls up to 3 thorns (Nature damage); Incarnation lasts 5 s longer. Passive.

## Stats
- Wowhead (both survival and DPS): Agility > Haste > Versatility > Crit > Mastery.
- Icy Veins survival: ilvl > Haste > Vers > Mastery > Crit; damage: Haste > Vers > Crit > Mastery. All extremely close; 10+ ilvl almost always wins.
- Mastery: Nature's Guardian#155783 = more max health and healing received.

## Disagreements
- Hero tree: Wowhead marks Elune's Chosen "Best" everywhere; Icy Veins says raid is even and leans Druid of the Claw for experienced players, Elune's Chosen for M+.
- Rotation order differs slightly (Wowhead DoTs first; Icy Veins Thrash/Mangle first). Both: keep Thrash, Mangle, Moonfire/Red Moon rolling.
