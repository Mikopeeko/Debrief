# Discipline Priest (healer)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). 12.1.5 (patch notes): Master the Darkness 60 s -> 30 s. No Discipline change in the Oct 23/26/27 hotfixes (audit, Oct 28).
Season 2 changes (Wowhead): new talent Grim Deliverance (Shadow Mend +30% healing, +4 s Atonement, +0.5 s cast); Shadow Mend is now a passive upgrade of
Flash Heal (stronger, costs more); Apex Master the Darkness rank 3: Mind Blast upgrades your next Power Word: Shield to Void Shield; Penance's Void Shield chance
33% → 15%; Void Shield absorb −28%; Master the Darkness holds 2 charges.

## The spec in one line
Heal by doing damage (Atonement#81749). Always be casting: Shadow Word: Pain up, Mind Blast and Penance on cooldown, Void Shield whenever you have it,
Smite/Void Blast as filler. Evangelism and Ultimate Penitence are the big cooldowns.

## Hero trees
- **Voidweaver** — Wowhead: best for raid AND Mythic+. Icy Veins: Voidweaver for raid and delves, **Oracle for Mythic+** (better single-target healing).
  Voidweaver: Mind Blast#8092 opens Entropic Rift (grows with Penance, collapses for a burst); Smite#585 becomes **Void Blast#450405** in the rift (very strong);
  Voidwraith pet after the rift. The 2-piece (more Mind Blasts) is what makes Voidweaver great — may feel weak without it. Weakness: movement, single target.
- Oracle: 2 Penance#47540 charges (Guiding Light), Twinsight#440742 extra bolts, stronger shields (Preventive Measures, Waste No Time), longer Atonements.
- Apex **Master the Darkness** (4 ranks): Penance can turn the next Power Word: Shield#17 into **Void Shield#1253593** (splashes to 2 nearby allies);
  ranks 2-3 = more Atonement healing and shadow damage; rank 4 = Void Shield reflects part of its absorb as damage (heals via Atonement) and Mind Blast always procs it.
  Penance procs use a **4-card deck**: exactly 1 proc per 4 Penances (longest dry spell 6 casts). Use a proc before your next Penance; can delay it for a big damage event.
- Choice node: Ultimate Penitence#421453 ("Uppies", default) vs Power Word: Barrier#81782 (only if the raid can stack under it through heavy damage).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Masterful Cunning, Overload.

## Raid rotation — core loop (Wowhead, Voidweaver)
- Shadow Word: Pain#589 on enemies if under 20 s left.
- Mind Blast on cooldown (Void Shield charge + empowers next Penance via Dark Indulgence#372972).
- Penance offensively; never overcap charges.
- Void Blast as much as possible. Void Shield any time it's available.
- Power Word: Shield often if mana is good and Weal and Woe#390786 is at ~6+ stacks.
- Surge of Light#114255 / Flash Heal#2061 to apply cheap Atonements.
- Power Word: Radiance#194509 on cooldown for mini-ramps; **hold 2 charges for Evangelism / Uppies**.
- Shadow Word: Death#32379 in execute (under 20%) — Icy Veins: Void Blast beats it, so it's a movement button for Voidweaver.
- Smite in downtime.
- Expect 5-10 Atonements between cooldowns, up to ~20 for Evangelism/Uppies. Ramps now take ~8 s (Evangelism casts a Radiance and makes the next 2 instant).
- **Atonement scaling** (Icy Veins): beyond ~5 targets each extra Atonement adds very little; ramping to 20 is only for coverage. Extra Plea/Flash Heal just to add
  Atonements wastes mana — except one Flash Heal per ramp (Binding Heals = Atonement on target AND you, and Protective Light).

## Evangelism ramp (Icy Veins) — Power Word: Shield = Void Shield when you have a proc
Refresh SW:Pain → Power Word: Shield → Flash Heal → **Evangelism#472433** → Radiance x1-2 → SW:Death → Mind Blast → Penance → Void Shield → Penance → SW:Death →
Smite / Void Blast until Penance or Shield is back.
Radiance before or after Evangelism is nearly the same — save the instant charges for a movement-heavy event if needed.
Evangelism also triggers Archangel#197862 (healing/absorb boost) or Mindbender. 1:30 cooldown — don't sit on it.

## Ultimate Penitence ramp (Icy Veins) — used separately from Evangelism
Refresh SW:Pain → Power Word: Shield → Flash Heal → Radiance x2 → **Mind Blast (open the rift first, Voidweaver)** → Ultimate Penitence (queue Void Shield while
floating up — a free GCD; casting after the channel starts can interrupt it) → Penance → Void Blast x3 → Power Word: Shield → Penance → Void Blast...
Always cast Uppies on an **enemy** in raid (bolts go where your target is). 4 min cooldown; also a big personal shield.
Voidweaver: Mind Blast → Uppies → queued Void Shield (Void Infusion's Penance buff applies to Uppies).

## Mythic+ (both)
Oracle: spread SW:Pain with offensive Penance; Shadow Mend for spot healing; Evangelism to apply Atonements; SW:Death under 20%; Penance and Mind Blast on cooldown
(Void Shields); Smite; Power Word: Shield as needed (spamming it runs you out of mana). Pain Suppression liberally (Protector of the Frail = many charges); on yourself too.
Voidweaver M+: SW:Pain → Mind Blast → Penance → Void Shield → SW:Death → Void Blast → Shadow Mend if needed → Smite.

## Cooldowns and defensives
Pain Suppression#33206 (big single-target reduction: tank or a heavy DoT target; don't sit at max charges). Desperate Prayer#19236 (heal + max health),
Fade#586 + Translucent Image (small reduction, short cooldown — use often), Protective Light (Flash Heal anyone → you take less damage).
Power Infusion: **never on yourself** (Twins of the Sun Priestess gives you one when cast on an ally); line it up with a DPS's cooldowns.

## Atonement rules
Only the Priest's own damage counts (no trinkets/racials unless they buff your spells). AoE spells (Holy Nova#132157, Inescapable Torment#373427) only atone from one target.
Damage taken modifiers on the boss don't matter (99% reduction still heals full) — but **immunity = zero healing**. Only Chaos Brand boosts it. Versatility/crit don't double-dip.
Huge range. Surge of Light: skip it if the Flash Heal and its Atonement would mostly overheal.
Voidweaver: clipping Penance to get back to Void Blast is a small gain (let it finish if Harsh Discipline AND Power of the Dark Side are both up).
Oracle: self-cast Void Shield when you have no shield (Prophet's Will → bigger reflect).
