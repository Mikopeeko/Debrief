# Preservation Evoker (healer)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): Dream Breath#355936 HoT greatly buffed, smaller upfront heal; Spiritual Clarity#376150 / Expanded Lungs#444845 nerfed; Font of Magic#375783 now also
cuts empower cast time (niche). 30-yard range (shortest healer range) — stand central, use Hover#358267 / Verdant Embrace / Rescue#370665 / Zephyr#374227 to move.

## The spec in one line
Put Echo#364343 buffs out (Echo, Temporal Anomaly), then consume them all at once with **Merithra's Blessing** (a super Reversion). Temporal Anomaly on cooldown,
Dream Breath often (it guarantees a Merithra's Blessing), never waste Essence or Essence Burst.

## Hero trees and builds
- **Flameshaper everywhere** (both guides; Wowhead "Raid (Best)", "Mythic+ (Best)"). Extra charge of Dream Breath and Fire Breath (Legacy of the Lifebinder), Consume Flame#444088
  (Emerald Blossom / Verdant Embrace explode Dream Breath HoTs), Lifecinders (give an ally a weaker Obsidian Scales). Easier on mana, more sustained HPS.
- Chronowarden: competitive; Nozdormu Adept#431715 (Temporal Anomaly ~25% more often), Tip the Scales#370553 + Temporal Burst#431695 as a pseudo-cooldown, stronger Verdant Embrace.
  More burst, more mana trouble, less total output.
- Many spec nodes are nearly equal (Wowhead): Lifespark#443177 (more DPS, harder), Renewing Breath#371257 vs Lifeforce Mender#376179 (Renewing Breath better if less experienced
  or before the tier set), Spiritual Clarity path goes through weak Lifebind#373270.
- **Stasis#370537 vs Dream Flight#359816** (choice node): stacked raid / burst matters → Dream Flight; spread raid → Stasis. Season 2: lean Stasis unless Dream Flight is perfect.
  Icy Veins: Dream Flight "will often be worse".
- Energy Loop#372233 (mana from Disintegrate#356995): ~2% healing trade; fix your casting before taking it.
- Apex **Merithra's Blessing#1256577** (3 ranks): Essence spenders can proc it — turns your next Reversion#367364 into a bouncing burst heal + full Reversion; rank 2: Reversion
  also reduces damage taken / heals per point; rank 3: Dream Breath's instant heal much bigger and **every Dream Breath procs Merithra's Blessing**.
  → keep Reversion on as many players as possible. Each Echo / Emerald Blossom has 20% to proc it (Wowhead).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Masterful Cunning (any secondary), Overload.

## Echo rules
The next healing spell copies onto everyone with Echo (with all its talents: Reversion copies Golden Hour#378196, Grace Period#376239 +10% healing taken, Essence Burst chances).
Consumed by Living Flame, Verdant Embrace, Reversion, Dream Breath (and Merithra's Blessing). **Emerald Blossom does NOT consume Echo** (Midnight). Lifebind doesn't copy.
Twin Echoes#1242031: each Emerald Blossom gives a stack (max 2) that makes your next Echo also hit a nearby ally.

## Raid priority (Icy Veins, Flameshaper; matches Wowhead)
1. Stasis on cooldown (Chronowarden: with Tip the Scales).
2. **Temporal Anomaly#373861 on cooldown** (Echoes on 5 + big absorb). Expensive but worth it — line up with the boss's damage; it's fine to skip quiet moments (don't blow 50% mana in the Bloodlust minute).
3. **Dream Breath on cooldown at Rank 1** (guaranteed Merithra's Blessing). Cast it **after** consuming Echoes (so it doesn't eat them).
4. Essence Burst#369297 → Emerald Blossom#355913 (for Twin Echoes) — unless already at 2 Twin Echoes stacks, then Echo.
5. Echo with any Essence.
6. **Consume Echoes with Merithra's Blessing** (Reversion if you have no proc).
7. Verdant Embrace#360995 after a ramp when out of Essence.
8. Fire Breath#357208 (max rank) between ramps → Leaping Flames Living Flame for 1-2 Essence Bursts.
9. Living Flame#361469 on the boss to farm Essence Bursts (mana refund on enemies). Disintegrate only with Energy Loop.
- Ramp cycle: Dream Breath → Echoes (Temporal Anomaly, Echo, Blossom on bursts) → Merithra's Blessing when damage hits → repeat. If Dream Breath is ready while you hold a Merithra
  proc, spend the proc fast and restart with Dream Breath.
- Verdant Embrace between ramps: with a burst: Merithra's → Blossom → one Echo → Verdant Embrace (eats 2 Echoes, max Primacy#431657) → Dream Breath. Without: Merithra's → Verdant → Dream Breath.

## Inner Flame window (after Stasis or Dream Flight; Inner Flame#1242745)
HoTs +50% for 15 s and Living Flame/Reversion double Essence Burst chance (+ 2-piece Living Flames) → **chain Emerald Blossoms** (Consume Flame on the big Dream Breaths);
when out of bursts: Merithra's Blessing / Temporal Anomaly + Merithra's / Leaping Flames to refill. Max-rank Fire Breath before Stasis for Leaping Flames.
OK to Blossom even at 2 Twin Echoes stacks in this window. Release Stasis after the bursts and Merithra proc are spent.

## Stasis combos
Stores your next 3 healing spells (no cost/cooldown on release, same order, same targets, same empower rank; facing direction for Dream Breath). 30 s to release once full.
Always put Temporal Anomaly **last** (or its Echoes get eaten by the next stored spell).
- Flameshaper raid: **Dream Breath, Dream Breath, Temporal Anomaly** (pool both Dream Breath charges; if one isn't ready, Emerald Blossom instead).
- Chronowarden: Dream Breath, Temporal Anomaly, Emerald Blossom/Echo (+ Tip the Scales).
- M+: Dream Breath, Temporal Anomaly, Merithra's Blessing / Verdant Embrace.
- Pre-pull cheese: 3 Temporal Anomalies (cast the third as you pull). Mass dispel: 3 Cauterizing Flames#374251.

## Other cooldowns
- **Rewind#363534**: heals 33% of damage taken in the last 5 s (doubled in dungeons) → press right **after** the most dangerous damage. Common mistake: holding it too long.
- Dream Flight: big AoE heal flying over a stacked group + long HoT; you get an absorb while airborne.
- Zephyr: you + 4 closest take 20% less AoE damage for 8 s (easier in M+).
- Time Dilation#357170 (single-target reduction, use often), Obsidian Scales#363916, Tip the Scales (Chronowarden: use right away in hard moments).

## Pre-pull (Wowhead)
Blessing of the Bronze#364342 every pull; Source of Magic#369459 on another healer (highest HPS); flask, food, Thalassian Phoenix Oil, rune; **Temporal Anomaly a few seconds before the pull**.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: consuming an Essence Burst fires a Living Flame at your target or an injured ally.
- 4-piece: Verdant Embrace grants an Essence Burst; green spells heal 5% more. Mostly passive.

## Stats
- Wowhead (raid): Intellect > Crit > Mastery > Haste > Vers; M+: Crit > Haste > Mastery > Vers. (Wowhead also says Mastery is "by far our best stat" until ~80-90% rating.)
- Icy Veins (all content): Intellect > **Mastery** > Crit > Haste > Vers.
- Mastery: Life-Binder#363510: full bonus when your health % ≥ the target's (always on self-heals). Crit doesn't boost absorbs (Temporal Anomaly) or Rewind; crits extend Reversion.

## Consumables (Wowhead)
Potion of Recklessness; Silvermoon Health Potion; Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Harandar Celebration; flask "see below" (Blood Knights fine in M+).
Enchants: Rite of the Hash'ey#273072 weapon, Mark of the Worldsoul, Arcanoweave Spellthread, Eyes of the Eagle#243957 rings; Indecipherable Diamond + Flawless Quick Amethyst / Masterful Garnet.
