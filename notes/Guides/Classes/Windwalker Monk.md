# Windwalker Monk (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): mostly tuning, brings the hero trees closer. Nerfs: Zenith Stomp#1272696 −30%, Weapon of Wind#1272678 5%, Tigereye Brew#1261703 crit damage 5/10%, Celestial Conduit#443028 −25%.
Buffs: autos +30%, Blackout Kick#100784 +50%, Tiger Palm#100780 +200%, Dual Threat +30%, Spinning Crane Kick#101546 +38%, Whirling Dragon Punch#152175 +30%, Jade Ignition +95%, Temple Training#442743 30%,
Calming Presence#388664 7% DR.

## The spec in one line
Turn Energy into Chi with Tiger Palm and spend it on cooldown spenders (Fists of Fury#113656, Rising Sun Kick#107428, Whirling Dragon Punch, Strike of the Windlord#392983, Rushing Wind Kick#1250566).
**Never press the same ability twice in a row** (Mastery: Combo Strikes#115636; a buff tracks your last press). Don't cap Energy or Chi, keep cooldowns rolling, spend Dance of Chi-Ji#325201 and Blackout Kick!#116768
(Combo Breaker#137384, both stack to 2) before they overstack. Zenith#1249625 (2 charges) is the burst window. Some downtime is normal.

## Hero trees
- **Shado-Pan** (both guides' pick for raid; Icy Veins: "all boss fights this tier", ahead in pure single target and AoE): baseline Windwalker + Flurry Strikes, Shado Over the Battlefield#1262612 splashes AoE. Simple, forgiving.
- **Conduit of the Celestials**: higher ceiling, faster, more punishing ("Advanced" raid builds on Wowhead; Wowhead/Icy Veins "high keys" M+ and Delves recommended). Invoke Xuen#123904 (mainly enables Celestial Conduit)
  and **Heart of the Jade Serpent#443294** windows (faster Fists, big cooldown reduction).
  Heart from Zenith stacks with Heart from Conduit / Strike / WDP — but Conduit's Heart does **not** stack with Strike/WDP's → stagger Conduit between Strike/WDP; cast Zenith during Heart.
- Defense: Conduit's Niuzao's Protection#442747 / Jade Sanctuary#443059 a bit better. Very weak defensively overall; no immunity.
- Apex **Tigereye Brew**: stacks in combat (out of combat fills back to 10), spent on Zenith for crit chance (cap 20). Spreading Zenith charges out keeps stacks high; passive otherwise.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power (sim), Overload.

## Single target — Shado-Pan (Wowhead)
1. Whirling Dragon Punch (in its grace window). 2. Strike of the Windlord if low on Chi or Zenith almost over. 3. Zenith Stomp if low on Chi or Zenith almost over.
4. Tiger Palm to avoid capping Energy outside cooldowns, or if short of Chi for Fists. 5. **Fists of Fury**. 6. Rushing Wind Kick.
7. Spinning Crane Kick with Dance of Chi-Ji **and** Unbroken Rhythm#1296624 (4-piece). 8. **Rising Sun Kick**. 9. Blackout Kick with Combo Breaker, or during Zenith with Obsidian Spiral#1249832.
10. Spinning Crane Kick in Zenith with >4 Chi or Dance of Chi-Ji. 11. Tiger Palm under 2 Chi. 12. Spinning Crane Kick with Dance of Chi-Ji. 13. Slicing Winds#1217413. 14. Tiger Palm without overcapping Chi.
Icy Veins extras: Rising Sun Kick with Bloodlust (no Dance of Chi-Ji); Blackout Kick at 2 Blackout Kick! stacks before Rising Sun Kick filler; Touch of Death#322109 low in the list; Blackout Kick last.
## Single target — Conduit (Wowhead)
WDP and Strike only if Xuen is ≥10 s away → Zenith Stomp (low Chi / Zenith ending / during Conduit channel) → Celestial Conduit if no Heart from WDP/Strike (cancel with the next GCD) →
Fists of Fury if Heart has <1 s left → Tiger Palm (Energy cap / Chi for Fists) → Fists → Rushing Wind Kick → Spinning Crane Kick with Unbroken Rhythm → Rising Sun Kick → Tiger Palm under 2 Chi →
SCK with Dance of Chi-Ji → Slicing Winds → Tiger Palm → Blackout Kick.

## AoE (2+ targets)
- Shado-Pan (Wowhead): WDP → Strike → Tiger Palm for Fists Chi → Fists → **SCK with Unbroken Rhythm (4-piece)** → Tiger Palm (Energy cap, no Zenith) → RSK to enable WDP → Rushing Wind Kick (no 4-piece) →
  SCK in Zenith at 5+ → RSK with Lust/Zenith (no 4-piece) → BoK in Zenith with Obsidian Spiral and RSK on cooldown → SCK → Slicing Winds → BoK with Combo Breaker → Tiger Palm (<5 Chi, no Zenith) → BoK (Shadowboxing Treads) → RSK → BoK.
- Conduit: Fists if Heart is ending → WDP / Strike (Xuen ≥10 s away) → Fists → RSK for WDP → Rushing Wind Kick (no 4-piece) → RSK in Heart (no 4-piece) → SCK → Slicing Winds → RSK → BoK.
- Tier (Icy Veins): with 4-piece, **SCK right after Fists finishes, before RSK, whenever 2+ targets**.

## Openers
- Shado-Pan (ST and AoE): Tiger Palm → **Zenith** + potion/racial/trinkets (stat buffs first) → Fists → RSK → Fists again if RSK reset it → WDP → Strike → Slicing Winds → priority.
- Conduit: precast Slicing Winds → Tiger Palm → Invoke Xuen → (Zenith with potions — Icy Veins) → Strike → Fists → RSK → (Fists if reset) → WDP → (Strike, Slicing Winds) → priority.
  Don't cast Conduit too early (overlapping Heart from Strike/WDP). Repeat this sequence at every Xuen.
- Pre-pull: cast Spinning Crane Kick well before the pull so Tiger Palm opens without breaking Mastery; place Transcendence#101643.

## Cooldowns
- Zenith (2 charges, 60/70/80/90 s by talents): spenders cost 1 less Chi, Blackout Kick gives 1 s more cooldown reduction (free BoK = main filler), resets RSK, +2 Chi. Plan for Lust, trinkets, add phases.
  With Obsidian Spiral BoK *generates* Chi in Zenith; weave Tiger Palm for Teachings of the Monastery#116645.
- Fists/RSK: hold Fists 1-2 GCDs for RSK only if you have 5 Chi for both; otherwise delay nothing.
- Touch of Death: on cooldown when it fits (Fatal Touch#394123 no longer buffs); bonus if the target's health < your max health (100% vs 35%).
- Touch of Karma#122470: defensive first, damage second. Fortifying Brew#115203 **before** Touch of Death / Touch of Karma (both snapshot max health).
