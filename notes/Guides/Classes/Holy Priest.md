# Holy Priest (healer)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Midnight changes (Wowhead): Divine Hymn now also gives you Guardian Spirit while channeling; Benediction rank 3 changed (Serenity now always upgrades your
next Flash Heal to Benediction; Apotheosis no longer does); Apotheosis cuts Holy Word mana cost by 70% (was 50%).

## The spec in one line
Holy Words (Serenity, Sanctify) are rotational: cast them almost as soon as they're up, and keep Prayer of Mending on cooldown.
Every other spell exists to shorten Holy Word cooldowns. Apotheosis = triple Holy Word cooldown reduction.

## Hero trees and builds
- **Oracle for raid** (both guides; Wowhead "Raid (Best)", "Mythic+ (Best)"). Steady passive healing (Prayer of Mending#33076 has 2 charges, Renew#139, Piety#1246802
  passive overheal redirect), triage via Prompt Prognosis#1246799. Wowhead: Archon can be stronger in 30-player raids. Icy Veins: Archon more overheal (all in windows).
- Archon: Halo#120517 (Archon only), Surge of Light#114255 procs usable on Prayer of Healing#596 (Spiritwell), stronger Apotheosis.
- Raid build focus (Wowhead): Oracle with heavy Flash Heal#2061 (Dispersing Light#1215265 cleave), lots of Prayer of Mending and Serenity via **Ultimate Serenity#1246517**
  (Sanctify's cooldown reduction goes into Serenity instead). Oracle does NOT use Prayer of Healing.
- Capstones (Icy Veins): Ultimate Serenity (popular, simplifies), Lasting Words (passive), Divine Image (weak but cheap to reach), Lightweaver (similar output, more mana, less forgiving → avoided).
- M+ (Wowhead): like raid, no Prayer of Healing, Holy Fire#14914 talents for downtime damage.
- Apex **Benediction#1262755**: Prayer of Mending bounces periodically turn your next Flash Heal into Benediction (more healing + Cosmic Ripple); rank 2 more healing;
  rank 3 Divine Hymn pulses trigger Cosmic Ripple and Serenity always upgrades the next Flash Heal. Procs are normalised (same for both trees) — cast Prayer of Mending often.
  Don't overcap Benediction stacks (2).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire (better than Void-Touched Orbs), Self-Mending (or Void-Tainted Shell), Lingering, Critical Power, Overload (any final node is fine).

## Raid priority — Oracle (Wowhead)
1. Holy Word: Serenity#2050 on cooldown (single target). Have a charge ready before Apotheosis.
2. Prayer of Mending on cooldown (pool one charge if you'll need to move; Icy Veins: on an injured ally for Prompt Prognosis, but never wait while at max charges).
3. Flash Heal to spot heal — don't overcap Benediction (2).
4. Smite#585 in downtime (Surge of Light procs).
5. Divine Hymn#64843 for raid-wide damage.
- Spread Renews: 4-piece makes each Prayer of Mending cast give a Renew to its target → put PoM and Serenity on people **without** Renew.

## Raid priority — Archon (Wowhead + Icy Veins)
- **Halo just before Apotheosis or Divine Hymn**, as damage begins (keep it expanding/contracting through the damage). Don't hold Halo more than 30 s.
- Apotheosis#200183 resets Holy Words; spend Surge of Light on Prayer of Healing (holds 2). Prayer of Mending on cooldown. Prayer of Healing = mana burn when you can afford it.
- Ideal Archon Apotheosis: Sanctify → Serenity → Chastise → Prayer of Mending (all onto cooldown) → Halo → Apotheosis → Power Infusion (if not assigned) → normal rotation.

## Icy Veins general raid priority (skip anything that would mostly overheal)
Sanctify#34861 on a cluster → Serenity on an injured ally → Chastise (for Divine Image) → Prayer of Mending (injured ally / melee) → Prayer of Healing with Surge of Light →
Benediction → Prayer of Healing with Lightweaver → Flash Heal with Surge → Prayer of Healing → Flash Heal (emergency) → Flash Heal → Holy Nova (moving / little healing) → Smite.
- Never cast Prayer of Healing without Lightweaver or Surge of Light (cast Flash Heal/Benediction instead).
- Casting a lower spell while a Holy Word is ready = a mistake (Flash Heal instead of Serenity, Prayer of Healing instead of Sanctify).
- Raid damage spells: only Smite and Holy Nova (cheap, Surge procs).
- Mana: use cooldowns as rotational spells; Surge procs on Prayer of Healing; Flash Heal only for near-death; Smite/Holy Nova when nobody needs healing.

## Holy Word cooldown reduction (Serendipity, Icy Veins)
Serenity −6 s per Flash Heal; Sanctify −6 s per Prayer of Healing; Chastise −4 s per Smite/Holy Nova. Voice of Harmony: Serenity −4 s per Prayer of Mending, Sanctify −4 s per Halo,
Chastise −4 s per Holy Fire. Apotheosis = 3x reduction. Light of the Naaru +10% per point. Archon Energy Cycle: Surge consumption reduces Holy Words.
Miracle Worker#235587: 2 charges of Serenity and Sanctify — avoid sitting at 2 (except right before big damage). Epiphany: after a Holy Word proc, cast Prayer of Mending instead of a second Holy Word.

## Cooldowns
- **Apotheosis**: press as often as possible; make every GCD in it cast or reduce a Holy Word. Pool Surge of Light / use Divinity#1215241 for it.
- **Divine Hymn**: raid cooldown (usually assigned). Can be channeled out of line of sight. 12.1: Guardian Spirit on you while channeling (stand in swirls). M+: time so the last 1-2 s overlap the damage.
  Talents: Seraphic Crescendo (shorter cooldown) or Gales of Song (Prayer of Mending stacks per pulse).
- **Guardian Spirit#47788**: +60% healing taken for 10 s, cheats death to 40% hp. Best on a tank before big damage (great on Blood DK); coordinate. Can self-cast.
- Defensives: Desperate Prayer#19236, Fade#586; Protective Light#193063 (−10% damage taken when you Flash Heal anyone, via Binding Heals#368275).
- Power Infusion: on a DPS cooldown (see power-infusion memory).

## Mastery: Echo of Light#77485
Direct heals leave a 4 s HoT; stacks and extends. Also boosts Renew. Nothing to track.

## Divine Image
Play normally; Chastise right before intense healing for a burst. Prayer of Mending on a tight clump (melee) for Blessed Light bounces, if it doesn't delay PoM.

## Tier set (Season 2)
Passive: rewards casting Prayer of Mending often (stat buff + Renews). Wowhead: 4-piece makes PoM always give its target a Renew.
