# Frost Mage (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): new tier set boosts Glacial Spike#199786 a lot (big for Frostfire); Ice Barrier#11426 absorbs less; Improved Warding#1297073 new; Temporal Realignment#1244090 nerfed → **Time Walk#1244087 for everyone**.

## The spec in one line
Most spells stack **Freezing#1221389** (cap 20, you will overcap) on the target; Ice Lance#30455 / Comet Storm#1247777 / Glacial Spike (with Glacial Shatter) consume it for **Shatter#1246769** damage.
Spend Fingers of Frost#112965 (Ice Lance as if max Freezing) and Brain Freeze#190447 (resets Flurry#44614) quickly, keep Ray of Frost#205021 (main cooldown, 4 s channel, 1 min) and Frozen Orb#84714 rolling,
Frostbolt#116 / Frostfire Bolt#431044 as filler, Ice Lance while moving. Always be casting.

## Hero trees
- **Spellslinger** for single target and most raid (both guides; Wowhead "Best ST", Icy Veins "recommended" ST and light cleave/raid). Frost Splinter#443722 stream from Ice Lance; only tree that cuts Frozen Orb cooldown (Spellfrost Teachings).
  Reactive Barrier#444827, extra Mirror Images, Slippery Slinging#444752.
- **Frostfire**: front-loaded burst, locked into Comet Storm (Isothermic Core#431095), Duality#1262843, Imbued Warding#431066 (Wowhead: generally the better defense). Tier set makes it strong:
  Wowhead "Best stacked cleave" and "Best M+"; Icy Veins recommended for M+/Delves.
- Raid builds (Wowhead): most Venomous Abyss fights need **Splitting Ice#56377 + Fractured Frost#378448** (cleave); pure single target skips them for Hailstones#1247742 (tier synergy).
  Odd Frostfire raid build puts **no points in the Apex** to grab all capstones (flatter damage, fewer Ray casts). Icy Veins light cleave: swap Rimecaster + Piercing Cold for Splitting Ice + Fractured Frost.
  AoE: Lonely Winter#205024 beats Summon Water Elemental#31687. Icy Veins: Frigid Focus over Splintering Ray when focusing one target.
- Apex **Hand of Frost#1263249**: Shatters can fire an extra hit (+1 Freezing), chance scales with stacks consumed; stacking 1% damage buff; 4 procs during Ray; **Ray of Frost gets 2 charges** →
  hold a charge for vulnerability phases, priority adds or trinket procs (keep it under 2 charges).
- Glacial Bulwark#1244110 + Improved Ice Barrier#1244069: 2 charges of Ice Barrier and Ice Block#45438/Ice Cold#414659 — plan defensives per boss.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload (Void-Touched Orbs for outdoor).

## Single target — Spellslinger
Wowhead: Flurry with Brain Freeze if Thermal Void#1247729 isn't up → Ice Lance at 2 Fingers of Frost → Frozen Orb → Glacial Spike → Ice Lance with Fingers → Ice Lance at **6+ Freezing** →
Ray of Frost at ≤3 Icicles#1246832 → Flurry → Frostbolt.
Icy Veins: Comet Storm → Flurry with Brain Freeze → Frozen Orb → Ice Lance with Fingers → Glacial Spike → Ice Lance at 6+ Freezing → Ray of Frost → Flurry → Frostbolt (ignore Thermal Void when deciding).
## Single target — Frostfire
Wowhead: Glacial Spike → Comet Storm → Ray of Frost → Frozen Orb → Ice Lance with Fingers + Thermal Void → Ice Lance at **12+ Freezing** → Flurry → Frostfire Bolt.
Icy Veins: Glacial Spike → Comet Storm → Flurry with Brain Freeze (if Thermal Void not up) → Ice Lance at 2 Fingers → Ray of Frost (cancel it once you have 2 Fingers / as soon as possible with another cast) → Frozen Orb →
Ice Lance with Fingers + Thermal Void → Ice Lance at 12+ Freezing → Flurry → Frostfire Bolt. Icy Veins FAQ: Lance at 6+ (Spellslinger), 10+ (Frostfire).
Ray of Frost sits low because it makes so many stacks — spend stacks first. Single-target list also covers 2 targets.

## AoE (3+)
- Spellslinger (Wowhead): Frozen Orb → Glacial Spike → Blizzard#190356 with Freezing Rain#270233 → Ice Lance with Fingers → Flurry → Blizzard → Frostbolt.
  Icy Veins adds Ice Nova / Cone of Cold, hard-cast Blizzard only at 5+ (keep making Splinters instead).
- Frostfire (Wowhead): Glacial Spike → Comet Storm a few GCDs after Glacial Spike (let Rapid Refreezing#1310248 finish) → Ray → Frozen Orb → Blizzard with Freezing Rain → Blizzard at 8+ → Flurry → Frostfire Bolt.
  Spend Frostfire Empowerment#431176 before Comet Storm so it consumes more stacks. Icy Veins: Cone of Frost undertuned for Frostfire.
- Frost relies on ambient cleave on a main target; aim Frozen Orb at the pack (it slows on first hit, then tracks your target after 3 s). Comet Storm can Shatter several targets.

## Openers
- Spellslinger: precast Frostbolt (Blizzard in AoE) → Flurry → Frozen Orb → Ray of Frost (→ Ice Lance).
- Frostfire: precast Frostfire Bolt at 2 s (Blizzard in AoE) → Ray of Frost → Flurry → Frozen Orb.
- Pre-pull: Arcane Intellect#1459, Water Elemental if talented, Ice Barrier.

## Cooldowns
- Ray of Frost: get the whole channel off; Shimmer#212653 to safety instead of cancelling (Icy Veins Frostfire cancels early on purpose — see above). Comet Storm replaces the button after Ray.
- Time Warp#80353 before Ray of Frost or on the raid call.
- No damage-boost cooldown otherwise → burst is flexible.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: each Freezing stack Shattered has 4% to make an Icicle; Glacial Spike +20%.
- 4-piece: Glacial Spike can rapidly make 5 Icicles over 1 s; Shatter +5%. Passive — just more Glacial Spikes.

## Stats
Both: Intellect > **Mastery** > Crit > Haste > Vers (Icy Veins: with higher gear Haste evens out with Crit and Mastery; no crit or haste caps). Mastery: Freeze and Shatter#1246752.

## Disagreements
- Priority order differs (Wowhead puts Brain Freeze Flurry/Ice Lance 2 Fingers first for Spellslinger; Icy Veins puts Comet Storm first — Spellslinger rarely has Comet Storm).
- Icy Veins Frostfire cancels Ray early; Wowhead says get the full channel.
- Hero choice by content: both pick Spellslinger for raid single target; Frostfire for stacked cleave / M+.
