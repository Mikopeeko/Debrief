## The spec in one line
Build Holy Power with Judgment#275779 and Blessed Hammer#204019 (keep both on cooldown), spend 3-5 on Shield of the Righteous#53600 to keep it up nearly 100%, never cap Holy Power,
stand in Consecration#26573, and roll your defensives.

## Hero trees
- **Lightsmith** — Wowhead "Raid (Best)", "Mythic+ (Best)" (both trees very close, play what you like). Icy Veins: Lightsmith for raid and M+ (more damage and defense now) —
  though one later line on the same page says "towards Templar"; the explicit recommendation box says Lightsmith.
  Holy Bulwark#432459 / Sacred Weapon#432472 once a minute; generate max Holy Power in Avenging Wrath for Blessing of the Forge#434132. Rite of Sanctification#433568 replaces oils.
- Templar: Divine Toll#375576 → Hammer of Light; burst every minute with Avenging Wrath; better for short burst / delves (Wowhead delves "Best"). Needs Divine Toll talented.
- Apex **Glory of the Vanguard#1267215**: Judgment/Hammer of Wrath can give Vanguard → next Avenger's Shield#31935 hits everything between you and the target; ranks 2-3: consuming Vanguard gives
  up to 3 Holy Power (Judgment +10/20% damage); rank 4: SotR +20% after Vanguard + AoE, and every Avenger's Shield in Avenging Wrath counts as Vanguard.
- Instrument of the Divine#1277162: SotR can spend up to 5 Holy Power for much more damage — DPS gain but less uptime and you lose a strong defensive talent.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Void-Touched Orbs, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power (damage) / Masterful Cunning (defense), Echoes.

## Opener (Wowhead)
Pre-pull Consecration near the boss, Blessed Hammer for Holy Power, Hand of Reckoning#62124 on pull, Ardent Defender#31850 if tanking first, Avenger's Shield, Avenging Wrath#31884,
Divine Toll (+ Hammer of Light as Templar), then rotation.

## Single target priority
Wowhead Templar: Avenging Wrath on cooldown → Hammer of Light → Judgment → SotR at 3-5 (or free) → Word of Glory#85673 under 50% / before a big hit → Avenger's Shield →
Divine Toll at 0 Holy Power (sync with Wings) → Blessed Hammer / Hammer of the Righteous#53595 → Consecration filler.
Wowhead Lightsmith: Avenging Wrath → Sacred Weapon (outside Wings) → Avenger's Shield → Judgment → Divine Toll at 0 HP → Holy Bulwark charges → Consecration.
Icy Veins (both trees): Avenging Wrath → Sacred Weapon (no Wings) → Divine Toll → Hammer of Light → Consecration if not in it → Avenger's Shield with Vanguard → **SotR at 3-5** →
Hammer of Wrath / Judgment → Blessed Hammer → Hammer of the Righteous → Avenger's Shield → Arcane Torrent → Word of Glory with Shining Light (under 50% first) → Consecration refresh.
AoE (Icy Veins): Avenging Wrath → Divine Toll → Hammer of Light → Consecration → Avenger's Shield (Vanguard first) → Hammer of Wrath/Judgment → Blessed Hammer → HotR.

## Active mitigation (Icy Veins)
- SotR: 3 Holy Power, 4.5 s armor buff, off GCD → aim for near-100% uptime; pool Holy Power in downtime (e.g. off-tanking). Don't cap at 5; avoid spending right at 3 if you'll need it again soon.
- Word of Glory: heals more the lower you are → use at ~30-50% health, ideally only when **free with Shining Light** (every 3 SotR gives a charge, max 2). Lightsmith: use Shining Light more freely
  (it reduces the hero ability's cooldown). Hand of the Protector#315924 can heal others (costs SotR and mana).
- Haste ~20% = very high SotR uptime; ~25-30% = room for Word of Glory too.

## Cooldowns
- Avenging Wrath: +20% damage/healing (+crit talented), Judgment → Hammer of Wrath. Sentinel (replaces it): 15 Divine Resolve stacks (2% HP and DR each), lose 1/s after 5 s unless you spend Holy Power;
  keeps the crit since 12.1 (no longer a DPS loss).
- Ardent Defender: −20% for 8 s, 1.5 min, cheats one killing blow (heal to 12%); use freely and often (Wowhead: as often as possible to smooth damage).
- Guardian of Ancient Kings: −50% for 8 s, 3 min — plan it for the most dangerous moments (second charge with Empyrean Authority#1246481).
- Divine Shield#642 (immunity drops threat — Final Stand taunts everything; or taunt and cancel), Blessing of Protection#1022 / Spellwarding#204018 (also dodge random target picks if cast before selection),
  Lay on Hands (save someone else; use Word of Glory for yourself), Blessing of Sacrifice#6940, Blessing of Freedom#1044.
- Auras: Devotion (3% DR; a Holy Paladin should run it), Crusader (mount speed, Divine Steed), Concentration (−30% silence/interrupt duration).

## Tier set (Season 2)

- 4-piece: Blessed Hammer / Hammer of the Righteous / Judgment deal 20% extra Holy damage (40% on crit). → shifts weight toward Crit.
