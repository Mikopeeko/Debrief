Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5).
Oct 27 hotfix (aimed at area damage): Trick Shots ricochet 75% -> 85%, Explosive Shot +10%, Volley +10%. No priority change (audit, Oct 28).
**12.1.5 change** (both): Unload#1277548 removed, Blood Fletching#1319011 added (crit Aimed Shot leaves a double Master Marksman bleed + 4 s of extra bleed). Rapid Fire#257044 no longer fires Arcane Shots that spend
Precise Shots#260240 → **Sentinel must spend Precise Shots manually before every generator again** (OK to waste one with Rapid Fire during Trueshot). Dark Ranger: Rapid Fire on cooldown regardless.

## The spec in one line
Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire on cooldown, **every one followed by a Precise Shots spender** (Arcane Shot#185358 / Kill Shot#53351 single target, Multi-Shot#257620 with Trick Shots#257621 on 2+), Explosive Shot (twice) and
Volley#260243 on cooldown, Trueshot#288613 on cooldown, and **always be casting** (Steady Shot#56641 fills every gap, even moving).

  Don't Look Back#450373 passive defense. Conditioning#1253887 for movement.

- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power (Mastery if 1300+ crit), Overload.

## Single target — Sentinel (Icy Veins full list; Wowhead same)
1. Explosive Shot on cooldown, cast twice (Unstable Trigger) / unless Lock and Load#194595 is up (Tactical Reload).
2. Volley on cooldown.
3. **Trueshot** on cooldown — hold the last one of the fight for 30 Bullseye#204089 stacks; delay up to 15 s so Explosive Shot goes right before it; press it on a target **without** Sentinel's Mark (Feathered Frenzy marks your target);
   rule of thumb: Trueshot right before a Rapid Fire.
4. Moonlight Chakram with ~5 s of Trueshot left.
5. During Trueshot: Aimed Shot when you have no Precise Shots (single target only).
6. Rapid Fire on cooldown if you have no Precise Shots to spend, or during Trueshot (or 2+ targets).
7. Kill Shot to spend Precise Shots (Deathblow procs); Multi-Shot on 2+ (unless focusing a priority target); Arcane Shot to spend Precise Shots.
8. Aimed Shot on cooldown (prefer Sentinel-Marked targets). 9. Moonlight Chakram. 10. Steady Shot.
Dark Ranger: Black Arrow with Precise Shots → Explosive Shot x2 → Volley → in Trueshot Aimed Shot without PS if Black Arrow is ready or Aimed Shot near 2 charges → Trueshot → Rapid Fire → Wailing Arrow → Arcane Shot (PS) → Aimed Shot → Black Arrow → Steady Shot.
Trinkets inside Trueshot (stat trinkets first).

## AoE (Trick Shots builds)
Explosive Shot x2 on **two different targets** (tier cooldown reduction scales with ticks) unless a priority target → Volley → Chakram before Trueshot ends → Multi-Shot to spend PS / restore Trick Shots (mouseover macro; prefer unmarked targets) →
Rapid Fire with Trick Shots → Aimed Shot with Trick Shots (prefer marked targets) → Chakram → Steady Shot. **Never Aimed Shot or Rapid Fire without Trick Shots.**
Hydra cleave: play the single-target list, Multi-Shot as the spender from 2 targets.

## Openers
ST: Hunter's Mark#257284 → precast Aimed Shot ~2.5 s before → (Black Arrow) → Explosive Shot x2 → Volley → Trueshot + potion + trinket + racial → Aimed Shot → … (Sentinel: Rapid Fire → Arcane Shot → Aimed Shot → Arcane Shot).
AoE: Explosive Shot → Volley → Aimed Shot → Rapid Fire → Aimed Shot → Explosive Shot x2 → Volley → Multi-Shot → Rapid Fire → Multi-Shot → Aimed Shot.

## Cooldowns and mechanics
- Trueshot (90 s Calling the Shots / 2 min Unerring Vision; 17 s): +crit chance/damage, faster Aimed Shot/Rapid Fire recovery, free Spotter's Mark.
- Potions: Potion of Recklessness with Trueshot; on 5+ min fights a second potion = **Liquid Luster** ~10 s before the execute Trueshot (Bullseye already gives lots of crit).
- Precise Shots doesn't stack → spend before the next Aimed Shot. Volley's free Trick Shots rarely matters.
- Movement: only stand still for Aimed Shot; move freely otherwise.

## Tier set (Season 2)
Explosive Shot lasts longer and each tick cuts Aimed Shot / Rapid Fire cooldowns by 0.5 s (4-piece#1296634).
