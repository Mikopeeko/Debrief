# Balance Druid (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): Stellar Amplification#450212 now one target only (new Starsurge moves it); Bounteous Bloom#429215 now +4 s Treant duration (not an extra Treant); Star Cascade#1271206 no longer from Wrath,
30% chance, 50% damage; Sculpt the Stars swapped with Astral Communion; Orbit Breaker trackable in the Cooldown Manager. Spenders up, generation down.

## The spec in one line
DoTs up (Moonfire#8921, Sunfire#93402), build Astral Power with Wrath#5176/Starfire#194153, spend on Starsurge#78674 (1 target) / Starfall#191034 (2-3+), never cap Astral Power, and line up
Eclipse#1239669 (32 s, 2 charges) with Celestial Alignment#194223 / Incarnation#102560 and your hero cooldown (Fury of Elune or Force of Nature). Always be casting.

## Eclipse rules (Midnight system)
- Eclipse is now a button: Wrath last → Solar Eclipse#1233346, Starfire last → Lunar Eclipse#1233272. Can't press Eclipse during Celestial Alignment; pressing CA during Eclipse replaces it. CA/Incarnation give both.
- Treat Eclipse like a damage cooldown: enter with lots of Astral Power (enough for 2+ Starsurges), don't sit at 2 charges, pair with other cooldowns.
- Inside Eclipse: spend (Starsurge/Starfall), then fillers. Don't waste Eclipse GCDs on DoTs/Fury of Elune/Force of Nature (do those just before) — except Lunation Fury of Elune which is sent on cooldown.
- Outside: maintenance (DoTs), generate, cast Force of Nature / Fury of Elune right before entering. Spending Astral Power at the very end of Eclipse is a tiny gain (keep Starsurge for movement).
- Expect 65-75% Eclipse uptime incl. cooldowns. Entering any Eclipse / CA resets Solstice#343647 to 6 s.

## Hero trees
- **Elune's Chosen — best in raid and M+** (both guides). Lunar Calling#429523: no Solar Eclipse; Starfire is the only builder (drop Wrath; you can open with Wrath to avoid pulling).
  Raid: **Arcane Affinity#429540** (not Lunation) → fewer Fury of Elune#202770 casts; plan Incarnation / Lunar Eclipse to be ready every 60 s for Fury of Elune (45 s with Radiant Moonlight#394121).
  M+ / multi-target: Lunation#429539 → Fury of Elune on cooldown.
- Keeper of the Grove: Force of Nature#205636 Treants (Harmony of the Grove#428731 buff); Control of the Dream#434249 (hold Force of Nature / CA / Convoke up to 15 s with no loss). Better for spread cleave and timed adds
  (Wowhead) / quick burst (Icy Veins). Raid KotG build = 1-minute Convoke#391528 + Force of Nature together with CA or Eclipse.
- Choice nodes: Incarnation vs Convoke; Starweaver#393940 vs Rattle the Stars#393954 (free choice with both trees). Meteor Storm (periodic burst AoE) vs Aetherial Kindling (long AoE).
- Apex **Ascendant Eclipses#1261564**: entering Eclipse makes the next Wrath/Starfire instant and the first 3 Starsurge/Starfall +20%; crits in Eclipse add a 20% "languish" DoT; entering Eclipse fires Solar/Lunar Bolts.
  → spend the instant on Starfire going into cooldowns; don't sit at 2 Eclipse charges before cooldowns.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload.

## Single target — Elune's Chosen (Wowhead)
Cooldowns: Lunar Eclipse if at 2 charges → Moonfire if it'd expire before the upcoming CA/Incarnation ends → **Fury of Elune with enough AP for 2 Starsurges when CA/Incarn or Lunar Eclipse is ready** →
CA/Incarnation on the GCD right after Fury of Elune (or if the fight ends before FoE returns) → else Lunar Eclipse right after FoE.
Outside Eclipse: refresh Moonfire in pandemic → Lunar Eclipse with 2+ Starsurges of AP if it fits before the next FoE + CA overlap → Starfall with a Touch the Cosmos#450356 / Starweaver's Warp#393942 proc →
Starsurge to avoid capping or with Touch the Cosmos / Starweaver's Weft#393944 → Starfire.
Inside Eclipse: Convoke in CA when you can't spend AP → Starsurge for Ascendant Eclipses stacks / procs / capping / movement → refresh missing DoTs → Starfire.
## Single target — Keeper of the Grove (Wowhead)
Eclipse at 2 charges → Sunfire before CA → Fury of Elune before Force of Nature → **Force of Nature with AP for 3 Starsurges and CA or Eclipse ready** → trinket with CA → CA/Incarnation the GCD after Force of Nature →
(else Eclipse after FoN) → outside: Moonfire/Sunfire pandemic, Eclipse with 2+ Starsurges if it fits, procs, Starsurge to avoid capping, Wrath. Inside: Convoke in the FoN window when you can't spend, Fury of Elune if FoN is >5 s away,
procs, Starsurge, DoTs, Wild Mushroom#88747 (movement), Wrath.
Icy Veins general list: don't cap AP → DoTs (recast before CA) → Fury of Elune / Force of Nature before Eclipse/CA → CA/Incarnation → Convoke inside CA under 40 AP → Eclipse (Solar 1 target, Lunar multi) →
Starfall with procs → Starsurge → Wrath/Starfire. Before leaving Eclipse: save AP if re-entering right away, spend if waiting several seconds.

## AoE
Spread Moonfire while gathering (biggest HP first) → Sunfire once stacked (Force of Nature spreads Moonfire to 6 more) → Force of Nature then Incarnation (KotG) / Fury of Elune (EC) → Lunar Eclipse on 4+ (KotG: Solar up to 3) →
Starfall to spend (Starweaver/Touch the Cosmos procs) → Starfire (KotG inside Incarnation at 3+ spends Ascendant Fires#1263363).
Breakpoints (Icy Veins): Starsurge on 1 target; Starfall always on 3, usually on 2 with Meteorites/Starweaver AoE builds; Lunar Eclipse when Starfire hits 3+. Starweaver Starsurge procs not used at 8+ targets.
DoT breakpoints: only Sunfire targets living 5+ s, Moonfire 8+ s. Convoke before Starfalls on multi-target.
