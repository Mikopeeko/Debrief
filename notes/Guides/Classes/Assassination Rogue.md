# Assassination Rogue (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): all damage +20%; Deathmark#360194 bonus 75% (was 100%); Kingsbane#385627 −15%; new Unstable Toxin (Envenom +18%, 2 s shorter); Apex Implacable rank 1 = Envenom +10% and 2 Energy per combo point;
Internal Bleeding from Kidney Shot/Rupture; Iron Wire silences on stealth/Improved Garrote; Dashing Scoundrel +4% Energy regen per lethal poison; Blindside 10%/20%; Deadly Momentum removed;
Thistle Tea choice (auto vs manual); Atrophic Poison 4% for 60 s; Fatebound Deal Fate 60%.

## The spec in one line
Slow, energy-limited (not GCD-limited) DoT spec: keep **Garrote#703** and **Rupture#1943** up (refresh in pandemic — 30% of the new duration), build with Mutilate#1329 (Fan of Knives#51723 at 2+), finish with
**Envenom#32645 at 5+ combo points**, chaining Envenoms in the last 1-2 s of the buff (Rapid Injection#455072, Inspiring Strike / Poisoner's Drive). Line up **Deathmark (2 min) + Kingsbane (1 min) + Vanish#1856 → Improved Garrote#381632**.
Great execute (Zoldyck Recipe#381798), Caustic Spatter#421975 cleave, best-in-class defensives.

## Hero trees
- **Fatebound**: Hand of Fate#452536 coins; easy, forgiving, uses Feint freely. **Deathstalker**: Deathstalker's Mark#457052 → Darkest Night#457058 (big 7-CP Envenom), Singular Focus#457055 funnel; clunky target swaps
  (Mark for Death to move it); loses value when the boss submerges or targets swap.
- Wowhead: Deathstalker 2-5% stronger everywhere but harder; recommends **Fatebound** for most (marked Best for raid ST, raid, M+), Deathstalker for title-level keys and Delves.
- Icy Veins: intro says "Deathstalker for virtually all content", but its build badges and text recommend **Fatebound Raid Cleave** (Deathstalker ≤1-2% more at best), Deathstalker pure ST only for **Sszorak**, Deathstalker for high keys.
- Raid builds: cleave build (Caustic Spatter + Crimson Tempest#1247227) for most bosses; pure single target possible on Coiled Altar / Entombed Sentinels (Icy Veins, not recommended).
  Wowhead: Scent of Blood#381799 worth it from 2-3 targets (e.g. Lost Explorers); Thrown Precision#381629 in any cleave; Fatal Concoction#392384 over Flying Daggers; **Sudden Demise#423136** instead of the last Implacable point
  on add fights like **Vashnik and Twin Fangs** (−2% single target). Don't drop Zoldyck Recipe in M+ (it's a myth that it "wastes" damage).
- Apex **Implacable#1265387**: Energy on Envenom; more bleed/Nature damage; rank 4 Kingsbane hit + 5 combo points over the next second.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Burning Haste, Overload.

## Single target — Deathstalker (Wowhead)
Maintain Garrote (applies the Mark) → **Shiv#5938 on cooldown** (Toxic Stiletto#1267182 makes it the most efficient builder; never with adds — Fan of Knives then) → Mutilate to 5+ CP → **Envenom at max CP with Darkest Night** (at exactly 6 CP use Shiv/Fan of Knives to reach 7) →
Rupture at 5+ CP — **but never Rupture during Darkest Night** outside the opener (it wouldn't consume a Mark stack; letting Rupture drop is OK) → Vanish → Garrote (Improved Garrote, with Deathmark) → Deathmark on cooldown →
Kingsbane on cooldown right after Deathmark → Envenom at 5+.
## Single target — Fatebound
Maintain Garrote (from Vanish before every Deathmark) → Rupture at 5+ → Deathmark → Kingsbane (synced) → Envenom at 5+ → Ambush#8676 when usable → Mutilate → Fan of Knives at 2+ → Thistle Tea when low (auto is fine).
Shiv on cooldown for Fatebound: unclear — sims show −1% to +4.5% depending on gear and Feint use; not a rule.

## AoE
Garrote + Rupture on the main target → **Crimson Tempest to spread bleeds** (it's the builder until all targets have bleeds, or Scent of Blood is maxed) → Fan of Knives at 2+ → Vanish → Garrote (M+: save it for chain pulls; raid: with Deathmark) →
Deathmark (worth it in AoE too: Finish the Job#1249809, Lethal Dose#381640) → Envenom at 5+. Crimson Tempest is expensive — plan Energy for add spawns. Vanish again if the first Improved Garrotes are running out and the pack lives 20+ s.

## Openers
ST: from stealth Garrote → Mutilate to 5+ → Rupture → Garrote again (extends Improved Garrote; Fatebound) → Mutilate to 5+ → Envenom → trinkets/racials/potion → Deathmark → Kingsbane → Envenom.
Vanish at the same time as Deathmark, then refresh Garrote at the end of the 6 s Improved Garrote window.
AoE: (Vanish if not stealthed) → Garrote x2 → Fan of Knives → Rupture → Crimson Tempest to 5 CP / max Scent of Blood → Envenom → Deathmark → Kingsbane → Envenom → Crimson Tempest until all bleeds out → Fan of Knives → Envenom.
Pre-pull: Deadly Poison#2823 + Amplifying Poison#381664; Atrophic Poison#381637 in raid (Numbing/Crippling for M+ tanks).

## Cooldowns and mechanics
- Deathmark: duplicates poison applications (fast Kingsbane ramp) and boosts bleeds/poisons; use on a target that lives through it; can delay to line up with Zoldyck (below 35%) as long as no cast is lost.
- Kingsbane: 14 s DoT ramping with poison procs (no haste scaling) — 100% melee and Envenom uptime while it runs; use Evasion/Cloak to stay in.
- Improved Garrote snapshot is lost if you overwrite it with a normal Garrote → let it run (doubled in Deathmark).
- Pooling ~1.5%: start a new Envenom chain at high Energy; pool before adds or damage-amp phases. Finish the Job: burn Energy in cooldowns.
- Defensives: Feint#1966 (40% AoE, 20% with Elusiveness), Cloak of Shadows, Evasion, Cheat Death, Crimson Vial.
