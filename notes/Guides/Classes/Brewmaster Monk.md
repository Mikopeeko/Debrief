## The spec in one line
Build everything around **Blackout Kick#100784 on cooldown** (fixed 4 s, not hasted; 1 s GCD → "Blackout + 3"). Always keep Energy for Keg Smash#121253 on cooldown (Shuffle + Brew cooldown
reduction). Keep Shuffle#322120 up 100%, use Purifying Brew#119582 after spikes, Celestial Infusion often, Niuzao and Exploding Keg on cooldown.

## Choice nodes (both guides)
- **Celestial Infusion** (default; ~20-30% absorb of each hit for 16 s, easier to heal, pairs with Niuzao's Resolve) vs Celestial Brew (100% absorb for 8 s; for overwhelming single spikes).
- **Niuzao's Resolve**: healing spheres become a HoT that grows with missing health (smooth, never full); makes Expel Harm#322101 weaker. Default; experienced players in Mythic raid / high keys may prefer
  classic spheres + Expel Harm.
- Awakening Spirit (single target; small absorb vs Stagger ticks on Purify) vs Vital Flame (AoE; heals 50% of fire/nature damage) — **with 2/4-piece, Vital Flame always**.
- Special Delivery#196730 (passive, simpler, more damage; can break CC) vs Rushing Jade Wind#116847 (active, AoE threat; hasted, easy to drop).
- Dragonfire Brew#383994 (passive, better AoE) vs Charred Passions#386965 (single target, punishes missed Blackout Kicks). Scalding Brew#383698 requires Charred Passions.
- Shadowboxing Treads#387638 always, except Fluidity of Motion#387230 for MoH + Black Ox Brew + all of Bring Me Another + pure single target.

## Single target priority (Icy Veins; Wowhead same)
Black Ox Brew (off GCD, no waste) → Touch of Death#322109 if usable → Blackout Kick (Blackout Combo#196736) → (MoH) Chi Burst → Celestial Brew/Infusion if not needed for defense (MoH: at 2 charges or
full vitality) → Niuzao (only if combat lasts 25+ s) → Tiger Palm#100780 to spend Blackout Combo (and Potential Energy) → Keg Smash / Empty Barrel → Breath of Fire (Charred Passions) → Exploding Keg
(Rushing Jade Wind up first; recast with Empty the Cellar#1262329) → (Shado-Pan) Chi Burst → Keg Smash → spare Purifying Brew if taking little damage (Special Delivery / Barrel chance) → Rushing Jade Wind →
Tiger Palm (keep Energy for the next Keg Smash) / Spinning Crane Kick#322729 (not twice in a row — Press the Advantage).
- Spend Blackout Combo on **Tiger Palm**, rarely Keg Smash. Use non-combo abilities (Breath of Fire, SCK, RJW, Chi Burst, cooldowns) between to keep the combo for Tiger Palm.
- Tier: space Keg Smashes ~4 s apart for Scorched uptime (refreshes, doesn't stack) — but always keep one charge on cooldown. Breath of Fire before every pull.
- Niuzao: fit as many Breath of Fire and Blackout Kicks inside its 25 s (Stomps); Mighty Stomp#322740 recast.
- AoE: same list, Niuzao first, Breath of Fire / Keg Smash during Niuzao; Shado-Pan still spends spare Energy on Tiger Palm; MoH almost never SCK.

## Stagger and brews
- Stagger#115069 splits each hit into instant + a 10 s DoT (15 s with Bob and Weave#280515); magic is staggered at 58% of physical. Shuffle multiplies Stagger hugely (one example: 42% → 64%).
  Light/Moderate/Heavy = <30% / 30-59% / 60%+ of max health staggered. Heavy is OK if healing keeps up.
- **Purifying Brew** (2 charges, 20 s): removes 50% of the pool — earlier after a spike = more prevented. Rules: never sit on 2 charges; use one right after a big spike; use one if the second is about to come back.
- Keg Smash −3 s and Tiger Palm −1 s on all Brews (Purifying, Celestial, Fortifying, Black Ox). Shuffle: Keg Smash +5 s, Blackout Kick +3 s, SCK up to +4 s, max 15 s — dropping it is catastrophic.
- Healing spheres: one per 100% max health of raw damage taken (before Stagger), last 30 s, max 5; Expel Harm (15 Energy, 5 s) pulls them all.
- Fortifying Brew#115203 (6 min base, effectively ~2-3 with Brew reduction): health + DR + guaranteed Barrel; use proactively; Expeditious Fortification#388813 −2 min (lose Ironshell Brew#388814).
- Touch of Death: on enemies below your max health (or Improved: under 15% for 35% of your HP); with Chi Transfer it's also a self-heal/Stagger clear.
