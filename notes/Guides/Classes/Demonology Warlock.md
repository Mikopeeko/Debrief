# Demonology Warlock (ranged DPS, pets)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Midnight removed Soul Strike, Bilescourge Bombers, Demonic Strength, Grimoire: Felguard, Fel Sunder, Immutable Hatred, the Invocations; new Diabolic Oculi, Grimoire: Imp Lord#1276452 / Grimoire: Fel Ravager#1276467, Summon Doomguard#1276672,
Reign of Tyranny#1276748, Empowered Felstorm, Imp Gang Boss, etc.

## The spec in one line
Generate Soul Shards (Shadow Bolt#686 / Infernal Bolt#433891, Demonbolt#264178 with Demonic Core#267102), spend them on **Call Dreadstalkers#104316 first, then Hand of Gul'dan#105174** (Wild Imps), never sit at 5 or overcap
(no Demonbolt at 4 / Shadow Bolt at 5). **Summon Demonic Tyrant#265187** (main cooldown, 20 s; damage scales live with every demon out; extends Dreadstalkers with Reign of Tyranny) → fill its window with as many Hand of Gul'dans as possible
(Apex Dominion of Argus summons). Keep ~2 Demonic Cores banked going into Tyrant (max 4).

## Hero trees
- **Diabolist**: Diabolic Ritual cycle (Overlord, Mother of Chaos, Pit Lord), predictable burst, passive AoE, Soul-Etched Circles#428911 mobility, Infernal Vitality#429115; Wowhead marks **Best everywhere** (most played; Soul Harvester sims higher).
- **Soul Harvester**: ~3% better pure single target (Icy Veins recommended single target and Delves), ranged Tyrant, much weaker cleave; faster Dominion setups via Shadow of Death.
- Icy Veins: Diabolist for any AoE/M+; Soul Harvester for pure single target.
- Raid cleave swap (Wowhead): Practiced Rituals#1250375 → Dreadlash#264078 and Mark of Shatug#455449 → Mark of F'harg#455450.
- M+ (Wowhead): Vilefiend build (set and forget, best) or Doomguard build (spread Doom#460551 with Demonbolt, Hand of Gul'dan on Doomed mobs reduces Doomguard cooldown).
- Apex **Dominion of Argus#1276163**: Nether Portal-like window after Tyrant — each two Hand of Gul'dans summon an extra demon → rotation shifted from pre-Tyrant setup to post-Tyrant spending. Enter Tyrant with max shards and cores.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending / Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power, Residual Energy ≈ Overload.

## Single target
- Wowhead Diabolist: Power Siphon#264130 / Implosion#196277 → Call Dreadstalkers → Hand of Gul'dan / Ruination#434635 → Grimoire (Imp Lord / Fel Ravager) → Demonic Tyrant → Doomguard → Demonbolt with Demonic Core → Shadow Bolt / Infernal Bolt.
- Wowhead Soul Harvester: Power Siphon → Call Dreadstalkers → Hand of Gul'dan → Tyrant → Doomguard → Demonbolt (Core) → Shadow Bolt.
- Icy Veins: Grimoire → Tyrant → Doomguard → Power Siphon with ≤2 Cores and 2+ imps → Implosion at 6 imps → Call Dreadstalkers whenever ready → Hand of Gul'dan at 4-5 shards → Demonbolt with 2+ Cores → Infernal Bolt at ≤2 shards → Shadow Bolt.
- Have Dreadstalkers out (and on cooldown) before Tyrant so it extends them.
- Implosion (15 s, up to 6 imps): main-target damage scales with imps' remaining energy, secondary targets full; costs ~0.75 shards of GCD — **never worth it in single target without To Hell and Back** (then it is, for extra imps/Cores). Implode with 6 imps.
- Doom: deals damage when it expires or the target dies; each Hand of Gul'dan cuts it 3 s → hold 1 Core to re-apply after it pops; in AoE Demonbolt different (low-health) targets.

## AoE
Implosion → Doomguard (5-target cap) → Call Dreadstalkers → Hand of Gul'dan / Ruination → Tyrant → Demonbolt (Core; spread Doom) → filler. Cores can be spent more freely when imploding often.

## Openers
Wowhead Diabolist: Demonbolt ≥4 s before pull → Grimoire: Imp Lord → Call Dreadstalkers → Shadow Bolt → **Tyrant** → Hand of Gul'dan x2 → Shadow Bolt → Implosion → Demonbolt/Shadow Bolt → build to 3 shards and Hand of Gul'dan until Dominion of Argus ends.
Soul Harvester: Power Siphon ≥5 s before pull with 2 imps → Shadow Bolt → Imp Lord → Dreadstalkers → Tyrant → (Hand of Gul'dan → Demonbolt) x3.
Icy Veins: pre-cast Demonbolt x2 / Power Siphon / Shadow Bolt → Doomguard → Grimoires → Shadow Bolts → Dreadstalkers → Tyrant → max Hand of Gul'dans → Implosion at 6 imps → Power Siphon after the second Hand.
Pre-pull: Felguard, Soulwell, portals.

## Cooldowns
- Grimoire: Imp Lord (self-dispel, Singe Magic while on cooldown) or Fel Ravager (on-demand interrupt + Spell Lock while on cooldown) — 2 min.
- Felstorm#89751 autocast off in pure single target is a tiny gain (min-max only).

## Tier set (Season 2)
2-piece Wild Imps +10%, Implosion +20%; 4-piece imps can self-implode at end of life. Passive.

## Stats
- Wowhead: Int > Haste = Crit > Mastery > Vers.
- Icy Veins: item level > **Haste to 22%** > Crit > Mastery > Haste > Vers. Mastery: Master Demonologist (demon damage; better in AoE).

## Disagreements
