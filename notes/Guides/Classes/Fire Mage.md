# Fire Mage (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): tuning moved to filler time between Combustions; Improved Warding#1297073 new; Improved Blazing Barrier#321708 gains a HoT and 2nd charge. Tier set **requires Pyroclasm#269650** and hard-cast Pyroblasts.

## The spec in one line
Crits give Heating Up#48107 → Fire Blast#108853 converts it to Hot Streak!#48108 → instant Pyroblast#11366 (Flamestrike#2120 at 4+). Cast Fireball#133/Frostfire Bolt into a queued Pyroblast so both land together
("double Pyroblast"). Combustion#190319 (1 min with Kindling) makes everything crit — cast it at the end of a hard-cast. Never sit on capped Fire Blast charges or waste Hot Streak. Always be casting.

## Hero trees
- **Sunfury in all content** (both guides): Arcane Phoenix during Combustion; after it ends **Hyperthermia#383874** (free, crit, ramping Pyroblasts/Flamestrikes — still convert Heating Up with Fire Blast for bigger Ignite) +
  Ashes of Inspiration haste; Scorch#2948 for mobility (Icy Veins: run Scorch in almost all cases). Better adapted to Pyroclasm.
- Frostfire: Frostfire Bolt#431044 (shorter cast), Frostfire Empowerment instant AoE bolt, stronger barrier/execute/slows; Wowhead delves "Best". Scorch is only for movement as Frostfire.
- Talents: Firestarter#205026 (crits above 90% health) — if Combustion goes on the pull, swap it for Critical Mass#117216. Fuel the Fire required for Flamestrike to be worth it. Time Walk#1244087 vs Temporal Realignment#1244090.
  Burnout#1271177 (Ignites explode for 75% of remaining when Combustion ends). Blast Zone → Meteor#153561 30 s.
- Apex **Fired Up#1257348**: spending Hot Streak can give a stacking fire damage buff; each proc −2.5 s/pt Fire Blast cooldown (−5 s); Combustion makes the first ~3 procs near-guaranteed and each extends Combustion 1 s.
  → you rarely run out of Fire Blast in Combustion; hard-casting there risks capping Fire Blast.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Void-Touched Orbs, Void-Tainted Shell (group) / Self-Mending (solo), Lingering, Burning Haste, Echoes.

## Combustion priority (Icy Veins; Sunfury)
Meteor timed to land before Combustion ends (with Burnout: in the last 2-8 s) → Pyroblast with Hot Streak → hard-cast Pyroblast with Pyroclasm only if ≥3 s left **and** you're at ≤1 Fire Blast charge (never cap Fire Blast for it) →
Scorch with Heat Shimmer and no Hot Streak → Fire Blast to convert Heating Up → Scorch/Fireball when out of Fire Blast. Then Hyperthermia: spam Pyroblast.
Once a Pyroclasm hard-cast starts, finish it (use Shimmer to move).
## Outside Combustion
Firestarter: delay the first Combustion until the target is under 90% (~18 s; if the Firestarter window lasts >30 s, Combust on pull); otherwise still ~8-10 s into the pull to pre-stack.
Combustion when ready, entered at the end of a hard-cast (Pyroclasm Pyroblast > Scorch under 30% > Fireball/Frostfire Bolt) → Meteor as soon as available (skip if delayed >10 s; save for Combustion) →
Pyroblast with Hot Streak queued as a Fireball lands → hard-cast Pyroblast on Pyroclasm (tier: always crits — use them freely outside Combustion; enter Combustion without unused procs) → Scorch with Heat Shimmer →
Scorch under 30% → Fireball/Frostfire Bolt filler → Scorch on the move.
Wowhead Frostfire: bank one Pyroclasm going into Combustion; Sunfury: Meteor → Pyroclasm (Sunfury Execution) → hard-cast into Combustion.

## AoE
Flamestrike replaces Pyroblast at **4+ targets** (both in and out of Combustion); never Flamestrike above 90% with Firestarter; never replace Fireball with Flamestrike; keep Pyroblast if focusing a priority target.
Bug: a hard-cast Flamestrike queued into an instant Flamestrike doesn't return Hot Streak → don't secure Hot Streak with Fire Blast during a Pyroclasm Flamestrike hard-cast. Cursor macro option.

## Openers
Precast Pyroblast → Fireball (Fire Blast during it) → Pyroblast x2 → alternate Fire Blast/Pyroblast while above 90% → at 90%: (Meteor) → Pyroblast hard-cast (or Scorch) with Fire Blast during it → Combustion right before the cast ends →
Pyroblast x2 → Combustion priority. Pre-pull: Arcane Intellect, Blazing Barrier#235313, Mirror Image for threat.

## Cooldowns
- Combustion: every 1 min — delay only for Time Warp/vulnerability/adds (easy to lose a use).
- Meteor: one between Combustions with Blast Zone; use immediately so timing doesn't slip.
- Mastery: Ignite#12846 banks damage; Fire Blast spreads Ignite to a nearby target without one.
- Defensives: Blazing Barrier, Cauterize#86949 cheat death, Alter Time (+ Time Walk emergency Shimmer trick).

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: Pyroclasm Pyroblast/Flamestrike always crit.
- 4-piece: Pyroclasm casts 20% faster and +20% Pyroclasm damage.
