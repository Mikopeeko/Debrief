12.1 (Wowhead): San'layn nerfs (Frenzied Bloodthirst#434075 5%/stack, Visceral Strength#434157 10%); buffs to Marrowrend#195182, Heart Strike#206930/Vampiric Strike#433895,
Reaper's Mark#439843 and Exterminate#441378 (primary target); defensive buffs (Rapid Decomposition#255203, Permafrost#207200, Umbilicus Eternus#391517, Sanguinary Burst#1263781,
Voracious#273953 15% leech); Bloodshot#391398 and Perseverance of the Ebon Blade#374747 now 4% damage reduction. Bug: Corrupted Blood#434574 buffed by 1400%. Icy Veins: an Echoing Fury bug.

## The spec in one line
Spend Runes to make Runic Power, spend Runic Power on Death Strike#49998 (heal after big damage, or above ~75 RP). Keep Bone Shield#195181 at 5+ stacks with as few Marrowrends
as possible, stand in Death and Decay#43265, never cap Blood Boil#50842 charges, and press Dancing Rune Weapon#49028 on cooldown.

## Hero trees
- **San'layn**: Wowhead "Raid (Best)" and Mythic+ (Best); Icy Veins "sensible default". Close to Deathbringer on pure single target; pulls far ahead with a second target or AoE.
  Dancing Rune Weapon gives Gift of the San'layn#434152 (Vampiric Strike always available, Essence of the Blood Queen#433925 tripled). Between windows you must keep Essence of the
  Blood Queen stacks via rare Vampiric Strike procs from Death Strike. Harder Bone Shield management (no hero help). Punishing in M+ and miserable in delves.
- **Deathbringer**: easier, more defensive (near-permanent 7.5% damage reduction, double Blood Plague#55078 healing, painless Bone Shield via Exterminate). Wowhead M+ (Best) and delves.
  Reaper's Mark (45 s; 30 s with talent) tallies shadow damage and detonates.
- Apex **Dance of Midnight**: spending Runes randomly spawns extra Dancing Rune Weapons (~50% uptime on at least one); they don't trigger Dancing Rune Weapon talents.
  Occasionally empowers the next Heart Strike. Parries during Dancing Rune Weapon also proc it.
- Raid flex points (Wowhead): March of Darkness#391546, Subduing Grasp#454822 (fight utility), Sanguinary Burst → Deadly Reach#1264235 (adds) or Bloodworms#195679.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload.

## Opener
- Deathbringer: Death's Caress#195292 → pre-placed Death and Decay (while it flies; don't have a target selected so a Dance proc doesn't aggro early) → Reaper's Mark (+ Raise Dead#46585)
  → Dancing Rune Weapon → Blood Boil → Marrowrend (spend an Exterminate) → Death Strike. **Always Reaper's Mark before Dancing Rune Weapon** (they line up every 90 s).
- San'layn: Death's Caress → Dancing Rune Weapon (+ Raise Dead) → Blood Boil (pandemic plague + 2 rune-weapon copies) → Vampiric Strike x2 → Death Strike.

## Priority — Deathbringer (Wowhead + Icy Veins)
1. Death Strike if you need healing or are above 75 RP (two Death Strikes banked; 45 RP from capping).
2. Keep 5+ Bone Shield; Marrowrend if it's about to expire. Exterminate charges about to expire (<3 s) → Marrowrend.
3. Death and Decay if its buff isn't up, or with a Crimson Scourge#81136 proc. Pre-cast it before Reaper's Mark (biggest source of mark stacks).
4. Reaper's Mark on cooldown → Dancing Rune Weapon on cooldown.
5. Blood Boil during Dancing Rune Weapon if the target lacks Blood Plague, or about to cap charges; with Boiling Point#1265790 proc.
6. Consumption at rank 1 on cooldown when Dancing Rune Weapon isn't up.
7. Exterminate: single target = low-priority filler in or out of DRW; AoE = use outside DRW.
8. Heart Strike filler — keep 3 Runes recharging; don't run out of Runes as Reaper's Mark comes back (45 s).

## Priority — San'layn (Icy Veins)
**During Gift of the San'layn**: Death Strike if about to die, >70 RP, or Coagulopathy#391481 about to fade → one Blood Boil (apply the rune-weapon plague copies, pandemic) →
Blood Boil at 5+ targets → Death and Decay with Crimson Scourge (if Visceral Strength isn't up) → keep 1+ Bone Shield → Vampiric Strike filler.
**Outside**: Dancing Rune Weapon on cooldown → Death and Decay (Crimson Scourge or 4+ targets) → Vampiric Strike when available → Consumption rank 1 (AoE: rank 3 right as DRW ends)
→ Blood Boil with Boiling Point → Death Grip#49576 / Death's Caress if Bone Shield about to expire → Marrowrend at ≤5 stacks ideally during an Apex rune weapon (bonus stacks) →
Blood Boil (don't cap) → Heart Strike.

## Bone Shield (both)
Marrowrend = 3 stacks (+3 per active rune weapon), Death's Caress = 2, max 10, 30 s duration, 2.5 s internal cooldown on losing charges. Ossuary#219786 at 5+ makes Death Strike cheaper.
Icy Veins: Marrowrend at 7 or fewer (unless you need Heart Strike's RP). Death Grip can refresh the whole stack at 8+ if taunting is fine. Avoid too many Marrowrends inside DRW (instant cap).

## Cooldowns (both)
- Dancing Rune Weapon: parry + copies Heart Strike/Marrowrend/Blood Boil/Death Strike; mainly a **resource and damage** cooldown — never hold it more than ~15 s for the parry.
- Vampiric Blood#55233: +30% max health and healing taken, 10 s — plan it for specific hits (doesn't raise the Blood Shield cap; makes a following Anti-Magic Shell bigger).
- Icebound Fortitude#48792: −30% for 8 s + stun immunity — save for raw-damage checks (Blood is weak against hits that count damage before absorbs).
- Anti-Magic Shell#48707: fully absorbing a magic hit stops its debuff; time it so other magic doesn't eat it first. Anti-Magic Zone#51052 is also 15% magic reduction for you.
- Lichborne#49039: 15% leech + charm/fear/sleep immunity. Purgatory#114556: uncapped cheat death.
- Threat (Icy Veins): tanks have +950% threat; 110% to pull aggro in melee, 130% at range; taunt = 6 s fixate, copies top threat, +800% threat; repeated taunts get shorter.
- If the cooldown plan isn't enough: ask for an external (Blessing of Sacrifice), a defensive trinket (e.g. Rotting Globule absorb), or Versatility jewelry as a last resort.

## Mastery: Blood Shield#77513
Absorb from Death Strike healing, capped at 50% max health (ignores Vampiric Blood); physical only. Easy to cap → not worth stacking.

## Tier set (Season 2 — "Baleful Grave-Knight's Lament")
- 2-piece: each Death Strike = Blood Debt stack (+0.5% Strength, up to 10, permanent); at 10 your next Marrowrend consumes them for +10% Strength for 10 s.
- 4-piece: that Marrowrend also gives 3 extra Bone Shield and deals big AoE damage.

## Stats
- San'layn: Strength > Haste (to ~30% unbuffed, Icy Veins) > Crit > Mastery > Vers (Wowhead: Haste > Mastery/Crit/Vers).
- Deathbringer: Strength > Crit > Mastery > Vers > Haste (Wowhead: Crit > Mastery/Vers > Haste).
- Crit = parry via Riposte#161797. Sims can't value defense — cooldown planning comes first.
