# Elemental Shaman (ranged DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
12.1 (Wowhead): Power of the Maelstrom#191861 redesigned (Lightning Bolt / Chain Lightning 15% → next Lava Burst +20%, stacks 2); Stormkeeper#191634 no longer guarantees a Lightning Bolt overload;
Chain Lightning#188443 and Flame Shock#470411 +60%; Tempest#454009 direct −20%; Ascendance#114050 overload bonus 30% (was 75%), bonus Lava Bursts at 50%; Molten Wrath 10%; Apex Feedback Loop rank 1 = overloads +10% and Elemental +10%,
rank 2 crit damage 15/30%; Fusion of Elements Elemental Blast buffs 40% duration; Farseer Ancestors' Chain Lightning hits 5, Ancestors' Lava Burst scales with crit but −20%; Supercharge#455110 = overloads +10%.

## The spec in one line
Keep Flame Shock up (Lava Burst#51505 always crits on it, +10% via Inferno Arc, Lava Surge#77756 procs), build Maelstrom with Lava Burst / Lightning Bolt#188196 (Chain Lightning at 2+), spend it on
Elemental Blast#117014 or Earth Shock#8042 (Earthquake#61882 in AoE) **without overcapping**, use Master of the Elements#16166 (from Lava Burst) on your biggest spell, line Stormkeeper → Ancestral Swiftness → Ascendance up together.
Always be casting; plan movement (Spiritwalker's Grace#79206, Nature's Swiftness#378081, instant Flame Shocks).

## Hero trees
- **Farseer in all content** (both guides: "uncontested"): Ancestors from Stormkeeper / Ancestral Swiftness#443454 copy your casts (Lava Burst for single-target casts, Chain Lightning for AoE casts; spells that apply Flame Shock make each Ancestor
  Lava Burst every target); easier, more mobile, better defense.
- Stormbringer: Tempest, more complex, less mobile, behind everywhere.
- Raid (Icy Veins): Elemental Blast build is much stronger; Earth Shock build "only" ~5% loss but more mobile. Area-targeted Earthquake with a @cursor macro.
- Apex **Feedback Loop#1270062**: passive (more overload damage, spell crit, crit damage, extra overload chance) — mandatory.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload.

## Single target — Farseer
Wowhead: Stormkeeper on cooldown → Ancestral Swiftness on cooldown → Ascendance (roughly on cooldown, always after Stormkeeper) → **Elemental Blast** with Overcharge!#1300222 (tier) / within 15 of cap / with Master of the Elements / whenever possible
(Earth Shock the same if that build) → Lava Burst (without MotE first) → Lightning Bolt to spend MotE → refresh Flame Shock (or Voltaic Blaze#470057) in pandemic without MotE up → Lightning Bolt filler.
Icy Veins: Stormkeeper (hold ≤10 s to cast 1-2 GCDs before Ascendance) → Ancestral Swiftness → Ascendance with trinkets, Bloodlust, Light's Potential → refresh Flame Shock if Fire Elemental fades within 3 s →
Flame Shock / Voltaic Blaze at ≤6 s (use for movement) → spender with both 4-piece buffs (Flowing Elements + Overcharge!) → Lava Burst if it won't overcap and MotE isn't up → Stormkeeper Lightning Bolt with MotE →
Elemental Blast (MotE or near cap) / Earth Shock → Lava Burst → Lightning Bolt.
## Stormbringer (Wowhead)
Stormkeeper → Flame Shock / Voltaic Blaze refresh (pandemic) → Lava Burst without MotE / with Lava Surge if no overcap → Tempest with MotE → Stormkeeper Lightning Bolt with MotE → Elemental Blast → Earth Shock → Lightning Bolt/Tempest.
Icy Veins (Stormbringer lists): **never hard-cast Lava Burst** — only on Lava Surge.

## AoE (2+ → Chain Lightning replaces Lightning Bolt)
- Farseer (Wowhead): Stormkeeper → Ancestral Swiftness → Earthquake at 3+ → Elemental Blast at 2 → Earthquake → Lava Burst with Lava Surge + Purging Flames#1259471 → Voltaic Blaze on cooldown → Chain Lightning.
- Icy Veins details: 4+ targets — Voltaic Blaze on cooldown, Elemental Blast on a target without Lightning Rod when no Elemental Blast buff, Earthquake (stack them — they add up), Chain Lightning with Stormkeeper, Lava Burst for Purging Flames.
  Earthquake vs Elemental Blast: with Ancestors up, Earthquake from 2 stacked targets (Earth Shock build); with Elemental Blast, Blast at ≤5 targets when no Ancestors are up, never at 6+. Exactly 2 targets: Flame Shock both (re-apply both after Ascendance before Fire Elemental fades).
- Burst order (Farseer AoE): **Stormkeeper → Ancestral Swiftness → Voltaic Blaze → Ascendance** (Voltaic Blaze outside Ascendance).
- Purging Flames trick: Lava Burst echoes to up to 5 *other* Flame-Shocked targets — Lava Burst an un-shocked target if that gains an echo (min-max only).

## Openers
ST: Stormkeeper ~3 s before pull → Lava Burst ~1.5 s before (lands on pull) → Ancestral Swiftness → Ascendance + trinkets + Light's Potential → Lava Burst / Lightning Bolt x2 (Elemental Blast as early as possible).
AoE: Stormkeeper → Ancestral Swiftness → Voltaic Blaze → rotation.
Pre-pull: Flametongue Weapon#318038 imbue (auto with Instinctive Imbuements#1270350), Skyfury#462854, Lightning Shield#192106 (Lightning Capacitor) / Earth Shield#974.

## Cooldowns
- Stormkeeper: next 2 Lightning Bolts +150% and an extra overload; Chain Lightning an extra overload per target; spawns an Ancestor (Farseer). With intermittent adds, hold it to Chain Lightning 5 targets.
- Ascendance: Flame Shock on 6 targets, extra overload on everything (+30% overload damage); Call of Fire#378255 Fire Elemental#198067 → recast Flame Shock after Ascendance before the Elemental fades.
  Line up Lust, potion, trinkets, Purging Flames (Voltaic Blaze), Stormkeeper; check no movement/intermission; Elemental Blast right before.
- Procs follow "deck of cards" bad-luck protection (Tempest, etc.); Lava Surge is normalized — can't re-proc for ~3 s.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: stronger spenders.
- 4-piece: builder buff (Flowing Elements, stacks to 4) and free spender charge (Overcharge!, does **not** stack — spend it before it's overwritten); with both cooldowns up it only procs after both fade (bug-like). Purging Flames eats several builder stacks.

## Stats
- Wowhead: **Mastery to ~1200 rating** (72% first breakpoint; useless past 100% — ~86% cap with Elemental Blast, ~76% with the embellishment set) > Haste = Crit > Vers.
- Icy Veins: Int > Mastery > Haste > Crit > Vers (same caps).
