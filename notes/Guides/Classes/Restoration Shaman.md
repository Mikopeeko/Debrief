# Restoration Shaman (healer)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins, both patch 12.1.5. No 12.1.5 class changes found.
Only healer with an interrupt (Wind Shear) and the only healer without an external defensive.

## 12.1 changes (Wowhead)
Riptide#61295 instant +25%, HoT +100%. Unleash Life#73685 +100%. Healing Wave#77472 +10%. Healing Rain#73920 +20% and 6 targets.
Earthen Accord#1271104 now additive (Unleash Life buff 45%). Calm Waters removed (folded into Current Control#1253093); Wavespeaker's Blessing#381946 moved;
new Swelling Tides#1312843. Totemic: Surging Totem#444995 bonus 20→10%, Pulse Capacitor#445032 25→10%. Farseer: Healing Rain 12 s cooldown (new one
cancels old); ancestor Chain Heal#447433 +50%, 5 targets, 10% falloff, 20 yd jumps (+180% total); ancestor Chain Lightning 5 targets; ancestor Lava Burst scales with crit.

## Hero trees and builds
- **Totemic is the pick** (both guides; Wowhead marks Totemic "Best" for raid, M+ and delves). More forgiving on movement, more passive healing
  (Surging Totem, Healing Stream Totem applying Earthliving via Primal Catalyst#1260874, free instant Chain Heals from Lively Totems#445034).
  Weakness: spread-out groups (Healing Rain radius).
- Farseer: close in power, harder (Riptide charge flood, target selection, hard-casting). Better spot healing, stronger Riptide/Chain Heal.
  Icy Veins: good if still wearing the Season 1 tier set (it buffs Unleash Life). Farseer is forced into Unleash Life.
- Raid Totemic talent options (Wowhead): Unleash Life instead of Overflowing Shores#383222 (only if still on S1 tier or want more single target); Earthen Accord not worth it;
  Ancestral Vigor#207401 if the raid lead asks (drop Overflowing Shores / Coalescing Water#470076); Acid Rain#378443 for damage (drop Overflowing Shores);
  Downpour#462486 = high reward, high RNG with the tier rains — not default; First Ascendant#462440 instead of Preeminence#462443 if the big mechanic is every 2 min.
- Farseer: "gambling ancestors" (Ancient Fellowship#443423 + Routine Communication#443445) beats "strong ancestors" (Latent Wisdom#443449 + Heed My Call#443444)
  because more spawns = more Riptide cooldown via Offering from Beyond#443451.
- M+: Totemic, Healing Tide over Ascendance (flexible; Ascendance needs standing still).
- Apex **Stormstream Totem#1267068**: a proc that upgrades your next Healing Stream Totem (big first wave, more targets, +40% via the talent#1267093).
  Every Nature's/Ancestral Swiftness guarantees one; each cast Riptide has 6% (free Riptides from Primal Tide Core#382045 don't count). Buff lasts 60 s, stacks to 2,
  and refunds/adds a Healing Stream charge, so it never costs a cast.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, **Critical Power**, Overload.

## Raid priority (Wowhead, Totemic)
1. Use all Stormstream Totem procs.
2. Riptide on cooldown — on people who **don't already have it** (Undercurrent#382194, Deluge#200076, Flow of the Tides#382039, Tidewaters#462424 all reward many Riptides).
3. Nature's Swiftness#378081 (Farseer: Ancestral Swiftness#443454, 30 s) — **on cooldown** (it's where most Stormstreams come from). Cooldown starts when you consume it, so cast right after.
   Spend it on Chain Heal (free + instant).
4. (Farseer) Unleash Life. 5. Maintain Surging Totem (Farseer: Healing Rain) — place it to hit the most allies, move with Totemic Projection.
6. Healing Stream Totem#5394 on cooldown (Icy Veins: for emergencies and whenever at max charges).
7. Filler: **Chain Heal#1064** (much stronger, expensive) when 3+ are hurt and mana allows; **Healing Wave#77472** (cheap) for 1-2 targets or to save mana.
- Stormstreams can be held up to 60 s to line up with damage (2 stacks max); Farseer can keep refreshing with Swiftness. Totemic should spend them soon (holding costs Healing Stream casts).
- Icy Veins adds: Chain Heal on injured allies **with Riptide** (Flow of the Tides); DPS when nobody's in danger (Lightning Bolt is free).

## Ascendance sequence (Wowhead)
Before: 2 Tidal Waves#51564 stacks (spend Riptides), Unleash Life and Healing Rain/Surging Totem right before. In: one cast to use Unleash Life, THEN Swiftness
(not before, or you waste Unleash's cast-time cut); drop all Healing Stream and Stormstream totems (free instant Chain Heals, shorter GCD); then spam Chain Heal (3+ hurt)
or Healing Wave (single target).
Ascendance#114052: heals injured allies within 20 yd on cast (fixed amount split); Healing Wave always crits and heals a second ally for 50%; Chain Heal +3 jumps, 10%
falloff; both −25% mana. Healing Wave in Ascendance gives two Resurgence#16196 procs each cast → net mana gain. "One of the strongest healer cooldowns in the game."
Deeply Rooted Elements#378270: 7% per Riptide **application** to proc Ascendance (bad-luck protection).

## Cooldowns
- **Healing Tide Totem#108280** (choice node with Ascendance; for Totemic): pulses over 10 s, 40 yd; reduced past 5 targets. Totemic adds Splitstream#445035, a free Chain Heal, Earthliving.
  Pre-plan for the most dangerous raid moments.
- **Spirit Link Totem#98008**: damage reduction (10%; 15% + a heal 1 s in with Spouting Spirits#462383) and health sharing every second. Best **right before** a big hit.
  Also an emergency save (no real healing — it only moves health). Pairs with Darkness for more reduction (Icy Veins).
- Spiritwalker's Grace#79206: cast while moving; a cast started in its last second can be finished moving.
- Bloodlust usually at the start; Wind Rush Totem#192077 (5 s buff, totem lasts 15 s — return for more); Earth Elemental (Primordial Bond: +15% health as a defensive);
  Astral Shift (use with plenty of health left); Tremor Totem#8143 (before the fear); Capacitor Totem#192058; Purify Spirit (don't double-dispel); Purge#370 costs mana.

## Pre-pull (both guides)
Water Shield#52127, Earth Shield#974 on yourself + second Earth Shield on a tank (Icy Veins: tank), Earthliving Weapon#382021 (Instinctive Imbuements#1270350 /
Therazane's Resilience#1217622 = hourly). Skyfury#462854 on everyone. If damage at the start: Healing Rain + Riptides just before the pull.
Earth Shield only boosts the first Chain Heal hit on its target.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece (#1296629): Healing Wave and Chain Heal can spawn an 8 s Healing Rain at the target (uses your talents; can't be moved).
- 4-piece: every second one ally inside Healing Rain gets a 10 s absorb shield. Strongest on stacked fights.

## Mana
Keep mana % about equal to the boss's health % and end the fight near 0% (Icy Veins). Chain Heal vs Healing Wave is the lever.

## Stats
- Wowhead (both trees): Intellect > **Crit** > Haste = Versatility > Mastery. Mastery "historically bad this season" — can be worth lower ilvl to avoid.
- Icy Veins: ilvl > Crit > Versatility > Haste > Mastery. Haste great in M+/short fights; Mastery near Crit on progression (people lower).
- Mastery: Deep Healing#77226 = more healing on low-health targets.

## Consumables (Wowhead)
Flask of the Shattered Sun#241326. **Potion of Recklessness#241288 paired with Ascendance** (more healing + Resurgence mana beats a mana potion); Lightfused Mana Potion alternative.
Silvermoon Health Potion#241304. Earthliving Weapon (no oil). Feast Silvermoon Parade#255845 / Royal Roast#242275, Sanguithorn Tea.
Enchants: Acuity of the Ren'dorei#244029 weapon, Mark of the Magister#244003 chest, Eyes of the Eagle#243957 rings, shared rest. Gems: Telluric Eversong Diamond#240968 when
mana matters (needs one of each colour) else Indecipherable#240983 + Flawless Versatile Garnet#240909; Deadly Peridot/Lapis/Amethyst.

## Disagreements
- Stats: Wowhead Haste = Vers; Icy Veins Vers > Haste.
- Healing Stream Totem: Wowhead "on cooldown"; Icy Veins "for emergencies and at max charges". Both: never sit at max charges.
