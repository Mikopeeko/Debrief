# Outlaw Rogue (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Midnight: Crackshot and Underhanded Upper Hand removed; new Deadly Pursuit#1259612, Fast Action#1259480, **Preparation#1277933**. Roll the Bones#315508 reworked into **stages** (each includes the ones below):
1 = Sinister Strike +20% double-strike / Opportunity chance; 2 = Sinister Strike and Ambush +1 CP and +15%; 3 = Restless Blades#79096 +30%; 4 = +10% crit. Odds (Icy Veins): 55 / 30 / 10 / 5%.

## The spec in one line
Fast, high-APM builder/spender with no big cooldown: every finisher's combo points cut cooldowns (Restless Blades: 1 s per CP for Adrenaline Rush, Between the Eyes, Blade Flurry, Blade Rush, Keep It Rolling, Killing Spree, Roll the Bones).
Keep **Adrenaline Rush#13750, Between the Eyes#315341, Blade Rush#271877** (and Killing Spree for Trickster) on cooldown, spend Opportunity#195627 / Ace Up Your Sleeve#381828 procs, never overcap combo points, never sit at max Energy
(no pooling for Outlaw), **Blade Flurry#13877 on 2+ targets** (cleave comes from you — up to 5, 8 with talents). Downtime hurts it most.

## Hero trees
- **Trickster in all content** (both guides; ~5-7% ahead): Unseen Blade#441146 x4 → **Coup de Grace#441423** (Dispatch as if +5 CP — counts for cooldown reduction, use ASAP); Killing Spree#51690 (Disorienting Strikes, Flawless Form#441321);
  Nimble Flurry AoE scaling. Fatebound: coin flips (Hand of Fate#452536, Lucky Coin#1248970), Dispatch at 5+ CP instead of 6+; easier, fine for theme.
- Builds: ST vs AoE differ only in Blade Flurry talents. M+ (Wowhead): Sleight of Hand → Dancing Steel#272026, Grand Melee#1259469, Blade Rush; **Deft Maneuvers#381878** = Blade Flurry gives CP per target → cast Blade Flurry aggressively at low CP.
  Icy Veins: single-target builds skip Blade Rush.
- Apex **Gravedigger**: Between the Eyes can add an extra buff stack; more Dispatch damage; builds to a **free Between the Eyes** (no cooldown/cost) — track Palmed Bullets (6 stacks); it hits with travel time.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, Versatile Warrior, Overload.

## Single target / AoE — Trickster (both guides)
1. Blade Flurry if 2+ targets (at 2 targets, a high-priority builder at ≤5 CP).
2. Roll the Bones on cooldown if at **stage 1 or lower** (with Loaded Dice#256170: recast at stage 2 or lower).
3. **Keep It Rolling#381989 at stage 3+** (Icy Veins: stage 2+ is fine for consistency; hold it if Loaded Dice is about to come with the next Adrenaline Rush).
4. Preparation when Between the Eyes, Adrenaline Rush and Killing Spree (and Blade Rush in AoE) are all on cooldown — it doesn't get Restless Blades, so on short fights you can save it for a burn/add phase.
5. Adrenaline Rush on cooldown at ≤2 CP, then Killing Spree (at 6+ CP, with Supercharger active).
6. Blade Rush on cooldown (as a builder).
7. **Between the Eyes at 6+ CP** on cooldown.
8. Dispatch#2098 with the 4-piece active; Dispatch at 6+ CP (Coup de Grace ASAP).
9. Pistol Shot#185763 at 6 Opportunity#279876 stacks, or 3 stacks at 1-3 CP (Fan the Hammer: each Opportunity Pistol Shot = 4 CP; never overcap stacks — that matters more than CP).
10. Sinister Strike#193315 at ≤5 CP.
Fatebound (Wowhead): Keep It Rolling at stage 2+, Blade Rush, Dispatch at **5+**, Pistol Shot at 6 Opportunity.

## Openers
From stealth with Instant Poison: Adrenaline Rush + Roll the Bones + Slice and Dice#5171 right before the pull (SnD doesn't eat Supercharger) → Keep It Rolling if stage 3+ → (Blade Flurry if cleave) → Blade Rush or Sinister Strike + potion/racial/trinket →
build to 6+ CP (Sinister Strike, Pistol Shot with 3+ Fan the Hammer stacks) → Between the Eyes → Killing Spree at 6+ CP → Preparation if Between the Eyes is on cooldown (else spend BtE again first) → rotation.

## Cooldowns
- Adrenaline Rush (3 min base, much less with Restless Blades): huge Energy regen, max Energy, attack speed; GCD drops to 0.8 s with ~22-25% haste (Alacrity#193539 helps). Stack with Bloodlust; capping Energy in it is normal.
- Between the Eyes: main finisher, stacking damage buff per cast — keep it on cooldown.
- Keep It Rolling: +30 s to the current Roll the Bones; ~1:15 effective cooldown.

## Tier set (Season 2)
No rotation change (Icy Veins); Wowhead: Dispatch has priority while the 4-piece buff is active.

## Stats
- Wowhead: Agility (item level) > Haste (to ~23%) > Crit > Vers > Mastery.
- Icy Veins: Agility > **Crit to ~40%** > Haste to 25% (30% in raid single-target builds without Blade Rush) > Vers > Mastery. Mastery: Main Gauche#76806.

## Disagreements
- Crit vs Haste first (Wowhead Haste-first to ~23%; Icy Veins Crit to 40%, then Haste breakpoint).
- Keep It Rolling at stage 3 (Wowhead) vs stage 2+ for consistency (Icy Veins).

## What a log can check
Between the Eyes / Adrenaline Rush / Blade Rush / Killing Spree casts vs possible; finishers below 6 CP (5 for Fatebound); combo points overcapped by generators; Opportunity stacks overcapped; Roll the Bones recast at stage 2+ (wasted) or Keep It Rolling unused;
Blade Flurry uptime on 2+ targets; Coup de Grace used promptly; Energy capped outside Adrenaline Rush; melee uptime.
