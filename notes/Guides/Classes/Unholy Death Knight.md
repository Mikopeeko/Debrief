# Unholy Death Knight (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): Army of the Dead#42650 now lasts 30 s and no longer lets you summon more ghouls during it — instead your active ghouls cast Death Order / Epidemic Order;
Commander of the Dead#390259 no longer extends Lesser Ghouls via Mastery; Soul Reaper#343294 consumes up to 3 Lesser Ghoul stacks; Lord of the Dead#1256813 also summons a Lich at 3+ Magus.
With the tier set, Lord of the Dead is locked in as the capstone.

## The spec in one line
Burst cooldowns on cooldown, always aligned (Dark Transformation every 45 s, Army of the Dead every 90 s). Build Lesser Ghoul#1254252 stacks with Festering Strike#85948, spend them with Scourge Strike#55090,
spend Sudden Doom#49530 procs on Death Coil#47541 / Epidemic#207317 right away, and dump Putrefy#1247378 charges inside Dark Transformation.

## Hero trees and builds
- **San'layn** — Wowhead: "pretty much always better" (Single Target/Cleave Best, M+/Delves Best). Icy Veins rotation page marks San'layn "best choice" for raid and M+; its talent page says
  **Rider of the Apocalypse minion build for single-target raid**, San'layn pet build for M+. → Disagreement (see below).
  San'layn: 45 s cycle, Vampiric Strike#433895 during Dark Transformation, keep Essence of the Blood Queen between windows (one proc bridges the 10-25 s gap — spend Runic Power to fish), Blood Beast,
  Blightfall#1271974 plague builds. Huge self-healing.
- Rider of the Apocalypse: 90 s windows, easier, Death Charge mobility, Horsemen; stay in Mograine's Death and Decay (+5%), burst Trollbane's Chains of Ice. Wowhead: open-world Best.
- Apex **Forbidden Knowledge**: Army of the Dead gives 30 s where Death Coil → **Necrotic Coil#1242174** (adds AoE) and Epidemic → **Graveyard#383269**; ranks 2-3: Putrefy gives Mastery + a Lesser Ghoul stack;
  rank 4: Dread Plague has 20% to trigger a full Putrefy. Needs high Dread Plague and Festering Scythe#455397 uptime, and **pets on assist**.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Critical Power, Overload.
- Pre-pull: Raise Dead#46584 (Timmy). Runeforge Rune of the Apocalypse#327082.

## Cooldown timings (Icy Veins, raid)
0:00 Army + Dark Transformation, 0:45 DT, 1:30 Army + DT, 2:15 DT, 3:00 Army + DT, 3:45 DT … Any delay pushes the 90 s window. Macro Dark Transformation (off GCD) with Army.
Enter Dark Transformation with high Lesser Ghoul stacks (6-8) and start it with 2x Putrefy. Don't spend Putrefy charges between windows (Commander of the Dead buff is in DT). Trinkets/potion/Power Infusion with Army.

## Single target priority
Wowhead (outside Forbidden Knowledge): Outbreak#77575 if Virulent Plague#191587 is down → Festering Strike before DT if ≤3 stacks → Soul Reaper under 35% → Blightfall just before your trinket ends or ~15 s
into DT → Army on cooldown → Dark Transformation#1233448 on cooldown → Soul Reaper ~7 s after DT → Putrefy if DT is up → Festering Scythe if its buff is about to expire → Death Coil with Sudden Doom →
Festering Strike at 0 stacks → Scourge Strike with stacks → Death Coil.
During Forbidden Knowledge: Putrefy on cooldown → Necrotic Coil → Scourge Strike / Vampiric Strike with stacks.
Icy Veins adds: Blightfall just before Soul Reaper (or DT) ends → Putrefy; Death Coil also when San'layn and 50+ RP; keep **2-4 Lesser Ghoul stacks** (3+ in execute and before DT, for full Soul Reaper);
Necrotic Coil as soon as you have 30 RP (or Sudden Doom + 15). Death and Decay not worth it in single target.

## AoE
Wowhead: Festering Strike before DT if ≤5 stacks → Army → DT → Soul Reaper → Death and Decay if they'll stay in it → Epidemic with Sudden Doom → Epidemic. In FK: Putrefy, Soul Reaper, Graveyard.
Icy Veins: Epidemic over Death Coil at 4+ targets (6+ during Forbidden Knowledge); Death and Decay at 3+ (Cycle of Death → Putrefy cooldown); 4-6 Lesser Ghoul stacks; snipe low mobs with Soul Reaper.
(Wowhead says swap to Epidemic at 3+ in M+ builds; Icy Veins single-target page also says 3+ / 4+ in FK.)

## Openers
- Wowhead: Outbreak → Festering Strike → Festering Scythe → Army → Dark Transformation + potion + trinket → Putrefy → Necrotic Coil x2 (San'layn: Vampiric Strikes, Necrotic Coil x3) → Putrefy → Soul Reaper ~7 s after DT.
- Icy Veins ST: Outbreak → Festering Strike x2 → Army + DT + trinket + racial + potion → Putrefy → Death Coil x2 → Putrefy → Soul Reaper 6 s after DT → Blightfall just before Soul Reaper's buff ends.

## Other mechanics
- Virulent Plague (AoE) and Dread Plague (one target; source of Sudden Doom and Forbidden Knowledge procs) via Outbreak or Putrefy with Blightburst#1254552.
- Festering Scythe buff speeds plague ticks → more procs: keep it up always.
- Blightfall (DT turns into it): consumes all plagues for their remaining damage — use near the end of DT with buffs up.
- Runic Corruption: nothing to play around. Anti-Magic Shell: absorbs magic and gives Runic Power — time it on raid damage (defense first on progression).
- Gargoyle (with Army): ramps with Runic Power spent → spend RP while it's out. Abomination: +20% minion damage cloud.

- Icy Veins: Crit > Mastery > Haste > Vers. Pets inherit your stats (0-5 s delay). Mastery: Dreadblade#77515 boosts shadow/minion damage and their crit damage.
