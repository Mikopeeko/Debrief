# Retribution Paladin (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): damage moved from cooldowns to sustained — Avenging Wrath#31884 down to 15% crit chance and damage; all main generators/spenders buffed; Crusading Strikes#404542 now raises attack speed but no
longer gives extra Holy Power with Skyfury#462854; **Art of War#406064 stacks to 2**.

## The spec in one line
Generate Holy Power (Blade of Justice#184575, Judgment#20271, Hammer of Wrath#24275, Wake of Ashes#255937, Divine Toll#375576, Crusading Strikes passive) and spend it on Final Verdict#383328 (single target) /
Divine Storm#53385 (2+ targets). **Never waste Holy Power** (don't generate at 5). Line up Avenging Wrath + Execution Sentence#343527 + Wake of Ashes (30 s / 1 min cooldowns that sync naturally).
Spend Art of War at 2 stacks (Apex) and Light Within#1261159 before it's wasted. Easy to learn, low ceiling, forgiving.

## Hero trees
- Both close. **Templar**: Hammer of Light#427453 after Wake of Ashes (3 Holy Power) or free at 50 Light's Deliverance#425518 stacks; Empyrean Hammer#431398 passive; better short burst (ST and AoE) and defense.
- **Herald of the Sun**: Dawnlight#431377 DoTs from Wake of Ashes, stronger Divine Storm / Hammer of Wrath (Walk Into Light), better sustained cleave and longer-window burst, better with Power Infusion.
  **Icy Veins default for raid and M+** ("sustained cleave in The Venomous Abyss"); Wowhead lists both as Best; Icy Veins Delves = Templar.
- Crusading Strikes over Templar Strikes (Icy Veins: significantly stronger).
- Builds (Wowhead): raid = all single-target picks; tier makes Tempest of the Lightbringer#383396 worth it on single target; Templar passive cleave: Divine Exaction#1260429 + Quickened Invocation#379391.
  M+: Blade of Vengeance#403826 + Blessed Champion#403010 (Templar also Tempest) instead of Heart of the Crusader#406154 x2 / Judgment of Justice; Sanctify#382536 over Jurisdiction for more AoE;
  Radiant Glory#458359 instead of Execution Sentence keeps cooldowns from desyncing (Avenging Wrath then triggers from Wake of Ashes — cast Wake inside Execution Sentence).
- Apex **Light Within**: Art of War / Righteous Cause empower the next Blade of Justice (bonus + AoE); spenders hit harder in Avenging Wrath. Blade of Justice at 2 Art of War **even at 4 Holy Power** (spend first at 5).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Masterful Cunning, Overload.

## Single target — Templar (both guides)
Avenging Wrath → Execution Sentence → Hammer of Light right after Wake of Ashes → free Hammer of Light (Light's Deliverance) if: Avenging Wrath/Execution Sentence ends soon, or no Undisputed Ruling#432626 haste and Wrath is ≥5 s away,
or the proc/target is about to expire → Divine Storm with Divine Arbiter#1306161 (from Final Verdict) at 5 HP → Final Verdict at 5 HP → Wake of Ashes → Divine Toll → Blade of Justice at 2 Art of War →
Divine Storm with Divine Arbiter → Final Verdict → Blade of Justice → Hammer of Wrath → Judgment → (Crusader Strike).
Tier note (Wowhead): Templar with Seal of the Templar#1263252 shouldn't actively spend Divine Arbiter on Divine Storm in single target.
## Single target — Herald of the Sun
Avenging Wrath → Execution Sentence → Final Verdict at 5 HP → Wake of Ashes → Divine Toll → Blade of Justice at 2 Art of War (outside Wrath) → **Hammer of Wrath at 2 charges** → Final Verdict → Hammer of Wrath → Blade of Justice → Judgment.

## AoE (2+ targets, all builds)
- Templar: Wrath → Execution Sentence → Final Verdict with Divine Arbiter (from Divine Storm) at 5 HP → Divine Storm at 5 → Wake → Divine Toll → Final Verdict with Divine Arbiter → Divine Storm → Blade → Hammer of Wrath → Judgment.
  Spend Divine Arbiter with Final Verdict only at 2-4 targets; at 5+ just keep Divine Storming.
- Herald: Wrath → Execution Sentence → Final Verdict with Empyrean Legacy#387170 → Divine Storm at 5 → Wake → Divine Toll → Hammer of Wrath at 2 charges → Divine Storm → Hammer of Wrath → Blade → Judgment.
  Divine Arbiter → Final Verdict only at exactly 2 targets.
- Execution Sentence: everything done to targets hit by its first hit counts → keep AoE-ing for its 10 s.

## Openers
Templar: Blade of Justice → Avenging Wrath → Potion of Recklessness + trinkets → Execution Sentence → Wake of Ashes → Hammer of Light → Divine Toll.
Herald: Blade → Wrath → Execution Sentence → Wake → Final Verdict → Final Verdict (Divine Storm in AoE) → Divine Toll. Alternative: Wake before Execution Sentence.
Advanced (Wowhead, <1%): hold Execution Sentence until Crusade#1253598 is stacked (and Divine Resonance#384027 Hammers land inside it) — a loss if done badly.

## Cooldowns and trinkets
- Avenging Wrath (off GCD) first, before other cooldowns; turns Judgment into Hammer of Wrath. Crusade haste stacks don't need priority anymore.
- Stat on-use trinkets with Avenging Wrath (alternate two across cooldown sets); instant damage trinkets inside Wrath (crit chance only); don't hold Wrath 30 s to line up trinkets.
- Defensives: Divine Shield#642, Divine Protection#498; strong self/off-healing. Mobility: Divine Steed#190784.

## Tier set (Season 2)
Raises Divine Purpose proc rate; after consuming a proc the *other* spender hits harder → weave Divine Storm after a Divine Purpose spend on single target, Final Verdict on AoE (Icy Veins) — Wowhead calls this the Divine Arbiter proc.

## Stats
Both: Strength (item level) > **Mastery** > Haste > Crit > Vers. Mastery: Highlord's Judgment#267316 (all Holy, Holystrike and Radiant damage). Haste feeds Crusading Strikes Holy Power. No breakpoints.

Flask of the Magisters; Potion of Recklessness; Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion; Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Void-Touched Augment Rune; Royal Roast / Harandar Celebration.
Enchants: Rite of the Hash'ey, Rune of Avoidance, Amirdrassil's Grace, Mark of the Worldsoul, Forest Hunter's Armor Kit, Lynx's Dexterity, Eyes of the Eagle; Indecipherable Eversong Diamond + Masterful Peridot everywhere.

## Disagreements
- Hero default: Icy Veins defaults to Herald; Wowhead calls both Best (Templar burst, Herald sustained).
- Icy Veins says Templar has the single-target edge; Wowhead says both very competitive.

## What a log can check

Final Verdict vs Divine Storm by target count; Hammer of Wrath capped at 2 charges (Herald); trinket/potion inside Wrath.
