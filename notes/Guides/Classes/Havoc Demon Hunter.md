# Havoc Demon Hunter (melee DPS)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes (Icy Veins: follow-up Aldrachi Reaver buffs made both trees competitive by 12.1.5).
Season 2 (Wowhead): Dash of Chaos → Never Say Die#427794; Serrated Glaive#390154 now a 12 s buff on you; Trail of Ruin#258881 instant damage; Burning Hatred#320374 30 Fury/10 s; Blind Fury#203550 20/s;
Demon Blades#203555 10-16 Fury (more Fury from autos); Inertia#427640 18% → 12%.
Brings Chaos Brand#255260.

## The spec in one line
Keep Blade Dance#188499 / Eye Beam#198013 / Essence Break#258860 / Immolation Aura#258920 / The Hunt#370965 on cooldown, always keep Fury for Blade Dance and Eye Beam, spend the rest on Chaos Strike#162794.
Make sure Eye Beam and Blade Dance are **on cooldown before Metamorphosis** (it resets them).

## Hero trees
- **Aldrachi Reaver for raid** (Wowhead "Raid (Best)"; Icy Veins Raid/ST "best choice"): Wounded Quarry#442806 funnel into Reaver's Mark#442679; Reaver's Glaive#442294 every 6 Souls (replaces Throw Glaive);
  Thrill of the Fight#442686 should be 100% uptime. In AoE spend empowerments **Chaos Strike first, Blade Dance second** (Bladecraft#1272153). Hold Reaver's Glaive for adds to funnel. Mark stays on one target.
  Always use a Reaver's Glaive you're holding before The Hunt (The Hunt gives one).
- **Fel-Scarred for Mythic+** (both): Demonsurge#452402 explosions after any Metamorphosis entry (Annihilation#201427 + Death Sweep#210152; after the real Meta also Abyssal Gaze#452497 + Consuming Fire#452487).
  Spend every Demonsurge before re-entering. Wowhead raid alternatives: Fel-Scarred Cleave (mixed), 2t+ (drop Isolated Prey#388113 on Twin Fangs, Lost Explorers, Coiled Altar), Pure ST (Sszorak).
- Apex **Eternal Hunt#1270898**: The Hunt empowers the next Eye Beam (+100%, longer); −15 s The Hunt each of 2 points (→ 1 min) and +2 DoT targets; Blade Dance +20% and a full Eye Beam channel resets Blade Dance
  → two Death Sweeps per Demonic. Fel-Scarred: buff Abyssal Gaze (use The Hunt after Meta when Meta is ready). Wait for Eye Beam to be within ~10 s before The Hunt (12 s buff).
- **Exergy#206476** is the Season 2 pick (Vengeful Retreat#198793 / The Hunt give +5% for 20 s, up to 30 s — keep 100% uptime). Inertia = alternative (Vengeful Retreat → Felblade/Fel Rush right before each Eye Beam).
  Fel-Scarred Low Mover build drops Exergy + Tactical Retreat#389688.
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Self-Mending, Lingering, **Critical Power**, Overload.

## Single target — Fel-Scarred (Wowhead)
Spend all Immolation Aura charges before Meta → Metamorphosis#191427 once reset abilities are spent → The Hunt when Eye Beam is <10 s away → Vengeful Retreat within 1 GCD before Eye Beam →
Immolation Aura if capped → Death Sweep / Annihilation with Demonsurge → Eye Beam → Essence Break → Death Sweep → Blade Dance → Immolation Aura (≤100 Fury) → Annihilation → Felblade#213241 (≤100 Fury) →
Chaos Strike → Immolation Aura → Throw Glaive / Fel Rush filler. Overcapping Fury is fine — cooldowns first.
## Single target — Aldrachi Reaver (Wowhead)
The Hunt (if no Reaver's Glaive) → Metamorphosis → Reaver's Glaive → Annihilation if Rending Strike is up and Glaive Flurry spent → Eye Beam → Essence Break → Death Sweep → Annihilation → Blade Dance →
Chaos Strike → Felblade → Immolation Aura → Fel Rush. **Don't cast Throw Glaive** (Screaming Brutality#1220506 throws it for free with Blade Dance).
Icy Veins: Essence Break — if Eye Beam is ≤4 s away, hold and cast after (tier makes the window longer; don't cut it with Eye Beam). Meta only when Eye Beam and Death Sweep are both on cooldown.

## AoE (2+ targets)
Same lists. Fel-Scarred leans on Immolation Aura (Flamebound#452413, Ragefire#388107 — stay in range when it expires), never sit at 2 charges, never Meta with charges left (A Fire Inside#427775 refunds).
Aldrachi: Chaos Strike before Blade Dance with both empowerments up.

## Openers
- Fel-Scarred ST (Wowhead): Immolation Aura x2 3 s pre-pull → potion + on-use trinket (e.g. Voracious Heart of Ula'tek#270175) → Felblade → Eye Beam → The Hunt → Essence Break → Death Sweep x2 → Annihilation →
  Vengeful Retreat + Metamorphosis → Death Sweep → Consuming Fire → Annihilation → Abyssal Gaze. **Never macro trinkets/potion into Meta** (half the burst is before it).
- Aldrachi ST: Immolation Aura 2 s pre-pull → The Hunt → potion/trinket → Reaver's Glaive → Eye Beam → Essence Break → Death Sweep → Annihilation → Death Sweep → Meta (+VR) → Eye Beam → Death Sweep x2.

## Cooldowns
- Metamorphosis (2 min): +20% Haste, Annihilation/Death Sweep; Chaotic Transformation#388112 resets Blade Dance and Eye Beam (Fel-Scarred also Immolation Aura). Enter with lots of Fury.
- Eye Beam: Demonic#213410 5 s of Meta; Cycle of Hatred#258887 −2.5 s per cast up to 4 (→ 20 s); don't start a channel you can't finish (Eternal Hunt reset).
- Essence Break: on cooldown (4 s, 6 s with tier); fit as many Death Sweep/Annihilation as possible (4, or 5-6 with tier).
- The Hunt: 1 min with Eternal Hunt; Aldrachi: generates a Reaver's Glaive.
- Fury: always enough for Blade Dance and Eye Beam; Felblade for active Fury; Immolation Aura as backup; waiting for Demon Blades is normal.

## Tier set (Season 2 — "Abyssal Doomhound's Pursuit")

- 4-piece: Essence Break gets Cycle of Hatred, +35% initial hit, +2 s duration → Essence Break locked in; ideally inside Meta but don't hold long (desyncs from Eye Beam's 20 s).

## Stats
- Both guides: Agility > Crit > Mastery > Haste > Versatility (keep Vers low — Venomcursed items and Potion of Recklessness can't push it below 0).
- Know Your Enemy#388118 turns crit chance into crit damage (affects trinkets too).
