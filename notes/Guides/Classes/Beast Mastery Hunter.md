# Beast Mastery Hunter (ranged DPS, fully mobile)

Read Oct 19 2026. Wowhead + Icy Veins (12.1.5). No 12.1.5 class changes.
Season 2 (Wowhead): Bestial Wrath burst trimmed (Piercing Fangs#392053 now a plain Kill Command crit buff, Bloody Frenzy#407412 halved, Withering Fire#466990 Black Arrows 30%, Wild Instincts removed);
Heart of the Pack#1282747 plain pet-attack buff; Cobra Shot talents nerfed for the tier; Beast Cleave#115939 10 s / 55%; Kill Cleave 20%; Lethal Barbs#1264781 random; Through the Eyes#1277565 = Black Arrow damage;
Auto Shot#75 damage up massively.

## The spec in one line
Bestial Wrath#19574 (30 s) on cooldown, never let Kill Command#34026 or Barbed Shot#217200 reach 2 charges, buff Kill Commands with Nature's Ally#1273126 (never two Kill Commands back to back),
Cobra Shot#193455 as filler, and manage your pets.

## Hero trees
- **Pack Leader everywhere** (both guides; Icy Veins: "we do not recommend" Dark Ranger). Howl of the Pack Leader#471876 beasts (Bear bleed, Boar charge, Wyvern buff); first Kill Command after Bestial Wrath
  launches **Stampede!#472741** — a ~30 yd line from you that doesn't follow terrain/height → aim it through where the mobs are (and will be).
- Dark Ranger: Black Arrow#466930 (+ Bleak Powder cone behind the target → aim at the front of the pack), Wailing Arrow#392060 after Bestial Wrath (15 s window; use near the end of Withering Fire), Smoke Screen#430709 defense.
- Never take Solitary Companion#474746 (~10% loss vs Animal Companion#267116).
- Apex **Nature's Ally**: Bestial Wrath deals big damage (each extra pet hit triggers its own Beast Cleave); last point: weave a filler between Kill Commands to keep the 30% buff.
- Raid options (Wowhead): Roar of Sacrifice#53480 + Misdirection macro instead of Guardian's Hide; Emergency Salve#459517 on Coiled Altar and Ula'tek. Misdirection alone isn't worth it in M+ (65% of damage is pets).
- Omnium Folio (Icy Veins): Unleashed Fire, Void-Tainted Shell, Lingering, Masterful Cunning (flexible), Overload.

## Single target — Pack Leader
Wowhead: Barbed Shot if Bestial Wrath is ≤3 s away or about to hit 2 charges → **Bestial Wrath** → Kill Command with Howl of the Pack Leader or Nature's Ally → Cobra Shot with Cobra Fang#1299389 →
Barbed Shot → Cobra Shot.
Icy Veins adds: Wild Thrash#1264359 if 2+ targets; bank a Kill Command charge if Bestial Wrath is ≤3 s away (or Howl ≤4 s with one charge); Cobra Shot at 4 Cobra Fang (3 with Pack Leader, per the tier note);
Barbed Shot below 75 Focus or near 2 charges; don't Cobra Shot if Bestial Wrath is <1 GCD away.
## Single target — Dark Ranger
Barbed Shot before Bestial Wrath → Bestial Wrath → Kill Command with Nature's Ally → Black Arrow (during Withering Fire, first 10 s) → Barbed Shot → Wailing Arrow (when Withering Fire is ≤2 GCDs from ending) → Cobra Shot.

## AoE
Pack Leader: Wild Thrash right after Bestial Wrath / whenever Beast Cleave is down (hold if Bestial Wrath is ready before Beast Cleave expires) → Barbed Shot near 2 charges on the **priority target only** (don't multi-dot) →
Bestial Wrath with Beast Cleave up → Kill Command (ignore Nature's Ally at 4+ targets) → Cobra Shot with Cobra Fang + Beast Cleave → Barbed Shot → Cobra Shot. Keep pets in the middle of the pack.
M+ opener: Liquid Luster#271887 ~5-6 s before → Wild Thrash → Barbed Shot → Kill Command → Cobra Shot → Kill Command → Bestial Wrath → Wild Thrash (burst halfway through the potion).

## Opener (ST)
Hunter's Mark#259558 + Misdirection#34477 on the tank pre-pull → Barbed Shot x2 → potion/trinket/racial → Bestial Wrath (pet must be attacking!) → Kill Command → Cobra Shot (Dark Ranger: Black Arrow first).

## Cooldowns and items
- Bestial Wrath: +20% for 15 s, on cooldown always; plan 2 Kill Command charges going in, Nature's Ally on every Kill Command, pet on the priority target (Bloodshed#1272099 only applies to the pet's target).
  Potion and stat trinkets must be up **before** the Bestial Wrath hit (macro is fine). Hold it in M+ if the pack dies within ~12 s.
- Stat trinkets with Bestial Wrath (Voracious Heart of Ula'tek#270175, Vile Vial of Volatile Venom#273796, Tattered Amani War Banner#273797, Void Execution Mandate, Freightrunner's Flask); damage trinkets on cooldown
  (Ophidian Bone Whistle#274496, Spirit-Rending Poison#274497); Font of Venomous Rage#270168 outside Bestial Wrath. Stat racials with Bestial Wrath.
- Pets: autocast Claw/Bite/Smack on; Dash, Spirit Walk, Growl off; use Dash manually; Follow/Passive/dismiss if stuck. Aspect of the Turtle#186265 immunity.

## Tier set (Season 2)
- 2-piece: Barbed Shot causes an extra Stomp at 50%.
- 4-piece: each Stomp adds a Cobra Fang stack (max 4): +20% Cobra Shot per stack; with Beast Cleave the Cobra Shot cleaves at 30%. → Cobra Shot before Barbed Shot at 3 (Pack Leader) / 4 (Dark Ranger) stacks.

## Stats
- Wowhead: weapon damage > Agility > Mastery > Crit > Haste > Vers (Pack Leader all content; Dark Ranger ST swaps Crit first). Ula'tek gear is heavy Crit.
- Icy Veins: single target Mastery > Haste > Crit > Vers; M+ Mastery > Crit > Vers > Haste (Haste "worthless" in AoE; Crit > Haste as an all-round rule). Mastery: Master of Beasts#76657.
