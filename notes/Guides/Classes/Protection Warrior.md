## The spec in one line
Generate Rage (Shield Slam#23922 above all — react to resets), spend it on Shield Block#2565 first (keep it up while being hit) and Ignore Pain second, never cap Rage,
and use offensive cooldowns on cooldown.

## Single target priority (Icy Veins; Wowhead is the same in short)
Charge#100 in (taunt as you charge). Avatar#107574 on cooldown → Demoralizing Shout#1160 on cooldown (Booming Voice) → Champion's Spear → Ravager on cooldown → Demolish (Colossus) →
Shield Charge#385952 → Thunder Blast at 2 stacks → **Shield Slam on cooldown** (reset by Thunder Clap, Revenge, Execute — Strategist#384041 — and melee via Devastator#236279; Burst of Power = next 2 free)
→ Thunder Blast → Execute (≤20%/35% talented) if you don't need the Rage → Thunder Clap#6343 if Phalanx isn't up → free Revenge → Thunder Clap on cooldown → Wrecking/Shattering Throw (unless needed
for a shield/silence) → Revenge above execute range if Rage isn't needed for defense.
- Execute over Revenge once in range, but only when Shield Slam and Thunder Clap are on cooldown.
- **Ignore Pain jumps to the top whenever the next cast would cap Rage**; otherwise it's the Rage dump (off GCD).
- Line up big hitters (Shield Charge, Ravager, Demolish) with Avatar and Demoralizing Shout — but don't delay so long you lose a cast.
- Impending Victory#202168 when low.

## AoE (Icy Veins)
Avatar → Demo Shout → Ravager → Champion's Spear → Shield Charge → Shield Slam if Phalanx up → Thunder Blast → Demolish → Shield Slam → Thunder Clap (never hold it >2 GCDs; before Rend#394062 drops) → Revenge.
In dungeons, Thunder Clap (or Revenge) over Shield Slam to grab new enemies. Heavy Handed: Execute over Revenge on 1-3 targets.

## Mountain Thane notes
Thunder Blast first in 2+ targets and during Avatar (Capacitance); Avatar gives 2 Thunder Blast stacks — spend old ones before Avatar; Storm Surge = Thunder Clap every other GCD in Avatar
(easy to Rage cap → dump into Ignore Pain/Revenge); Snap Induction: don't press Avatar and Demo Shout back to back.
## Colossus notes
Demolish on cooldown (ideally in Avatar/Demo, don't delay); no enemies behind you, not standing in bad; interrupt it with Heroic Leap; extra Rage → Revenge (hits harder than Thunder Clap) if safe.

## Active mitigation (Icy Veins)
- **Shield Block**: 30 Rage, 2 charges, 16 s recharge (haste), blocks all melee for 6 s, Shield Slam +30%. Aim for high **effective** uptime (while being hit). Time it after a swing, before a known
  blockable big hit, not during a long boss cast. Not tanking for a while → spend it for Shield Slam damage but have ~2 charges when you take the boss back.
- **Ignore Pain**: 35 Rage, absorbs 50% of each hit, cap 30% max HP, 12 s. Use on top of Shield Block; pool Rage for it before a big spike; don't waste it when not tanking (unless Rage capping —
  then spam it anyway for Anger Management/Indomitable).
- Stances: Defensive (−16% damage) when damage is high/unknown/unblockable; Battle (+3% crit) otherwise. Swap freely.
- Rage: 100 max; ~2 per auto-attack, 3 when hit (≈1 s ICD); Ravager up to 60, Demo Shout 20 (Booming Voice), Shield Charge 20/40, Charge 20, Shield Slam 15, Thunder Clap 5, Champion's Spear 10.
- Block: shield's Block stat (2.5x its armor) → DR; crit blocks double it (85% cap). Shield upgrades are huge.

## Cooldowns
Shield Wall#871 before big spikes (esp. unblockable); Last Stand heal only as a last resort. Demoralizing Shout (on cooldown with Booming Voice; delay only for a known heavy hit).
Spell Reflection#23920 (magic reduction; reflecting a spell ends the buff). Shockwave / Intimidating Shout stop incoming melee (careful, fear can pull more). Rallying Cry#97462 (plan it).
Intervene (co-tank, fixates, knockbacks), Heroic Leap#6544 (resets Taunt), Heroic Throw for threat at range, Berserker Rage vs fears, Pummel, Piercing Howl. Battle Shout#6673 pre-pull.

## Stats
- Wowhead (both survivability and DPS): Strength > Haste > Crit > Versatility > Mastery.
- Icy Veins: ilvl > Haste > Crit ≈ Vers > Mastery (Vers first on magic-heavy fights). More total stats beat "better" stats.
- Haste also shortens Shield Block recharge (best stat). Crit = parry (Riposte), big in M+.
