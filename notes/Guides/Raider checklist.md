# Raider checklist

One line per thing a raider is judged on, in Debrief's three flags:
🟢 **done right** · 🟡 **some mistakes** · 🔴 **failed** (half or more of the chances went wrong, or one that cost the pull).

Built from `Raiding - what matters.md` (sources there). Numbers are starting points for Mythic progression; check
each against top kills before a check goes live (user rule: logs confirm, guides decide). Casual/Heroic: loosen one
step. "Per pull" means up to the wipe filter.

**Logged?** says whether a combat log can see it: ✅ yes · ◐ partly (needs the note, a map or many pulls) · ✗ no (voice,
attitude — officers judge these themselves).

## A. Staying alive

| # | Line | 🟢 Done right | 🟡 Some mistakes | 🔴 Failed | Logged? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Deaths | Alive every pull (or only deaths that weren't on them) | A death on them in some pulls | Died on their own mistake in half or more of the pulls, or died first and started the wipe | ✅ |
| A2 | First death | Never the first death | First death once in the night | First death in 2+ pulls, or first death that started a cascade | ✅ |
| A3 | Same mechanic twice | Never died to the same mechanic twice | Died to one mechanic twice in a night | Same mechanic 3+ times, or across weeks | ✅ |
| A4 | Could they have lived? | Every slow death (5+ s) had a defensive/stone/pot used first | One slow death with something ready | Half or more of their slow deaths with something ready | ✅ |
| A5 | Fast deaths (≤2 s from healthy) | Never held against them (unless it was their own mistake) | — | — | ✅ |
| A6 | Close calls | Low (under 30% for 5+ s) and pressed something | Sat low once with a stone/pot ready | Sat low with kit ready in half the close calls | ✅ |

## B. Mechanics and assignments

| # | Line | 🟢 | 🟡 | 🔴 | Logged? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Their assignments in the note | Did every job, on time | Some late or partly done | Half or more missed, or one missed that wiped | ◐ (needs the note) |
| B2 | Soaks | In their soak every time | Missed one, or 1–2 of their group missing (fine per user rule) | 3+ of the group missing, or soaked with the wrong group repeatedly | ✅/◐ |
| B3 | Interrupts on assignment | Every assigned kick landed, in order | One late or missed | Half or more missed, or a miss that killed people | ◐ (needs the kick order) |
| B4 | Dispels (dangerous debuffs) | Removed fast | Slow sometimes | A death with it on, or half slow | ✅ |
| B5 | Dispels (harmless debuffs) | Advice only, never red (user rule) | Slow = yellow at most | — | ✅ |
| B6 | Avoidable damage | No avoidable hits, or a stray tick | A few hits | Hit in half or more of the pulls, or a hit that led to a death | ✅ |
| B7 | Positioning and spread/stack | In the right spot, moving with the group | Off spot sometimes | Out of place in half the pulls, or a spot error that hit others | ◐ (maps, positions) |
| B8 | Baits and frontals | Baited where the plan says; frontals away from the raid | Once off | Frontal/bait hit the raid in half the pulls | ✅ |
| B9 | Add priority | Priority adds hit fast, share on adds near the DPS average | Below half the average on adds sometimes | Ignored priority adds in half the pulls | ✅ |
| B10 | Tank swaps (tanks) | Taunted on time every time | One late swap | Missed swaps that killed or nearly killed | ✅ |

## C. Using the kit

| # | Line | 🟢 | 🟡 | 🔴 | Logged? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Personal defensive on known hits | Used on the planned/big hits | Missed some | Took known big hits with nothing, half the time | ◐ (needs the hit list) |
| C2 | Healthstone / health potion | Used when low and it mattered | Once unused when it mattered | Unused in half the moments that mattered | ✅ |
| C3 | Combat (or mana) potion | One per pull, two when the pull is long enough (pre-pot counts) | Missed in some pulls | None all night | ✅ |
| C4 | Raid cooldowns (healers/utility) | Pressed at the planned time, as often as back | Late or one missed | Half missed, or unused when the raid died | ◐ (needs the note) |
| C5 | Burst lined up | Big cooldowns, potion, Lust in the right windows (adds, vulnerable boss, execute) | Some off-window | Most off-window or wasted into immunity/downtime | ✅ |
| C6 | Power Infusion | Lined up with the target's cooldown (user rules) | Early, or on a healer | Holy/Disc: nothing near; target died or stacked | ✅ |
| C7 | Battle res | Used on the right player at a useful time | Late | Wasted on a lost pull while others needed it | ✅ |

## D. Output

| # | Line | 🟢 | 🟡 | 🔴 | Logged? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Always be casting | Casting whenever not forced to move | Some idle time | Idle a lot outside forced movement (bar from top kills) | ✅ |
| D2 | Rotation priority | Right button when it was ready | Some wrong presses | Half the checks wrong on lines that add damage (proc-driven lines ungraded) | ✅ |
| D3 | Big cooldowns used | Used as often as they fit | One use lost | Half their possible uses lost | ✅ |
| D4 | Throughput vs the fight's check | Pulls their weight for the job they have | Below the role median without a reason | Far below (single-digit level) with no job, over weeks | ✅ |
| D5 | Consistency (median vs best) | Median close to best | Big gap | Median far below best, no change over kills | ✅ (many pulls) |
| D6 | Healers: mana and cooldowns vs damage | Mana lasts the pull; cooldowns on the damage | Ran low once, a cooldown off-time | Out of mana on wipes; cooldowns idle when the raid died | ✅ |
| D7 | Overhealing | Never judged (user rule) | — | — | — |
