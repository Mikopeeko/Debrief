# Wipe detection — how it works (Oct 23, 2026)

The user's idea: "instead of having a wipe filter… create logic across my whole website that detects when a wipe was occurring and
filters all things beyond that out." It replaces "stop at the Nth death" with an automatic **wipe call** that every page uses.
Checked against the user's own calls on the Oct 21 Coiled Altar night: "these marks match where I'd call each wipe."

## The rule (ui.js `wipeAt(r)`, used through `cutInfo`)
On a wipe (never on a kill), the wipe is called at the first moment of:
1. **More dead than any kill survived.** Players dead at once (deaths minus battle rezzes, counted per 20 players), after the raid's
   battle rez charges still up (1 at the pull, +1 every 90/raid-size minutes: 4.5 min for 20), is more than the most any kill of this
   boss and difficulty ever had dead at that point of the fight, with no cushion since Oct 23 (Twin Fangs Heroic +1). The user: "5 deaths in a pull before a wipe is called is a lot"; the old +1 cushion put Coiled Altar Mythic at 5. Without it: 15 of the 800 deathiest kills would have been called (mostly late), and last night (bcA9kw3mxHKzr4fR) 17 of 35 pulls get called earlier, mostly at 4 deaths. Tried and turned down: the 95th percentile of kills instead of the worst (barely moves Coiled Altar, calls some pulls at 2 deaths) and no battle rez credit (39 false alarms, some in the first 15% of a kill). Script: wipe/lab.js (Q, CH, M) and labours.js. Not while the boss is at 10%
   or less (raids that close still win: checked in the logs), and not past the median kill length (the line gets too thin there).
2. **The damage check was missed.** A red "Phase changes" row (the boss's Pace line), dated to the moment the phase ran past the
   longest any kill took ("took 3:33 … never past 2:56" → late at end − 3:33 + 2:56). The user on pull 19: "no way we would have met
   the damage check."
3. **Half the raid dead** (the wipe's last stand).
Everything up to and including the call counts (what caused the wipe); after it isn't graded. The setting is one switch: "Stop at
the wipe call" (off shows everything). A boss without a kill line keeps the old 2nd-death cut until its line exists.

## The numbers (WIPE_LINE in ui.js, generated by `mk-lines.js`)
Per boss and difficulty: the 30 kills with the most deaths and 10 random ones from Warcraft Logs' kill lists (up to 400 per boss),
with every death and battle rez (Rebirth, Raise Ally, Intercession, Soulstone…). Values are "most dead at once a kill survived",
per 30 s, a minute either side, never fewer than earlier.
- The Coiled Altar Mythic: 2 dead until 4:00, 3 until 7:30, 4 until 9:00 (40 kills, 94 rezzes).
- Nymrissa Mythic: 1 → 3 by 1:00 → 5 by 2:00 → 8 by 4:30. Most of its wipes pass the line at 5:44 (the enrage).
- Twin Fangs Mythic: never more than 1 dead until 3:00, 2 until 5:00. Ula'tek Mythic: 1 until 3:30, 2 until 6:30.
- Full table: WIPE_LINE (19 sets: all 10 bosses Mythic, 9 Heroic; Kith'ix Heroic still gathering).

## How we know it's right
- **Never on a kill:** every kill judged against a line built from the *other* kills (leave-one-out): 760 kills, **0 false alarms**.
  The only two near-misses were "last stands" with the boss at 0/0/4% (Lost Explorers Mythic) and 8%/6% (Entombed Sentinels Heroic),
  which the 10% boss rule covers.
- **What we tried and dropped:** "4 deaths in 5 s" (kills survive bursts: 13 false alarms), "both tanks / half the healers dead"
  (a kill lost 2 of 4 healers), boss damage dropping (intermissions do that too).
- **Wipes:** 452 other-guild wipes; most are called 10–30 s before the raid is all dead.
- **The user's night:** 19 of 19 pulls match their calls (one 2 s later, because the boss was under 10%).

## Checklist (a new boss, a new tier, or a tuning patch)
1. Gather slowly: `wipe/gather.js` (1 request / 10 s, pauses 20 min on any error; memory wcl-rate-limit-shared). Never faster.
2. `node mk-lines.js > wipe-lines.txt` → prints each boss's leave-one-out false alarms. Aim for 0 or 1 per set (margin 0); raise that set's margin
   (MARGIN) only if a false alarm isn't a "boss under 10%" last stand.
3. Paste wipe-lines.txt into ui.js (`wipe-call.js` does it), run `wipe-test.js` on a real night, show the user a per-pull timeline
   with the marks before going live.
4. A big tuning patch (boss health, timers) can move the line: re-gather that boss.

## When the raid plays on past the call (Nov 6, widened Nov 13)
A wipe call is a line no kill ever crossed, but a raid can outlive it. When it does, the whole pull counts ("Wiped at", not "Wipe called at"), and the cause is read to the end.
One rule decides it, in the engine (`playedOnAfter`), asked by the page's wipe call (`playedOn` in ui.js) and by both root-cause readers:

1. **The pull ran on longer than the call took to come** (20 s at least, 60 s at most). Nov 6.
2. **Nobody died for 15 s or more after the call, and 30 s or more of pull were left.** Nov 13, Main for Flex. Only for calls that count deaths (the kill line, half the raid dead):
   a missed damage check or a wipe mechanic is not something a raid outlives. Deaths within 1.25 s of the call are the call's own, not "a death after it".

Deaths in that stretch are judged like any other, by the proof-only rule, until a quarter of the raid is dead.

Where it came from: Wolfthorn's Nov 12 Ula'tek night. Pulls 4, 9 and 13 were called at 1:03, 1:00 and 1:49 on two or three deaths, then went 18, 35 and 16 s without a death
and fought on 45 to 57 s. Pull 9's Rattler Slam (the tank 37 yards from the tail, 11 dead) and pull 13's second coil set were never read.

Measured on the saved nights' 94 wipes (Oct 14, 21, 22, Nov 11, Nov 12): 7 pulls changed, the three above and four older Coiled Altar pulls (Oct 21 pulls 5 and 16, Oct 22 pulls 2 and 27); 4 deaths became "You".
Nothing changed on the Nov 11 night. One read got vaguer: Oct 21 pull 16 went from "Gloombomb killed 3 of the 4" to "Too many individual mistakes" (8 deaths read instead of 4).

Checks: `tests/ulatek-check.js` AA (the rule on the pulls' shapes in every build, pull 9 from the real log in every build, all 13 with `--all`). Tool to measure a change to the
rule: the Ula'tek chat's `cmp.js` (old code next to new: call, cause and every death's verdict).
