# Note suggestions: how Debrief picks players (researched Oct 20, 2026)

The Note builder's "Suggested" picks (when there's no pull or note to copy from). Built from the boss guides in
notes/Guides/Bosses (gospel), the class talent lists inside Debrief (TALENT_DATA, so every ability named here exists in
Midnight 12.1), and your own pulls for cooldown timing. Every pick shows its reason on the page.

## Abilities by class (checked against the Midnight talent lists)
- Kicks: E.INTERRUPTS (engine). Healers can't kick, except Restoration Shaman (Wind Shear). Warlock kicks come from the pet
  (Spell Lock): picked last for Broodlings, since a stuck pet cost Tactyks a wipe.
- Area stuns / incapacitates (orb CC): Shockwave (Warrior), Blinding Light (Paladin), Incapacitating Roar (Druid),
  Song of Chi-Ji or Leg Sweep (Monk), Dragon's Breath or Ring of Frost (Mage), Blinding Sleet (Death Knight), Chaos Nova or
  Sigil of Misery (Demon Hunter), Psychic Scream (Priest), Shadowfury (Warlock), Capacitor Totem (Shaman), Binding Shot (Hunter),
  Tail Swipe (Evoker). Single-target ones (Hammer of Justice, Storm Bolt, Polymorph, Paralysis, Hex…) are the backup.
- Immunities: Aspect of the Turtle (Hunter, 3 min), Divine Shield (Paladin, 5 min), Ice Block (Mage, 4 min);
  against magic also Cloak of Shadows (Rogue, 2 min). Blessing of Protection / Spellwarding from a Paladin on the target.
- Raid cooldowns: each healer's own (Aura Mastery, Divine Hymn, Revival, Rewind, Spirit Link Totem, Tranquility,
  Power Word: Barrier), plus Rallying Cry (Warrior), Darkness (Demon Hunter), Anti-Magic Zone (Death Knight), Zephyr (Evoker).

## The Twin Fangs (checked against the three fastest Mythic kills on Warcraft Logs, Oct 20)
Read in Debrief from the #1-#3 speed kills (CRBwpKLmyrFnA6QG f31, AqPpnvDb2C6Z9BMz f25, b3WzVLdMX1HCqatA f10). The user said
their own note isn't proof of the best way, so these kills decide where the guides are silent.
- Ravenous Feast groups: tanks DO soak. Kills #1 and #3: the first two strikes are the ranged + one tank + healers (2, then 1),
  the third strike is the melee + one healer. Kill #2 put both tanks with the melee. Rule: first two groups = ranged, a tank and
  the healers each (two in the first); the third = the melee and one healer. Warn under 6 on Mythic (Tainted Blood).
- Broodling kicks: about 10 kickers, ~4 kicks each over 40 Broodlings, none missed. Both tanks kick the most (Protection
  Paladins 8-9 each); the rest are ranged; melee almost never (1 kick in 120). A Restoration Shaman helped in two kills, Warlocks
  in two. Rule: a tank in front, the other tank and the ranged (shortest kick first, Warlocks last) on the sides, 2 close + 2-3
  far spots per side, a Restoration Shaman (or the next free kicker) as the spare; melee only if the ranged run out.
- Orb CC (Mythic, Blood Torrent): every class's area stun/fear/incapacitate, tanks and healers too (Psychic Scream, Chaos
  Nova, Dragon's Breath, Leg Sweep, Blinding Light, Shadowfury, Howl of Terror, Incapacitating Roar, Deep Breath...). The
  engine notes (real logs) say shields break only on a stun, fear, disorient or incapacitate, never a slow. Rule: share area-CC
  players evenly over the odd and even sets, melee and ranged in each.
- Heroic and Normal have no Broodlings or Blood Torrent: those pages are Mythic only.
- Tested on Wolfthorn's Mythic roster (20): groups 7/7/6, healers 2/1/1, 10 kickers + a spare.

## The Coiled Altar (checked against the three fastest Mythic kills on Warcraft Logs, Oct 20)
Read in Debrief from the #1-#3 speed kills (xw649bM3KCvFtDqn f49, m7MTBJ3b1f4KCwQy f40, vRPGT4MN6VALQWFh f38).
- Guillotine: two groups that alternate ALL fight. Soak 1 (6-7): the Hunters (who immune soak 2) and tanky players, mostly
  melee walls (Arms Warriors, Retribution, Monks, a Shadow Priest, a Rogue) and a healer. Soak 2: both tanks plus the
  immunities (Hunters, a Paladin's bubble); in kill #1 nobody took damage (all immune). P3 axe 1 = soak 1's group again,
  axe 2 = other players, axe 3 = soak 1's group. Immunities (Turtle, Cloak) show up on two P3 axes.
  Correction to the boss guide: players hit in P1 soaked P3 axes and lived in every top kill, so "Guillotined is permanent"
  doesn't stop P3 soaks. The old rule (nobody hit in P1 soaks P3) is gone.
  Rule: soak 2 = both tanks + Hunters, then other immunities; soak 1 = soak 2's immunities + walls ranked Warrior, Rogue,
  Shadow Priest, Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Monk, others; P3 1 and 3 = soak 1 in its order, P3 2 = the rest.
- Purple orbs: both Arcane Mages and the off-tank Protection Paladin in all three kills (5-9 each), then Warlocks, Evokers,
  Hunters, a Balance Druid. Rule: Mages, the off-tank, Warlocks, Evokers, Hunters, Balance Druid.
- Spiteful Soulcoiler kicks: the marked add went to the off-tank Protection Paladin (15-18 kicks) in all three; the unmarked
  add mostly to ranged and a Restoration Shaman (melee kicked it in only one kill). The guide's "second kick on the melee add
  must be melee" isn't what top raids did. Rule: off-tank (a Protection Paladin if there is one) first on the marked add,
  then ranged by shortest kick, melee only to fill; 3-4 per add.
- Dreadmarch CC: Ring of Peace, Ursol's Vortex, Shockwave (then Hammer of Justice, Deep Breath, Intimidation). Rule order:
  Ring of Peace, Ursol's Vortex, Shockwave, Chaos Nova, Capacitor Totem, Gorefiend's Grasp, Hammer of Justice.
- Curses: top raids' Warlocks used Curse of the Satyr (a Midnight talent) every time; the note line names both.
- Externals and Zephyr: Blessing of Protection from a Paladin, Zephyr from Evokers in turn (unchanged).
- Tested on kill #3's own roster: marked add = their Protection Paladin; soak 1 = 4 of their 6; purples = their Mages and
  off-tank; mind-control CC = Ring of Peace, Ursol's, Shockwave, as they used.

## Special kicks (Oct 20, the user's idea: notes for classes with unique utility)
- Protection Paladin: Avenger's Shield also interrupts (up to 3 targets) and Divine Toll throws 5 at once. Top kills: 17 of 21
  Soulcoiler kicks and 8 of 16 Broodling kicks were Avenger's Shield. Shown beside the player; counts as two kickers.

## Cooldowns from your pulls' damage
Adds up every raider's damage taken each second, per phase, over your pulls on that boss (kills and the long wipes). The
biggest moments get the raid cooldowns, biggest cooldowns on the biggest hits, each player only as often as their cooldown
allows. Each row says why ("the raid's 2nd biggest hit of P1 in your pulls").
