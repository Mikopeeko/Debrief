# Vashnik the Malignant (Mythic): research notes

Sources: the Method Mythic and Heroic guides. These notes may name sources; the app's text stays neutral.

## Fight shape
- One phase, on a loop: Imbibe at 100 energy (about every 84 s).
  - It hits the raid, adds a Toxic Vapor stack (the soft enrage), and drains the two fountains nearest to him: Blood, Shadow or Flame.
  - Each drained fountain gives him an infusion (90 s, stacking) and spawns that fountain's venoms, which crawl to the Malignant Cavity
    in the middle. Malignant Burst there is a near wipe.
- Adds:
  - Burning Venom (fire): Burning Presence pulses while it lives, and Caustic Surge goes off when it dies. Kill them one at a time.
    Mythic has 3 per Imbibe.
  - Shrouded Venom (shadow): Miasmic Coating, so it's killed twice. Umbral Ejection leaves circles when it dies.
  - Clotting Venom (blood): splits when it dies.
  - Anything alive for 60 s hardens.
- Mythic: each Imbibe raises Malignant Totems. Only Plague Waves remove them, and any still up at the next Imbibe casts Malignance (one is
  survivable, two wipe). Plague Froth targets walk to markers to aim their lines.
- Malignant Catalyst: a raid hit, plus Catalytic Bile across the room. Each bile is split within 6 yd; one that lands on nobody hits the
  whole raid.
- Plague Froth: a few players. After 6 to 8 s, Plague Wave goes out in four cardinal lines from each.
- Adaptive Infection: Exploding (fire; removing it is a Caustic Explosion on the raid, so dispel one at a time) or Stygian (shadow;
  bursts on players within 6 yd). Blood gives Siphoning (not seen).
- Dripping Fangs: tank, 100% more physical damage taken for 32 s. Swap every cast.
- Lust on pull. Wolfthorn holds him between the Shadow and Fire fountains (Flame + Shadow at every Imbibe).

## Thresholds that are my call (not from a guide)
- Two Burning Venoms dying within 3 s count as dying together (amber).
- Two Exploding Infections removed within 1.5 s count as together (amber).
- Malignance: one totem left is amber, two or more red (the guide: one survivable, two a wipe).
