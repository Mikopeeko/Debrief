Launch review · Sunday, Oct 18

Debrief, ready for Monday

The fight audits are the strongest part of Debrief: all ten bosses are covered, and tonight's checks found them reading your logs correctly. What stands between Debrief and a launch is the first five minutes for a raider who has never seen it. There is too much on screen at once, four different ways of grading, a tall header on phones, and a few empty or behind-the-scenes panels showing.

Nothing below has been changed yet. Each idea says how big a change it is, and the mockups show the bigger ones. Pick what you want and I'll build it.

Done overnight

  • The website in Chrome. The "Failed to fetch" error came from ad blockers. They block any web address ending in "/api/events" as tracking, and that's where the site downloaded each pull. It now uses a different address, and the error message says to allow the site if a blocker is still in the way.
  • A crash after browsing many pulls. Every Warcraft Logs pull stayed in memory for the whole visit, more than 100 MB for a long Mythic pull. A pull is now let go once it's saved on the PC. Before, 14 pulls crashed my test tab; now it stays steady.
  • Twin Fangs, Coiled Altar and Ula'tek, checked on Mythic and Heroic with your team's logs (and other guilds' logs for game facts only). There were no errors, and every phase was found. I fixed eight wrong or noisy flags, all listed in FEEDBACK.md (round 116). The biggest: Coiling Ichor went from 634 small mistakes on 30 pulls to 69, and Heroic Coiled Altar is no longer graded against the Mythic orb plan.

Fix before Monday

These are ranked by what a new raider would notice first. Small changes take an evening or less; medium ones take a day.

  1. Open shared links on Latest pull

    A link to a pull opens on whatever tab you used last, so a raider clicking your link landed on an empty Assignments page. A shared link should always open on Latest pull. Your own tab memory can stay for reopening the app.

    High impactSmall
  2. A shorter header on phones

    On a phone, the brand, boss picker, Live button, banner, pull strip, wipe filter and tabs fill about 60% of the first screen before any results appear. One slim bar and a smaller banner bring the results up to the first screen.

    High impactMediumMockup A
  3. A kill should feel like a kill

    Your Twin Fangs kill shows "Fix first on the next pull", and the night's report card gives a D (62/100). On a kill, lead with the kill, then say what to tidy up "next time". Grade the progression nights, but celebrate the kill night.

    High impactSmallMockup B
  4. One way to show good and bad

    Debrief grades in four ways: ✓ ! ✕ in green, amber and red; letter grades (S, A, B, C, D) with a score; Warcraft Logs parse colours; and pull colours by how far a pull got. Use ✓ ! ✕ everywhere for "how it went". Keep parse colours (raiders know them) and pull colours (with their legend). Keep letter grades only for the night's report card, or drop them; the Survival kit table and the Player audit tiles would switch to ✓ ! ✕.

    High impactMedium
  5. Better empty states

    Without an NSRT note, Latest pull shows two tiles that just say "— no NSRT note for this boss", and the Assignments tab is a blank page with one button. Hide those tiles. Turn the empty Assignments page into a short explanation with an example of what it checks.

    SmallMockup C
  6. Hide the Data check from raiders

    The "Data check" panel at the bottom of Mechanics lists spell IDs and event counts. It's there so I can confirm a module reads the log, and to a raider it looks like an error report. Hide it behind a small "Show the data check" link at the very bottom, or show it only on your own PC.

    Small
  7. Load the whole night by itself on the website

    Summary and Player audit say "Showing 2 of 30 pulls" until someone clicks Load all pulls, which then downloads every pull one after another. Start that download in the background as soon as a night is opened, with a small progress bar ("12 of 30 pulls"), so those tabs fill in on their own.

    High impactMedium
  8. The website's start page: what, how, and is it safe

    The "What you get" preview shows 4 of the 6 tabs, in a different order from the real ones. There's also no "how it works" and no line about privacy. Add three short steps, a privacy line, and preview all six tabs in their real order.

Organization

Mechanics: one list instead of three

The Mechanics tab shows the same mechanics three times: as a grid of 12 tiles, then as "What went wrong / What went right" (a copy of Latest pull), then as the list of sections. The list repeats per phase too, so Barbed Bulwark, Caustic Globule and Stone Breaker each appear twice on a Twin Fangs kill. My suggestion: a slim scoreboard strip that jumps to each mechanic, then one list, most important first, with each mechanic once and its phases as small chips.

Tab names and the "All pulls" switch

  • Shorter, plainer tab names: Latest pull · Mechanics · Notes (for Assignments) · Performance · Night (for Summary) · Players (for Player audit). They fit on a phone, and "Night" says what the Summary is about.
  • One place for "this pull / all pulls": it's now a pill to the right of the tabs and a switch inside Performance, Summary and Player audit. Keep only the in-page switch.
  • Tab tooltips: hovering a tab name pops up a description over the page, and it stays while the mouse rests there. The names explain themselves, so drop the tooltips.

Speed and reliability

  • Opening a long Mythic pull on the website took about 5 seconds in my test, and it's instant after that (it's saved in the browser). That's fine, but the loading line could show a moving bar instead of text.
  • Raid night load: when 20 raiders open the same pull right after it ends, only the first person's download asks Warcraft Logs; everyone else gets the site's saved copy.
  • A limit to raise before #7: each visitor may make 90 fresh downloads a minute, to protect your Warcraft Logs allowance. A long Mythic pull is a few dozen downloads, so loading a whole 30-pull night that nobody has opened yet would hit that limit and crawl. The background load (#7) should go newest pull first and get a higher allowance.
  • Browsers to try before Monday: Chrome with uBlock Origin, Edge, Firefox, Safari on an iPhone, and Chrome on Android. The ad-blocker bug only showed up in one browser.
  • An error should be easy to send you: add a "Copy details" button on error screens that copies the message, the report link and the browser, so raiders can paste it into Discord.
