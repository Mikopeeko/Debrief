## A. Guild post (Wolfthorn)

**Debrief: your pull recap, right after every boss pull 🛡️**

I've been building something for us, and it's ready for the guild to try.

After every boss pull, a short recap pops up on your PC, over the game:
- what you did well
- what to fix first, from your rotation, boss mechanics and survival
- one thing to try on the next pull

It also shows the raid's side: what wiped us, deaths and avoidable damage.

**Set it up (2 minutes, Windows only):**
1. Go to **debriefme.io/?ingame** and press **Download for Windows**.
2. Run Debrief-Setup.exe. It installs the PC app and the addon in one go. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The app is signed, it's just new.
3. In WoW, type **/reload**. A short guided setup asks how much you want to see. That's it.

**During raid:** the recap pops up by itself. In WoW, **/recap** shows it too, and **/db** changes what it shows.

**Is it safe?** Yes. The app only reads the combat log file WoW already writes, the same file Warcraft Logs uses. The addon just shows windows: nothing is automated and nothing presses keys. Your log stays on your PC.

**Something off?** Press **Report a problem** at the bottom of the Debrief window, or tell me. It's a beta, so every "this reads wrong" helps. 🙏
