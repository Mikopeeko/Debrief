## Front page and sign-in

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/fr-1-hero.png)

🚪 **Front page and sign-in: your first minute**
This is what a new visitor sees. One line says what Debrief is: **"Every pull, explained."** Debrief checks every mechanic, assignment and death, and tells you what to fix before the next pull.

Two buttons: **Start now** and **See a real raid night**. Underneath: *Free · No account needed · Warcraft Logs or your own combat log.* You don't sign up for anything to look around.

On the right is a small example pull, so you can see the kind of answer you'll get before you give it anything.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/fr-2-ways.png)

**Three ways in: before, during and after raid**
• **Before raid:** the boss guides. All ten bosses, Heroic and Mythic, with maps that play each mechanic in order and your job on each.
• **During raid:** the in-game addon and PC app, which show the mistakes and what to fix before the next pull starts.
• **After a pull:** *Every pull, explained*. Your Warcraft Logs report or your own combat log, mechanics, deaths and rotation, pull by pull.

Below that is a row of all ten bosses this season. Pick one to see what Debrief checks on it.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/fr-3-night.png)

**The whole night, made simple**
Three things sit at the heart of it:
• **Debriefing:** every mechanic done right or not. Each soak, kick and frontal gets a green, yellow or red flag, with the players and a map of who stood where.
• **Rotation:** your buttons, checked the way a coach would. Plain sentences that say what to press instead, for every raid spec, with your cooldowns lined up against the fight.
• **Your NSRT note:** your raid's plan next to what happened. Kicks in order, soaks and crowd control, and who did their job.

**Message 4** (pictures: shots/fr-5-signin.png)

**Start now: adding your character**
Press **Start now** and Debrief asks one thing: who are you?
• **Sign in with Warcraft Logs.** One click. Warcraft Logs asks you to allow it, then sends you back with your characters and guilds. No password is shared with Debrief.
• Or **type your character name and realm**, and press *Find me*.
• Or paste a Warcraft Logs link.

You can skip it all and just paste a report link too.

**Message 5** (pictures: shots/fr-6-teams.png, shots/fr-7-set.png)

**It finds your team for you**
Next it shows the raid teams you've logged nights with this tier. Press **Add** on the ones you raid with, and Debrief brings in their raid nights so you can see the whole team's progress, with everyone's grades.

Then a short *Show me* tour points at where to click to go through a raid night, pull by pull. It takes about a minute, and new raid nights come in by themselves after each raid.

**Message 6** (pictures: shots/fr-4-ready.png)

**Ready for raid night?**
The front page ends with the three steps:
1. **Add your team.** Your character and your team's Warcraft Logs page. About a minute.
2. **Raid with logs on.** Turn on Warcraft Logs live logging, or upload your log after the raid.
3. **Read the debrief.** Between pulls, see the mistakes and what to fix first.

**Why it feels different:** no account, no paywall, no spreadsheet. It starts from your own raid night and gives you a plan, not just a pile of numbers.

A Discord box sits right on the page too: patch notes the day they land, bug reports, and a say in what gets built next.

## Home

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/home-a-team.png)

🏠 **Home: your team and you, in one place**
Home is where Debrief keeps your raid team and your own characters, so you never paste a link twice.

**Your team** sits at the top: your guild or raid team, its progress this tier (here 7/10 Mythic) and where it ranks: world, region and realm, plus a speed rank. A small *Edit* button changes the team.

**You** sits under it: your character, your best and median parse average, your Mythic progress and your Heroic result (here the *Ahead of the Curve* title for finishing Heroic 10/10). *See your summary* opens your own page, and *Add your other characters* brings in alts.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/home-b-nights.png)

**Where you are right now**
The big card is the boss your team is working on: **The Coiled Altar, 78% of the encounter done**, best pull, pulls, nights and total time on the boss. The chart under it draws every pull across your nights, so you can see the whole progression at a glance. The dashed lines mark the phases.

**Open the last night** takes you straight to your most recent raid night.

On the right is the list of **Raid nights**: newest first, each with the boss, the pulls, your best pull, and how far you got. Nights you killed the boss say **Kill**. Click any one to go through it pull by pull: the mistakes, what went well, and what to fix first.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/home-c-raid.png)

**The raid, boss by boss**
At the bottom: all ten bosses in order. **Gold means killed**, and the rest show how far you got, with pulls and time spent. Click a boss for its chart and its nights.

The **difficulty switch** (Mythic, Heroic, Normal, LFR) keeps progress for each, so a team that raids Heroic one night and Mythic the next sees both.

**What's different:** instead of starting from a report link, Debrief starts from *you*: who you are, what team you raid with and where you are in the tier. Every night comes in by itself after raid.

## Plan

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/pl-1-roster.png)

🗺️ **Plan: your raid's prep for every boss**
Plan is the page you use *before* the pull. It doesn't read your logs. It builds your raid's plan for each boss: who plays, who does which job, which cooldowns go where. Then Debriefing checks that plan against what really happened.

**First, your roster.** One tap builds it from your raid nights ("everyone who was at 1 or more of your raid nights this tier"). Everyone is colored by class and sorted into tanks, healers, melee and ranged. Add a raider by hand, or paste a list of names.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/pl-2-tonight.png)

**Tonight, part 1: who plays**
One compact table. Tap a **name** if they're out tonight. Tap a **box** to sit or play someone on one boss. **Drag a box** onto another to swap. Untick a boss to skip it this week. Everything saves as you go.

It knows raid sizes: Mythic is exactly 20, except Nymrissa and Kith'ix, which take up to 25. The **Sits** column shows whoever sits the most, so benching stays fair.

**Part 2: Bosses.** Every boss has **Make it for me** (Debrief fills the plan) or **Build it myself**. **Make the 10 missing plans** does all ten bosses in one press.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/pl-3-bossplan.png)

**One press, ten bosses**
When it makes a plan it fills every job, kick and cooldown **the way the fastest kills do**, for the players you picked in Who plays. After that it tells you what to look at ("To look at: Vashnik, 1 thing").

Open a boss and the top shows how ready it is: **100%**, *20 of 20 have a job*, *every buff*, *big hits covered*. Buttons: **Edit plan**, **Make it for me**, **Paste a note**, **Copy for the game**, **Show me how**.

Under it sits a row of checks for the things raids forget: buffs, debuffs, healer cooldowns, raid cooldowns, externals, immunities, soaks, grips and knockbacks, movement, utility and kicks.

**Message 4** (pictures: shots/pl-4-timeline.png)

**The cooldown timeline**
The whole fight on one line: **Whole fight, P1, P2, Intermission, P3**. Boss mechanics are marked along the top. Under them, every raid cooldown sits where it is used, named for the player who presses it, and the colored curve at the bottom shows how hard the raid is being hit.

A green tick says *Whole fight big hits covered*. You can see in seconds whether the big damage has a cooldown for it, and where two people are about to press the same button.

**Message 5** (pictures: shots/pl-5-jobs.png, shots/pl-6-raidcds.png)

**The jobs, with names on them**
Every job gets its own card with the players in class color: **kicks** in order for marked and unmarked adds, **soaks**, P3 grim guillotines, **purple orbs**, **mind controls**, **curses**. Each shows a *Done* tag, and **Change** opens just that part of the plan.

Below are the **raid cooldowns by time**, plus the **defensives** and the Healthstone call-outs.

**Message 6**

**Into the game, and back**
• **Copy for the game** gives you your raid's **NSRT note**, ready to paste into Northern Sky Raid Tools.
• Already have a note from your raid leader? **Paste a note** and Debrief checks it with you, then saves it.
• After you raid, **Debriefing** checks every pull against the plan: who did their kick, who soaked, who pressed their cooldown.

**What's different:** most planning tools are a blank page. Plan starts you with a full plan built from how the fastest kills play each boss, tells you what it changed and why, and carries your choices over when you swap a player. And it connects to your logs, so the plan gets graded.

## Debriefing

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/deb-1-top.png)

🎯 **Debriefing: every pull, read for you**
This is the page you open right after a pull. In a few seconds you know why it ended, who did what, and what to change next pull.

**At the top, your raid lead's read.** A short summary in the voice of a raid leader: *"We wiped in P3 at 44%. Our best pull yet, and the kill is right there. Next pull, line up our big cooldowns, potions and Bloodlust for the damage check."*
Under it: **What wiped us**, **Other big mistakes**, **What we did well**, and **Since last pull** (did we get better or worse?).

**Below that, the fight in order,** phase by phase. Every mechanic has a bar for how many players did it right, and a name when someone didn't.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/deb-2-pace.png)

**Pace: when will the kill come?**
The strip under the summary tracks your night. **Pulls on this boss** comes with a kill estimate, and *Pace chart* opens it: your progress against other guilds, shown as a curve. Here it says **"Kill ≈ pull 56, likely between 43 and 72."**

The comparison only counts other guilds' pulls from *after the boss's last nerf*, so it's fair. The question every raid leader is really asking is "are we on track?", and this answers it.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/deb-3-deaths.png)

**Deaths, judged fairly**
Press *Who and why*. Deaths are counted **up to the moment the wipe was called**. After that the raid is falling apart, and nobody should lose points for it. Red bars are deaths that were on the player; grey ones came late. Tap any death to see exactly what happened. A small graph shows how many players were dead against how many most kills can lose.

**Message 4** (pictures: shots/deb-4-ranks.png)

**Raid DPS, damage taken and healing, against real kills**
Each number has a *Ranks* button. It lines your raid up against 66 real kills of the boss: **top 10%, top 25%, typical kill, you, bottom 25%.** Under it, a damage-over-time chart sits next to the middle of seven top guilds, with call-outs per phase, like "5% more than top guilds" in phase one and "8% less" in phase two.

The line under each number says it in words: *"Even with the typical 4-healer kill."*

**Message 5** (pictures: shots/deb-5-powerinf.png)

**The details: cooldowns, potions and externals**
On the right: **Power Infusion and Innervate**, avoidable damage, Healthstones and potions, defensives, combat potions and raid cooldowns. Open any row and you get every single press:
• *Power Infusion → Raider J1* ✅ **"Lined up with their Shadow Blades."**
• *Power Infusion → Raider J1* ❌ **"None of their big cooldowns was up."**

It tells you *4 of 7 pressed, 2 on time*, and how many more could have been pressed.

**Message 6** (pictures: shots/deb-7-raidnums.png)

**Raid numbers**
At the bottom, DPS, damage taken and healing for every player in three ranked lists, with a link to the full Graphs page.

**What's different:**
• It tells you **what to do**, not only what happened. Every problem has a fix next to it.
• The **wipe call** decides what counts. A late death never ruins a grade.
• **Names are in the line.** You don't click to find who it was.
• It judges against **real kills** of the boss, not a guess.
• Your raid's **NSRT note** is checked pull by pull when you load it.

## Rotation

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/rot-1-hero.png)

⚔️ **Rotation: your spec, checked like a coach would**
Pick a player and Debrief reviews how they played their own spec on that pull. Every raid spec is covered.

**Top left:** a **rotation score** out of 100 (here 95), the spec and hero talent, and the Warcraft Logs parse when the pull was a kill.

**On the right: What to fix.** The few things that matter most, in order, with **Fix first** on the biggest. Each one says it in a plain sentence: *"Press Beacon of Virtue first, then Aura Mastery, so everything that comes with it heals five players."* Next to each one is how you did (0/1, 19/39) and what top players reach, in grey.

Below that, **Done right** lists what you did well, so it isn't only bad news.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/rot-2-checklist.png)

**Your checklist**
Everything Debrief checks, grouped: **Rotation, Cooldowns, Resources, Prep and Stats**, each with a score and a one-line "what to improve". Open one and you get the numbers behind it:
• *Divine Toll 9/10. It waited unused for 38 seconds.*
• *Aura Mastery did about 1.84M: 1.31M of damage prevented, plus 537.8K healed by the Divine Toll it carried.*

It checks your **prep** too: beacons on others, aura on, weapon oil or rite on. And your stats: versatility kept low, haste and crit close. *Summary* and *Everything* switch how much you see.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/rot-3-gear.png)

**Gear and extras**
The numbers that aren't graded, and what your gear added: here **about 57.2K HPS in all**, split into healing done, trinkets, tier set and enchants, and other gear like leech. Every estimate has a *How we estimated* link. It isn't a score. It's so you know what your items are doing.

**What's different:**
• Plain sentences on **what to press instead**, not just a percentage.
• The bars are set from **top players' real kills**.
• Your **cooldowns are lined up with the fight**, so a cooldown pressed into a quiet moment gets noticed.
• Built from what the leading guides for each spec say, and from what your talents actually do.

## Graphs

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/gr-1-damage.png)

📈 **Graphs: the raw numbers, no advice**
Damage, healing, damage taken, mitigation, survival, casts and buffs, for every player on the pull. This page is for people who just want the numbers.

The **left rail** is your control panel: pick a **metric**, a **target** (all enemies or one), **By player** or **By ability**, and a **phase** (whole pull, P1, P2). Every player shows in class color with the total, damage a second and share. Click a name and their damage opens **by spell**: total, share, hits and crit rate.

On the right: the biggest damage spells in the raid, and a **boss or adds** split showing who put their damage where.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/gr-2-healing.png, shots/gr-3-taken.png)

**Healing and damage taken**
The same layout for healing and for damage taken. Click a healer to see their healing spell by spell, or a player to see what hit them hardest. Mitigation and survival sit beside them in the same rail.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/gr-4-casts.png, shots/gr-5-ability.png)

**Casts a minute, and damage by ability**
*Casts* shows how often each player presses buttons (here the raid averages 57.6 casts a minute). Open a row and every spell shows its count and per minute rate.

**By ability** swaps the page around: total, per second and share for every ability in the raid, so you can see what made the damage instead of who.

**What's different:** the page is pure numbers. All the advice lives on the other tabs, so the numbers never get buried in opinions. And it respects the **wipe call**: the footer says *Counted up to 4:44*, so the last seconds of a wipe don't pad anyone's totals.

## Report card

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/rc-1-top.png)

🧾 **Report card: the whole night on one page**
The page you read at the end of raid. It grades the night, then every raider.

**The header:** pulls (19), deaths that counted (66), combat potions drunk (92%), mistakes that cost points (112), raiders graded (20), and the **raid grade** (here B, 86 out of 100).

**The night, in short:** *"No kill yet after 19 pulls. Our best was pull 19, at P3. Next pull: cancel shields and absorbs when mind controlled, and keep a stun plus a grip or ring ready for every Dreadmarch."*

**The raid's report card:** six subjects, each graded: **Mechanics, Rotation, Survival, Prep, Potions, Output**, and the best raider in each.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/rc-2-roster.png)

**The ranked roster**
Every raider, ranked, with one overall grade and the six numbers behind it. Switch between **Everyone, Tanks, Healers and DPS**. Each row shows the class and spec, the six scores and the number of deaths.

Tap a raider to open their card.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/rc-3-healers.png)

**One raider's card**
The card shows **22 of 26 checks passed**, grouped into *Ready to raid, Mechanics, Staying alive, Your toolkit* and *Performance*, with the pulls where each slip happened.

On the right, in plain words: their rank in the raid and in their role, **Their night** (a sentence), **Next pull**, **Went well**, **Work on** (with *getting worse* tags), a note on deaths, **Best tonight** and **Room to grow**.

Buttons: **Gear and stats**, **Every pull**, **Open their whole night**.

**Message 4**

**Why it's fair**
• Deaths **after the wipe call don't count**. The card says so ("the other 12 came after the wipe was called").
• Every spec is checked against its own rotation and its own job, and you can filter the roster by **Tanks, Healers or DPS**.
• A raider's card is something you can show them, with the pulls where each thing happened.

**What's different:** most tools rank DPS and stop. Debrief grades the whole job: mechanics, rotation, survival, prep, potions and output, and tells each person what to do next.

Put your pull next to a top player of your spec and see exactly where the gap is.

**Message 6**

## Boss guides

**Message 1** (pictures: shots/gd-0-top.png)

🎬 **Boss guides: see the fight before you pull**
All **ten bosses**, **Heroic and Mythic**, open to anyone. Each guide starts with the fight in one paragraph: phases, who tanks what, how many healers, which part is hardest, and which patch it was written for.

Three switches at the top:
• **Learn the fight / Quick**, for the full guide or the short one.
• **Show me: Everyone, Tank, Healer, DPS**, so you only see your role.
• **Watch the whole fight**, for the animated simulation.

*Screenshots are from a real Mythic raid night, with the players' names swapped for "Raider X" and friends.*

**Message 2** (pictures: shots/gd-3-watch.png)

**The fight at a glance, your job, and what wipes us**
A timeline from a **real kill** shows every mechanic in order. Tap one to jump to it. Under it: **Your job** (the first three things each role must do) and **What wipes us**: the few ways the fight is really lost, each in a sentence.

Then a tab for each phase: Phase 1, Phase 2, Intermission, Phase 3, **Whole fight**, **Your role**.

**Message 3** (pictures: shots/gd-1-map.png)

**Every mechanic: a name, steps and a map**
Each mechanic is titled the way it's played: **"Toxic Deluge (Carry orbs to the front)."** Under it come a plain explanation and numbered steps. Heroic and Mythic differences are tagged. Beside it, an animated **map**: tanks, healers, melee and ranged each have their own icon, with a *Key* and a compass.

Press the map's play button to watch the mechanic run in the right order.

**Message 4** (pictures: shots/gd-2-mistake.png)

**The right way, and the mistake**
Every map has two buttons: **The right way** and **Show a mistake.** Show a mistake plays the wrong version and says what it costs: *"Too many orbs in one frontal: nine orbs in one Sever. The raid takes nine hits at once and the healers can't keep up."*

When a pull goes badly, this is the page that explains it.

**Message 5** (pictures: shots/gd-3-watch.png)

**Watch the whole fight**
Press **Watch the whole fight** for an animated simulation of a real kill, in order: quiet stretches cut, repeated loops played once, the real clock on screen. **Follow the action** moves the camera with the fight, **Full screen** fills your monitor, and **Read it** gives the same thing as text. Pick any moment to jump to it.

**What's different:** most guides are a wall of text or a video you scrub through. Here the map moves, your role is highlighted, the mistakes are shown, and it's written in our own words from real kills. The maps are examples of where to stand, not a real pull.

## In-game addon

**Message 1** (pictures: addon/01-card-after-a-wipe.png)

🎮 **Debrief in game: your recap, right after the pull**
The website tells you everything, but hours later. The addon brings the answer **into WoW, right after the pull**, while the raid is still deciding what to change.

A small **PC app** reads your combat log, and when a boss pull ends the **Debrief card** opens in game.

**The card, "Me" tab:** the result (here "Wipe · 38%", with a note that the wipe was called at 5:57), **Your raid lead says** with the **Next pull** fixes, your healing or damage with your place among healers, avoidable damage, dispels, and folded sections for Rotation, Boss mechanics, Survival and **Who died**.

`/recap` opens it again, `/db` opens settings. It stays inside Midnight's addon rules: the PC app does the reading, and the card only arrives after the pull. *Windows only for now, and still in beta.*

*Screenshots are from a real raid night, with the players' names swapped for made-up ones.*

**Message 2** (pictures: addon/02-card-after-a-kill.png, addon/03-raid-view-after-a-wipe.png)

**After a kill, and the raid's view**
A kill gets its own card (**"Kill"**), with a note on what the next pull should look like.

The **Raid view** tab is the raid lead's: the headline for the pull, **what wiped us**, other big mistakes, **what we did well**, every mistake in detail, deaths, avoidable damage, and a **Survival** section (defensives pressed, Healthstones used). Use the arrows to look at **anyone** in the raid.

**Message 3** (pictures: addon/05-simple-style.png, addon/06-detailed-style.png)

**Pick how much you see**
Three styles: **Simple** (your number, your top fix and the next pull tip), **Standard** (adds your fixes and survival, worse and better than last pull, and the raid's deaths) and **Detailed** (everything, in two columns, with your report card grade, charts of how far each pull got, and every rotation tip with *when it slipped*).

**Message 4** (pictures: addon/07-big-window.png, addon/08-dungeon-boss.png)

**The big window**
Press **Expand** for the big window: all your saved pulls on the left grouped by boss and difficulty (raid and Mythic+ both), and on the right your numbers pull by pull, **progress tonight**, **top mistakes** for the raid, boss mechanics and **who died**, with the **Next pull** line at the bottom. Dungeon bosses get a card too.

**Message 5** (pictures: addon/11-setup-every-step.png, addon/09-settings-db.png)

**Set up in four steps, then make it yours**
1. **Check three pieces:** the addon is in, the PC app is running, and combat logging is on (Debrief turns it on for you).
2. **Pick a style.**
3. **Four everyday switches:** use my class colour, hide during a fight, only the mistakes, and card size.
4. **How to use it.**

`/db` opens every setting any time: colours, text size, what the card shows, when it closes, and more.

**Message 6** (pictures: addon/12-save-this-pull.png, addon/13-pc-popup.png)

**Never miss a pull**
If your newest pull isn't in the addon yet, a strip on top of the card says so and offers **Save this pull**. The PC app also has a small pop-up for each new pull.

**What's different:** other tools tell you what happened hours later, on a website. This puts the answer in front of you **between pulls**, in plain words, with your own fixes and the raid's mistakes, and it never touches live combat data.
