Okay guys, big news! Thank you for letting me share this. I've been building something for a while now, and Debrief is officially live! 🎉

It tells you exactly what to fix after every pull. No more digging through logs trying to figure out what happened. You give it your Warcraft Logs report (or just your own combat log) and it reads every pull the way a raid lead would: what wiped us, who missed a kick or a soak, who died and why, and what to do before the next try. You get a short summary at the top with a plan for the next pull. Everything is judged against how the top guilds actually kill the boss, and deaths after the wipe call don't count against anybody.

And it goes way beyond mechanics:
- Every raid spec gets coached in plain English, built from what top players really do, like "Divine Toll sat unused for 38 seconds"
- Boss guides for all 10 bosses with animated maps (tanks, healers, melee and ranged each get their own icon) that show the right way AND the mistake
- A Plan page that builds your whole raid setup for all 10 bosses in one press (kicks, soaks, cooldowns, who plays what) the way the fastest kills do it, and gives you the NSRT note
- A report card for every raider, a kill estimate against other guilds, and a Compare page that shows exactly where you trail the top players of your spec

Oh, and there's an in-game addon! A little PC app reads your log, and right after each pull a card pops up in WoW with your fixes for the next pull and who died. The raid lead gets a Raid view too. You get answers between pulls instead of hours later. Windows only for now, and still in beta: https://debriefme.io/?ingame

It's 100% free, no account and no paywall: https://debriefme.io 🔥
