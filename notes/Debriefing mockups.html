The groups come from each boss's own rhythm (cycles, adds, tanks, staying alive), the way the guides teach the fight.

My take
  • A · Coach's whiteboard: amazing. It's the closest to what a raid lead says after a pull, and it cuts the page to a third. It risks hiding the 4th to 10th problems, so the "Also slipped" list has to stay.
  • B · Replay: solid, the most "wow". It shows off the maps, which raiders love. It's the most work, and the weakest on a phone.
  • C · Checklist: solid. It's the easiest to learn and the calmest. It's the least exciting, and long fights make it long.
  • A mix I'd suggest: A's top (four numbers, the three "Fix these" map cards, the next-pull line) and C's checklist under it, with B's replay one click away.
