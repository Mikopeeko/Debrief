Main tabs: order and names

Your tabs today, then three ways to order and name them so each one says what it does. Only the tab bar changes: every page stays the same. Performance is shown faded, as it's greyed out on the website.

Today current

The problems: "Latest pull" shows any pull you pick, not only the latest. "Summary" doesn't say it's the whole night. And the one-pull tabs and the whole-night tabs are mixed together.

Option A: two labelled groups my pick

"This pull" tabs on the left and "Whole night" tabs on the right, with a small label over each group. Only two names change: Latest pull becomes Pull recap, and Summary becomes Night summary.

On a phone the bar scrolls sideways as it does today, and the labels stay over their groups.

  • Pull recap: the pull at a glance, and what to fix before the next one.
  • Mechanics: every boss mechanic in this pull, with maps.
  • Assignments: your NSRT note, checked against this pull.
  • Performance: how each player played their character.
  • Night summary: every pull of the boss tonight, together.
  • Players: each player's grade and page, for the night or one pull.

Why I'd pick it: the labels answer the question raiders actually have ("is this about one pull or the whole night?") without making anyone relearn much. Mechanics moves before Assignments because more raiders look at it.

Option B: same order idea, no labels simplest

The same order and names as A, with a thin divider instead of labels. Less to read, but the divider alone doesn't say what it means.

Trade-off: the cleanest bar, but new raiders may not notice that the last two tabs cover the whole night.

Option C: night first, for after the raid different use

The night summary comes first, since after raid people review the whole night. The pull tabs follow, and Players moves next to them.

Trade-off: better for review the next day, worse during raid, when the pull that just ended is what everyone opens. Debrief already opens on the latest pull while you're live-logging, so the first tab would often be the wrong one.

Things I'd keep whichever you pick

  • Your teams stays in the top bar, on every page, as it is now.
  • The page tips and the tour get the new names.
  • Old links keep working: a saved link to "Summary" opens Night summary.
  • Other names I considered and dropped: "Recap" alone (too vague), "Tonight" (wrong for a log from last week), and "NSRT note" for Assignments (Assignments already says it well).
